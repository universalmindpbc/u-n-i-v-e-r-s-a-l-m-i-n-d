# International Food Fair flags

The flags identify the food destination in the illustrative festival scene. They are included within the existing single footer disclosure of proposed partners.

- United Nations: official web-use image linked from the [UN Dag Hammarskjöld Library flags page](https://research.un.org/en/maps/flags). Original image preserved, including its embedded credit.
- Asset source: https://libapps.s3.amazonaws.com/accounts/2786/images/MicrosoftTeams-image.png
- Local asset: `lab/assets/un-flag.png`
- SHA-256: `f93d5393a9ccd4620f3b10b34deedccb1cf1ba9da8833894b67781d649e610cf`
- Australian Aboriginal flag: black upper half, red lower half and central yellow circle, drawn procedurally following the [Australian Government flag description](https://www.pmc.gov.au/resources/australian-flags-booklet/part-3-other-official-flags-australia/flags-australias-indigenous-peoples).
- Japan, France and Italy: procedural rectangular flag designs, an illustrative selection for the international dining area.

All flags use the existing scene wind animation and appear with the festival fit-out. No partner flower is composited over national or UN flag designs.
