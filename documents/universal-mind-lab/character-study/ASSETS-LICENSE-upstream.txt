The character assets in this folder (base mesh, morph targets, skeleton, skins,
eyes, eyebrows, eyelashes, hair, clothes and proxy meshes) are derived from
MakeHuman (http://www.makehumancommunity.org) and were explicitly released
under CC0 1.0 Universal (public domain dedication) in September 2020 by:

  Data Collection AB, https://www.datacollection.se
  Joel Palmius
  Jonas Hauquier

CC0 1.0: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

Motion capture: the Walk, Slouch walk, Run, Strafe left/right, Jump, Low punch,
Cry and the chair (sit down, sit idle, stand up) animations are retargeted from
the CMU Graphics Lab Motion Capture Database (http://mocap.cs.cmu.edu), using
cgspeed's BVH conversion. CMU's terms: the data is free to use, including in
commercially sold products, but may not be resold directly, even in converted
form. Acknowledgement: "The data used in this project was obtained from
mocap.cs.cmu.edu. The database was created with funding from NSF EIA-0196217."

projects without attribution.
