## Prompt

Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: first architectural spatial study for the Universal Mind Lab, a proposed touring and community installation in regional Australia.
Primary request: Create one refined BLACK-AND-WHITE architectural pencil-and-ink sketch on white paper, wide landscape sheet, with a large elevated oblique bird's-eye view of a small landscaped campus. This is a conceptual adjacency and atmosphere study, not a measured or engineering plan.
Input images: Reference 1 is the Foster Droneport single masonry vault, use its distinctive thin curving four-corner shell and arched openings for the main pavilion. Reference 2 is the ZHA Volu dining canopy, use as a form reference for two smaller satellite pavilions. Reference 3 is the Moroso Tropicália hanging seating in trees, use for woven seat silhouettes and the intimate garden atmosphere. All inputs are references, not scenes to copy literally.
Scene: a gently rolling Australian landscape with eucalyptus-like trees, grasses, low planting and a clear usable central gathering area. Keep the ground and routes readable, avoid a dense jungle. Community site and exact regional planting remain undecided.
Composition: one main Droneport vault near the centre, a FOOD pavilion and a PLAY pavilion around a shared courtyard on its left, a quieter LISTENING GARDEN set away on its right. Place a long road train with a tractor and two trailers at the rear-left service edge on a separate service route, so visitor paths never cross the vehicle manoeuvring area. The road train is a conceptual placeholder for the proposed Linfox partnership. Mark a small service/amenities area near this service edge. Add a clear arrival path from the foreground and lightweight covered connections between occupied zones. Small human silhouettes and loose seating groups indicate human scale only, not verified capacity. Keep the main vault visibly dominant, with just ONE vaulted shell; do not draw the long multi-vault permanent centre.
Form and materials: unify the main shell and satellite forms through related curves, subdued natural material hatching, modest footprints and integration into the planting. The material choice remains open for collaboration with host communities and architects. Include small loose silhouettes of Tropicália armchairs, cocoons and day beds.
Listening garden: clustered TRANSPARENT TUBULAR CYLINDRICAL pylons rising directly from the ground at varied heights, circular or softly organic cross-sections, like clear mineral growths. Music artwork is subtly suspended within the columns. Their form must remain round and volumetric. NO rectangular screens, NO tablet slabs, NO flat billboards, NO angular crystal spikes. Include a small enlarged inset in the bottom-right showing one curved tubular glass pylon beside a woven cocoon chair and headphone rest. The reference is a restrained futuristic music discovery garden combining the transparent interactive feeling of Minority Report and an earth-emerging Superman crystal-cave atmosphere.
Graphic treatment: calm precise black linework with soft graphite shading, no colour, no photorealism. Plenty of white space. Use legible restrained labels with thin leaders, no complex legend. Title at top: "UNIVERSAL MIND LAB". Subtitle: "Spatial study 01 — single-vault community concept". Labels: "MAIN IAM PAVILION", "FOOD", "PLAY", "LISTENING GARDEN", "ROAD TRAIN", "SERVICES", "ARRIVAL". Footer: "Concept only — not to scale. Layout and materials developed with host communities."
Constraints: show a proposal, not confirmed partnerships or a validated construction solution. No Apple logos, no invented corporate endorsements, no flags, no drones, no bridge or elevated climbing structure. The separate satellites are functional pavilions. No stage lighting, no fantasy spaceship, no airport, no nightclub.
