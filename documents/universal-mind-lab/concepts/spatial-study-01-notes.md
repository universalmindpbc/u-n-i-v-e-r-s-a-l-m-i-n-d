# Spatial study 01 — review notes

Date: 2 November 2026.

## Purpose

The first black-and-white ImageGen study explores a single main Droneport-derived vault, smaller food and play pavilions, a separate listening area, arrival and a service edge in an Australian landscape. It is a visual discussion aid for the next spatial and 3D stage.

## Directions represented

- A single main vault is the first community installation.
- Food and play sit in separate satellite pavilions.
- Listening pylons have tubular cylindrical bodies, with woven hanging seats nearby.
- A road train uses a service edge separated from the main arrival route.
- The scene includes nature and open gathering space.
- Satellite shapes relate to the Volu reference, with a shared visual palette.

## Assumptions and unresolved details

- All sizes, distances, people counts, furniture counts, service footprints and pylon heights are illustrative. The drawing does not verify a 100-person capacity.
- The road train is a generated vehicle placeholder. It is not a verified Linfox fleet model or a loading and turning plan.
- The listening garden is shown in a fine-weather state. Its full rain shelter and acoustic separation remain to be designed; planting alone is not an acoustic barrier.
- Canopy connections illustrate an intention for sheltered circulation. Roof drainage, access dimensions, wind behaviour and construction remain untested.
- Furniture silhouettes approximate the supplied references. No exact manufacturer model was used to generate this drawing.
- Tubular glass imagery is speculative display design. This drawing does not establish available display technology.
- The landscape has a general Australian character. An actual regional ecology, site and community brief have not been selected.
- Material hatching indicates a visual relationship, not a final masonry or metal specification. Local material choices and their impacts remain to be evaluated collaboratively.
- The proposed collaboration and partnerships have not yet occurred. References to community development in the image describe the intended process.

## Discussion points

1. Does the relationship between the main vault, food, play and listening feel right?
2. Is the listening area visually part of the Lab while sufficiently separate in experience?
3. Is the arrival welcoming, with the vehicle and service area discreet?
4. Which structures and public uses are valuable to retain in a host community?
