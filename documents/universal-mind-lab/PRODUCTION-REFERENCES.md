# Universal Mind Lab — production references

Checked 2 November 2026. These references support the creative brief and supplier conversations. They do not represent bookings, an engineering specification or confirmed collaborations.

## Sound: four-corner d&b line arrays

Ben requests four directional line-array locations around the main experience, with coverage designed around the audience and quiet areas. The manufacturer currently lists the compact cardioid **CCL** family alongside its other mobile line-array families. Treat CCL as a candidate for an appropriately scaled demonstration; cabinet selection, count, coverage, alignment and rigging remain the audio team's design work. [d&b line-array catalogue](https://www.dbaudio.com/global/en/products/all/product-types/line-arrays/).

**NAS** is listed by d&b as an official distributor. NAS also documents an Australian CCL launch and identifies Sightline as an early purchaser. These provide concrete Australian enquiry routes. Retain **Jands** as Ben's preferred candidate/contact; this review has not verified its d&b supply or rental role. [d&b distributor listing](https://www.dbaudio.com/global/en/distributors-and-partners/nas-solutions/), [NAS Australian CCL launch](https://nas.solutions/db-audio-ccl-australian-launch-at-city-recital-hall).

## Screens and projection: TDC and teamLab

Ben requests full-height stage LED screens, artist footage blended with the planned audiovisual content, and projection-mapped sculptures across the site. TDC's Vivid Sydney 2026 account provides a relevant Australian example of large-format projection delivery and digital planning. It supports a technical-delivery conversation; the Lab's LED dimensions and pixel pitch remain unset. [TDC at Vivid Sydney 2026](https://www.tdc.com.au/projects/inside-tdcs-creative-technology-after-dark-at-vivid-sydney-2026/).

teamLab's **Forest of Resonating Lamps** and its wider interactive environments inform the desired relationship between visitor movement, light and surrounding space. The Lab direction is fluid, precise and integrated with nature. The cited installation is a creative reference, not a purchased or commissioned asset. [teamLab installation description](https://api.team-lab.cn/en/w/forest_of_resonating_lamps/), [teamLab Biovortex Kyoto announcement](https://www.teamlab.art/press/kyoto250929/).

This note does not attribute every current Coldplay wristband system to Xylobands. [Xylobands — Coldplay](https://xylobands.com/clients/Coldplay).

## Apple Watch, Immersion Neuroscience and SIX

Immersion's public wellbeing page explicitly describes using wearable devices to **infer simultaneous oxytocin and dopamine release**. Its FAQ identifies cardiac rhythm data as the input and describes derived engagement, connection and Safety metrics. The organisation is **Immersion Neuroscience**; **SIX** is its individual-facing app. [Provider wellbeing explanation](https://www.getimmersion.com/measuring-well-being-with-value-measurement), [Provider FAQ](https://www.getimmersion.com/faq).

The Lab's clinical pitch also retains Ben's account of his meetings with the provider: **Emotional Connection as an oxytocin–dopamine axis inference, Psychological Safety as a cortisol inference, a 90% oxytocin / 10% dopamine explanation, and development with DARPA**. The specific weighting, cortisol mapping and historical development account are attributed to those discussions here; they have not been independently established by the two public pages reviewed above.

This attribution does not reduce the implementation scope. The Apple Watch → provider inference → responsive environment loop is an **MVP requirement**. The intended outcomes are reduced short-term stress and increased felt connection. The browser demonstration currently implements visitor-controlled music, video, motion and a lighting preview; it does not yet receive participant Watch signals.
