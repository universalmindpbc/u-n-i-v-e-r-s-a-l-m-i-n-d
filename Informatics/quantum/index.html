Model & numerical checks

H / ℏ = Ω(t)/2 · (X₀ + X₁) − Δ(t) · (n₀ + n₁) + V · n₀n₁

V = C₆/r⁶, C₆ = 5.42 × 10⁻²⁴ rad m⁶/s. Initial state |gg⟩. Phase is zero. The drive ramps over the first and final 2.5% of the pulse; detuning sweeps linearly between them.

The browser solves the four-state Schrödinger equation with RK4, repeats at half the step size, and requires probability difference and norm error below 10⁻⁶. This check measures numerical convergence, not physical fidelity.

Ideal closed system. No noise, atom loss or preparation/readout errors. g = ground; r = Rydberg excitation. Basis order: gg, gr, rg, rr, with atom 0 first.

How funded access will work

The server authenticates the researcher and checks their project, approved provider, validated experiment and remaining allocation. It reserves the estimated cost before dispatch. A retry keeps the original submission identity; an intentional rerun creates a new run and budget reservation.

Job status, costs and results stay linked to the exact experiment version. Credentials stay on the server. Researchers choose whether to share results. Hardware controls remain locked until these services and provider connections pass end-to-end tests.

Current release: public evidence, browser simulation and downloadable review requests. Researcher sign-in, shared storage, budget administration and website hardware dispatch are pending.
