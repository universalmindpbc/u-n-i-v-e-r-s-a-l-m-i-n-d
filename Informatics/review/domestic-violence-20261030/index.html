How to read this review

Direct evidence explicitly studies domestic, family or intimate-partner violence. Context discusses that setting. Related evidence has an editorial connection and a stated transfer limit; it does not establish effectiveness for this population.

One cross means a verified randomized controlled trial, including single-blinded trials. Two crosses require verified double blinding. Reviews and meta-analyses retain their own classification. Crosses describe methods, not human approval or certainty of benefit.

Multiple reports may describe one study. A review does not add all of its pooled trials to our independently assessed study count. Abstract-only assessments are visibly marked; missing information remains unestablished. Existing library publications are identified separately.
