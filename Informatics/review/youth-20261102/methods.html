How this candidate review was prepared

Status: pending Ben's content sign-off. Prepared 2 November 2026. A$0 additional expenditure. No paid papers, paid APIs, new credentials, external messages or production changes.

This is a single-agent rapid evidence review. It is not an exhaustive systematic review or an independent, duplicate risk-of-bias assessment. Selected methods, results, limitations and tables were inspected where available. methodsInspected must not be interpreted as a complete critical appraisal of every full-text passage or supplementary file. No numeric Jadad, RoB or GRADE scores were invented.

The search used free Europe PMC core records, PubMed/PMC, publisher DOI metadata from Crossref, and targeted primary/institutional website searches. The machine search log retains queries, dates, pagination and response hashes. The 2,747 discovered PMID records are a discovery pool, not a claim that all were fully screened. A purposive set of 187 records received individual notes across the requested modalities and youth/lab rationale. The package includes supportive, mixed, null and adverse-experience findings.

The original institution affiliation query was insufficient; corrected quoted institution queries are retained in the log. Institution membership is based on primary author affiliations or a stated participating service, not an institution's bibliography, a later author appointment or the Orygen Evidence Finder alone. Commercial Headspace meditation apps are distinct from Australian headspace services. Black Dog website HTML returned no readable content in the available web extractor; readable official reports, indexed sources and primary affiliations supplied the evidence instead.

Qualification and age

The qualified subset requires a peer-reviewed empirical study or structured review, a relevant youth overlap or institutional youth need, accessible methods inspected, a meaningful design-specific use and explicit limitations, and no unresolved material reporting issue. Preliminary items, context-only/framework papers, abstract-only records, reprints and held records do not meet the 100-paper target. This threshold does not mean every included paper has low bias or high certainty. A well-described qualitative study can substantiate a need; it cannot establish treatment efficacy.

The initial demographic is approximately 16–24. Exact-range studies, overlapping samples, younger adolescent studies, mixed adult samples and unresolved ages are visibly separated. A mean near 20 does not establish that all participants were 16–24. Institutional research about younger youth is retained for the expressly requested broader Black Dog/Orygen sections. No youth-only effect was inferred from an overall mixed-age result.

Deduplication and independence

The baseline was staging commit 05da69656bbe9fae4498e306d1e1b9171b51e372. Seven complete library/expansion sources were indexed: 1,335 record IDs, which are not claimed to be 1,335 independent papers. Matches use DOI, PMID and normalized exact title. All record IDs and source hashes are retained in baseline-corpus.json. Case and DOI URL prefixes are normalized. Imperfect historical metadata may still conceal additional matches; candidate papers have not been inserted into the library.

One Focus reprint is linked to its original BMC Psychiatry review and excluded from unique-publication counts. Publications from Horyzons, LifeBuoy, MYRIAD, TM, Smooth Sailing and other programmes are linked. The three MVC cycles are conservatively grouped pending a fuller participant-overlap audit. BiteBack community and school cohorts are distinguished despite sharing a programme/registration. Family counts are descriptive clusters, not independent trials. Randomized-study-unit counts collapse known or unresolved overlap and are conservative identified units, not a claim that all independence questions are settled. No total participant count is calculated across overlapping reviews and trials.

Design markers and human review

  • Proposed ×: other peer-reviewed publication, including systematic reviews/meta-analyses; also used provisionally when a claimed RCT lacks inspected methods.
  • Proposed ××: randomized trial with methods inspected, including single/assessor-blinded trials.
  • Proposed ×××: participant and investigator masking verified in the methods.

Allocation concealment, analyst masking and assessor masking alone are not double blinding. Alternating assignment is not automatically random. A systematic review of RCTs remains a review and does not receive trial crosses. The chapter Trust Factor is separate. All approved crosses remain null, and every review decision remains pending. Publication status and access

Each record retains indexed publication types, linked notices and Crossref update metadata. Linked corrections/commentaries were fetched where freely available. Two MVC corrections concern author/collaborator attribution, not outcome estimates. The social-connection review's published criticism and author reply are retained. Absence of a notice in these sources is not a guarantee that no correction or retraction exists.

A retracted music review, PMID 40421183, was excluded after its retraction PMID 41768047 was identified. Papers with inconsistent effect metrics, age tables, allocation descriptions or reporting were held or downgraded. Raw full texts and abstracts are kept in the private task cache and are not redistributed. Public sources contain original summaries, citations, retrieval dates, status and hashes. Abstract-only records are visible but not methods-appraised or qualified.

Harms and attrition are retained where found in the inspected material. Unknown or unextracted information is explicitly labelled; no reported event is not equivalent to demonstrated safety. Registry identifiers are included only when tied to the article's abstract or a manually identified parent family; identifiers from cited references are not silently assigned to the paper.
