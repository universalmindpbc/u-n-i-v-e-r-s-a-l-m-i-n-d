Effect of therapeutic suggestions during general anaesthesia on postoperative pain and opioid use: multicentre randomised controlled trial

Nowak H, Zech N, Asmussen S, Rahmel T, Tryba M, Oprea G, Grause L, Schork K, Moeller M, Loeser J, Gyarmati K, Mittler C, Saller T, Zagler A, Lutz K, Adamzik M, Hansen E. · BMJ · 2020 · Multicentre double-blind randomized controlled trial; 385 analysed

Original publication

Recorded summary

Music with therapeutic suggestions during general anaesthesia reduced postoperative opioid use relative to a blank recording. Median morphine-equivalent use was 4.0 versus 5.3 mg.

Recorded limitations

The intervention combined music and suggestions. Anaesthesia differs from awake masking, and the design does not isolate the spoken component.
