Soluble gp130 is the natural inhibitor of soluble interleukin-6 receptor transsignaling responses

Jostock T, Müllberg J, Ozbek S, Atreya R, Blinn G, Voltz N, Fischer M, Neurath MF, Rose-John S. · European journal of biochemistry · 2001 · Recombinant soluble gp130 and IL-6 receptor signalling experiments

Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

Soluble gp130 inhibited IL-6/soluble-IL-6-receptor trans-signalling responses in the tested systems, providing a mechanistic distinction from classical membrane-receptor signalling.

Recorded limitations

Selectivity depends on the experimental concentration and system. This is pathway biology, not evidence that oxytocin selectively inhibits IL-6 trans-signalling. Access: Indexed abstract and bibliographic record reviewed.
