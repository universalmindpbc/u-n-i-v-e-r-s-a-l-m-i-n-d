The Role of Oxytocin in Abnormal Brain Development: Effect on Glial Cells and Neuroinflammation

Knoop M, Possovre ML, Jacquens A, Charlet A, Baud O, Darbon P. · Cells · 2022 · Review of glia, neuroinflammation and abnormal brain development

Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

Summarises developmental glial mechanisms that connect oxytocin-related signalling with inflammation and brain injury models.

Recorded limitations

Developmental animal and cell evidence remains distinct from a human therapeutic trial. Several underlying studies overlap with other reviews in the supplied collection. Access: Indexed abstract and bibliographic record reviewed.
