Psychological interventions for common mental disorders in women experiencing intimate partner violence in low-income and middle-income countries: a systematic review and meta-analysis.

Keynejad RC; Hanlon C; Howard LM · The lancet. Psychiatry · 2020 · Systematic review and meta-analysis

Original publication · Original publication · Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

IPV exposure did not predict a smaller treatment response. Anxiety response was larger in IPV-exposed women; differences for depression, PTSD and distress were uncertain.

Recorded limitations

These are subgroup differences in treatment effects, not direct pooled effects of therapy versus no therapy in IPV survivors. Trials were not necessarily tailored to IPV.
