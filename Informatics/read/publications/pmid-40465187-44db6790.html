Brief Interactive Virtual Reality Mindfulness Training with Real-Time Biofeedback for Anxiety Reduction: A Pilot Study

Xu Qiuxia, Gu Yingqi, Hu Xizhe · Applied Psychophysiology and Biofeedback · 2025 · Randomised controlled study

Original publication

Recorded summary

Seventy-five university students with recent anxiety symptoms were randomised to VR, guided audio or no intervention. Fog, sound and visuals responded to HRV. The reported immediate changes motivate an event pilot. The correction concerns corresponding authorship.

Recorded limitations

Live HRV drove changes in fog, sound and visuals.
