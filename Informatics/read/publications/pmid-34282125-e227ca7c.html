Longitudinal genome-wide methylation study of PTSD treatment using prolonged exposure and hydrocortisone

Yang Ruoting, Xu Changxin, Bierer Linda M., Flory Janine D., Gautam Aarti, Bader Heather N., Lehrner Amy, Makotkine Iouri, Desarnaud Frank, Miller Stacy A., Jett Marti, Hammamieh Rasha, Yehuda Rachel · Translational Psychiatry · 2021 · Longitudinal molecular analysis within a randomised augmentation trial; 42 participants in the reported table

Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

The study measured methylation before and after prolonged-exposure therapy and at follow-up, identifying candidate associations with recovery including FKBP5 and CREB–BDNF signalling.

Recorded limitations

This supports the research strategy of pairing molecular change with clinical change. It does not establish a DOCK2 endpoint, EMDR-specific mechanism or an effect of Universal Mind interventions; the molecular analysis was small. Access: Relevant full-text methods and results reviewed.
