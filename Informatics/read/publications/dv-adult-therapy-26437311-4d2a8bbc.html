A group intervention to reduce intimate partner violence among female drug users. Results from a randomized controlled pilot trial in a community substance-abuse center.

Tirado-Muñoz J; Gilchrist G; Lligoña E; Gilbert L; Torrens M · Adicciones · 2015 · Randomized controlled trial

Original publication · Original publication · Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

No significant reduction in any IPV, depression, quality of life or self-rated health; selected maltreatment, assertiveness and drinking outcomes showed moderate short-term effects.

Recorded limitations

Very small feasibility pilot; selected intermediate outcomes cannot establish broad violence reduction.
