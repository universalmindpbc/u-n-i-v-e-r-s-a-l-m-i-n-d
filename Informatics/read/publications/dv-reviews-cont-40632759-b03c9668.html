Emotional skills and health assessment in interventions for intimate partner violence perpetrators: A systematic review of randomized controlled trials.

Mora-Pelegrín M; Aranda M; Montes-Berges B · PloS one · 2025 · Systematic review of randomized trials

Original publication · Original publication · Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

Eight of 15 trials favored an emotional-skills programme on at least one selected outcome, but only one of five trials using official IPV recidivism records favored the intervention. A count of positive trials does not establish a pooled effect.

Recorded limitations

No review registration or published protocol. Heterogeneous interventions and active controls often shared emotional components; four pilot/feasibility trials and frequent self-report limit causal attribution to emotional skills alone.

Evidence and provenance

Chapter Trust Factor uses a minimum of three distinct qualifying studies. Publication records retain their own evidence and assessment.
