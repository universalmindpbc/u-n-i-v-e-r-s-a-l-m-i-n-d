Oxytocin and Related Peptide Hormones: Candidate Anti-Inflammatory Therapy in Early Stages of Sepsis

Mehdi SF, Pusapati S, Khenhrani RR, Farooqi MS, Sarwar S, Alnasarat A, Mathur N, Metz CN, LeRoith D, Tracey KJ, Yang H, Brownstein MJ, Roth J. · Frontiers in immunology · 2022 · Review of oxytocin and related peptide candidates in early sepsis

Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

Integrates preclinical anti-inflammatory findings and proposes potential therapeutic roles for oxytocin and related peptides in early sepsis.

Recorded limitations

Candidate status is not clinical validation. Sepsis involves changing immune states, and suppression of an inflammatory marker alone is not evidence of improved survival. Access: Indexed abstract and bibliographic record reviewed.
