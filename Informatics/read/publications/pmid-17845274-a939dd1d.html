Immune variations in bipolar disorder: phasic differences.

Ortiz-Domínguez A, Hernández ME, Berlanga C, Gutiérrez-Mora D, Moreno J, Heinze G, Pavón L. · Bipolar disorders · 2007 · Cross-sectional mood-phase comparison

Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

The depressive subgroup had higher IL-6; the reported manic pattern included lower IL-6 relative to the depressive phase, contrasting with some other mania studies.

Recorded limitations

Only ten participants per illness phase. This discordant finding is retained rather than forcing all mania evidence into one direction. Access: Indexed abstract reviewed; full-text methods not comprehensively assessed.
