Virtual reality versus 2D biofeedback for children and adolescents with stress related disorders: a randomized controlled trial

Felnhofer Anna, Goinska Katharina, Hattinger Johann, Kamper Adrian, Lenz Andreas, Hlavacs Helmut, Kothgassner Oswald D. · Virtual Reality · 2025 · Randomised controlled study

Original publication

Recorded summary

Thirty-nine patients aged 9–18 received ten sessions of VR or conventional screen-based biofeedback. Both groups improved, with comparable outcomes through three months.

Recorded limitations

A baseline heart-rate threshold guided feedback in both the 2D and VR training.
