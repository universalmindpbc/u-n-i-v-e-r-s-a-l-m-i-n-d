Cell 183(7):1986–2002 (DOI: 10.1016/j.cell.2020.11.040; Addgene #128483, pRSET-iSeroSnFR, Lin Tian Lab, UC Davis) — ML-guided binding-pocket redesign (Rosetta + random forest + GLM) produced iSeroSnFR genetically encoded fluorescent 5-HT sensor; >5,000× improved affinity over iAChSnFR0.6; 800% ΔF/F₀; ~350 pM inference; millisecond kinetics; fiber photometry in freely behaving mice (fear conditioning, social interaction, sleep/wake); OSTA assay for hSERT/SLC6A4 pharmacology (SSRIs, cocaine, MDMA). Invasive (genetically encoded sensor + viral delivery + implanted fiber optics)
