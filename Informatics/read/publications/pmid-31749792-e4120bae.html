Cocaine Use Disorder Is Associated With Changes in Th1/Th2/Th17 Cytokines and Lymphocytes Subsets.

Zaparte A, Schuch JB, Viola TW, Baptista TAS, Beidacki AS, do Prado CH, Sanvicente-Vieira B, Bauer ME, Grassi-Oliveira R. · Frontiers in immunology · 2019 · Case-control stimulated-cell study

Original publication · Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

Stimulated immune cells produced higher IL-6 particularly in women with more severe cocaine withdrawal, alongside changes in lymphocyte subsets.

Recorded limitations

Ex vivo stimulated secretion is not resting blood IL-6. Withdrawal severity, sex and detoxification timing limit generalisation. Access: Indexed abstract reviewed; full-text methods not comprehensively assessed.
