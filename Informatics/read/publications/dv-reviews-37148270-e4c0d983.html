Efficacy of Psychosocial Interventions for Survivors of Intimate Partner Violence: A Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis.

Micklitz HM; Glass CM; Bengel J; Sander LB · Trauma, violence & abuse · 2024 · Systematic review and meta-analysis

Original publication · Original publication · Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

Small post-treatment improvements in depression and PTSD were found, but IPV re-exposure did not improve; benefits were not maintained long term.

Recorded limitations

Low-quality evidence and moderate heterogeneity; harms remain unclear and integrative subgroup results do not establish which component causes benefit.
