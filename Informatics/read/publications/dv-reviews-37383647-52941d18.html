Randomized Clinical Trial of a Brief Alcohol Intervention as an Adjunct to Batterer Intervention for Women Arrested for Domestic Violence.

Brem MJ; Shorey RC; Ramsey SE; Stuart GL · Psychosocial intervention · 2023 · Randomized controlled trial

Original publication · Original publication · Original publication · Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

The adjunct improved alcohol outcomes and reduced reported physical IPV perpetration. Injuries experienced were less frequent numerically, but the discussion says that difference was not statistically significant.

Recorded limitations

Unmasked assessors, self-reported outcomes and a selected hazardous-drinking court-referred sample. The abstract and discussion differ in emphasis on injury; no confirmed injury benefit is claimed.
