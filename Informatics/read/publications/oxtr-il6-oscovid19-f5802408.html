Phase II, Multicenter, Open-label, Rct With an Adaptive Design, to Assess Efficacy of Intravenous Administration of Oxytocin in Hospitalized Patients Affected by COVID-19

Azienda Ospedaliero-Universitaria di Parma · ClinicalTrials.gov · 2020 · Withdrawn clinical trial registration; no enrolled participants

Original publication

Recorded summary

OsCOVID19 (NCT04386447) was registered to test intravenous oxytocin in hospitalised COVID-19 patients. The registry records withdrawal because the Italian Medicines Agency did not approve the study; actual enrolment is zero.

Recorded limitations

A withdrawn registration supplies no efficacy or safety results. Registry last update: 14 January 2021; status checked 24 November 2026. Access: Official ClinicalTrials.gov API record reviewed.
