Targeting the Oxytocinergic System: A Possible Pharmacological Strategy for the Treatment of Inflammation Occurring in Different Chronic Diseases

Friuli M, Eramo B, Valenza M, Scuderi C, Provensi G, Romano A. · International journal of molecular sciences · 2021 · Narrative review of oxytocinergic signalling across chronic-disease models

Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

Maps anti-inflammatory mechanisms and potential disease applications across cell and animal studies, providing a useful route to primary research.

Recorded limitations

The review is not an independent replication of the studies it cites. Broad therapeutic conclusions need evaluation against the human trials and counterexamples in this chapter. Access: Indexed abstract and bibliographic record reviewed.
