Oxytocin and microglia in the development of social behaviour

Gonzalez A, Hammock EAD. · Philosophical transactions of the Royal Society of London. Series B, Biological sciences · 2022 · Review of oxytocin, microglia and social development

Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

Connects oxytocin signalling with microglial development and social-behaviour research, highlighting interactions between neural and immune systems.

Recorded limitations

Developmental mechanisms and animal behaviour do not establish adult human IL-6 lowering or efficacy of a social intervention. Access: Indexed abstract and bibliographic record reviewed.
