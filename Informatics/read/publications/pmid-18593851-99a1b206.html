Oxytocin alleviates the neuroendocrine and cytokine response to bacterial endotoxin in healthy men

Clodi M, Vila G, Geyeregger R, Riedl M, Stulnig TM, Struck J, Luger TA, Luger A. · American journal of physiology. Endocrinology and metabolism · 2008 · Randomised placebo-controlled crossover endotoxin challenge in 10 healthy men

Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

Intravenous oxytocin attenuated the acute endotoxin-induced IL-6, TNF and stress-hormone response. Several regulatory mediators, including IL-1 receptor antagonist and IL-4, also decreased. Parallel human monocyte/PBMC experiments did not reproduce direct cytokine suppression.

Recorded limitations

Ten healthy men under acute experimental inflammation cannot establish sustained benefit in chronic disease, other sexes or intranasal delivery. An indirect neuroendocrine or autonomic mechanism remains a possibility rather than a demonstrated causal chain. Access: Indexed abstract and bibliographic record reviewed.
