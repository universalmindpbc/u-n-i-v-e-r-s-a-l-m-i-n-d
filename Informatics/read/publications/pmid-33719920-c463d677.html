A sham-controlled clinical trial to examine the effect of bilateral tDCS on craving, TNF-α and IL-6 expression levels, and impulsivity of males with opioid use disorder.

Eskandari Z, Mostafavi H, Hosseini M, Mousavi SE, Ramazani S, Dadashi M. · Journal of addictive diseases · 2021 · Sham-controlled clinical trial

Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

Active tDCS improved impulsivity, while craving declined in active and sham conditions. IL-6 and TNF reductions did not reach statistical significance.

Recorded limitations

Very small three-group study; no demonstrated IL-6 effect. Masking and randomization are recorded separately rather than inferred from one another. Access: Indexed abstract reviewed; full-text methods not comprehensively assessed.
