Interleukin 6 as a Treatment Target for Depression: A Proof-of-Concept Randomized Clinical Trial.

Foley ÉM, Turner N, Margelyte R, Jones HJ, Kaser M, Lewis G, Jones PB, Khandaker GM. · JAMA psychiatry · 2026 · Randomized placebo-controlled trial

Original publication · Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

A single tocilizumab infusion produced no statistically significant improvement in the primary day-14 somatic depression outcome (adjusted difference −0.12; 95% CI −2.51 to 2.28). Later symptom trends favouring treatment are hypothesis-generating. Baseline CRP, not IL-6, tracked improvement.

Recorded limitations

Small inflammation-enriched proof-of-concept trial; four-week follow-up. Descriptive response and remission rates do not establish efficacy. Access: Abstract and selected full-text passages reviewed.
