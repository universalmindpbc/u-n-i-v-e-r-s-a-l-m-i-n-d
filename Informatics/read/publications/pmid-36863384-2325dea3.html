Alteration patterns of peripheral concentrations of cytokines and associated inflammatory proteins in acute and chronic stages of schizophrenia: a systematic review and network meta-analysis.

Halstead S, Siskind D, Amft M, Wagner E, Yakimov V, Shih-Jung Liu Z, Walder K, Warren N. · The lancet. Psychiatry · 2023 · Systematic review and network meta-analysis

Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

IL-6 and several other markers were elevated in both acute and chronic schizophrenia-spectrum illness. Other cytokines varied by stage.

Recorded limitations

Peripheral protein findings do not directly establish brain inflammation. Case composition affected IL-6 estimates; observational heterogeneity and selective publication remain relevant. Linked correction notice identified; its full content remains unresolved in this appraisal. Access: Indexed abstract reviewed; full-text methods not comprehensively assessed.
