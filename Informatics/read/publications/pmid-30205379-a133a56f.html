The Phosphodiesterase Inhibitor Pentoxifylline as a Novel Adjunct to Antidepressants in Major Depressive Disorder Patients: A Proof-of-Concept, Randomized, Double-Blind, Placebo-Controlled Trial.

El-Haggar SM, Eissa MA, Mostafa TM, El-Attar KS, Abdallah MS. · Psychotherapy and psychosomatics · 2018 · Randomized placebo-controlled trial

Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

Pentoxifylline added to escitalopram improved depression ratings and reduced IL-6, TNF and IL-10 over 12 weeks.

Recorded limitations

Small proof-of-concept trial with many endpoints. IL-10 also fell, illustrating that lower regulatory cytokines can accompany reduced inflammatory activation. Linked correction notice identified; its full content remains unresolved in this appraisal. Access: Indexed abstract reviewed; full-text methods not comprehensively assessed.
