A mobile VR-based respiratory biofeedback game to foster diaphragmatic breathing

Rockstroh Christoph, Blum Johannes, Göritz Anja S. · Virtual Reality · 2020 · Participant study

Original publication

Recorded summary

Repeated sessions improved breath awareness and breathing measures in a within-person study. Longer follow-up and an active comparison are needed to isolate the training effect.

Recorded limitations

A controller on the abdomen sensed movement caused by breathing and fed it into the game.
