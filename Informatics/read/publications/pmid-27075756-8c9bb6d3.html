Oxytocin inhibits lipopolysaccharide-induced inflammation in microglial cells and attenuates microglial activation in lipopolysaccharide-treated mice

Yuan L, Liu S, Bai X, Gao Y, Liu G, Wang X, Liu D, Li T, Hao A, Wang Z. · Journal of neuroinflammation · 2016 · BV-2 and primary microglia; LPS-challenged male mice

Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

Oxytocin pretreatment reduced inflammatory responses in microglial systems and microglial activation in mice. ERK and p38 responses were reduced under the tested conditions, while JNK did not show the same pattern.

Recorded limitations

Pretreatment of an acute endotoxin model differs from treating established human neuroinflammatory disease. Concentrations, route and timing constrain translation; peripheral cytokines do not directly measure brain inflammation. Access: Relevant full-text methods and results reviewed.
