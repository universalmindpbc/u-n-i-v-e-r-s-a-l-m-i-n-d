Beating stress: music with monaural beats reduces anxiety and improves mood in a non-clinical population

PMC publication record · 2025 · Single-blind randomized sound study; 308 participants

Original publication

Recorded summary

The trial compared music with monaural beats, beats and pure-tone conditions on anxiety and mood.

Recorded limitations

No music-only arm; nonlinguistic beats are distinct from verbal messages.
