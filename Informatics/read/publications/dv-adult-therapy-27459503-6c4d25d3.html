Comparison of Adding Treatment of PTSD During and After Shelter Stay to Standard Care in Residents of Battered Women's Shelters: Results of a Randomized Clinical Trial.

Johnson DM; Johnson NL; Perez SK; Palmieri PA; Zlotnick C · Journal of traumatic stress · 2016 · Randomized controlled trial

Original publication · Original publication · Original publication · Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

HOPE improved PTSD trajectories, but did not reduce future IPV relative to standard services. Depression p=.052 is not treated as statistically significant.

Recorded limitations

Small Phase I trial; no attention-matched comparator and restricted eligibility limit transfer. This is a separate 60-person trial from the earlier 70-person shelter-only study.
