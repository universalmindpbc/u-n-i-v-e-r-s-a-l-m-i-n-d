Recorded summary

Supplementary Table 5 explicitly tested the original DOCK2 site cg16277944: p = 0.21 and adjusted p = 0.69. This study did not replicate that association.

Recorded limitations

An unsuccessful replication reduces confidence in a broadly reliable marker; it does not rule out effects in every population, tissue or symptom definition. A case-control replication also does not directly test response to treatment. Access: Main paper and Supplementary Table 5 inspected directly.
