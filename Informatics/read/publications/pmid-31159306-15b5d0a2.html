Oxytocin Suppresses Inflammatory Responses Induced by Lipopolysaccharide through Inhibition of the eIF-2-ATF4 Pathway in Mouse Microglia

Inoue T, Yamakage H, Tanaka M, Kusakabe T, Shimatsu A, Satoh-Asahara N. · Cells · 2019 · Mouse MG6 microglial cell experiments

Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

Oxytocin reduced LPS-associated IL-6, TNF and IL-1β responses and inhibited the eIF2α–ATF4 stress pathway. An oxytocin-receptor antagonist reversed the suppression in the tested model.

Recorded limitations

The study used a mouse cell line and small numbers of independent experiments. NF-κB p65 and p38 were not notably altered at the assessed time points, illustrating that one signalling account does not fit every model. Access: Relevant full-text methods and results reviewed.
