Oxytocin receptor gene methylation in male and female PTSD patients and trauma-exposed controls

Nawijn L., Krzyzewska I.M., van Zuiden M., Henneman P., Koch S.B.J., Mul A.N., Frijling J.L., Veltman D.J., Mannens M.M.A.M., Olff M. · European Neuropsychopharmacology · 2019 · Human case-control OXTR methylation study

Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

This study examines OXTR methylation in PTSD and trauma-exposed controls and reports sex-dependent associations relevant to the oxytocin-system research rationale.

Recorded limitations

The modest sample and observational design limit interpretation. OXTR methylation is not direct receptor activation, and this study does not demonstrate an OXTR-to-DOCK2 intervention pathway. Access: Publication metadata and abstract reviewed.
