A preliminary investigation of rare variants associated with genetic risk for PTSD in a natural disaster-exposed adolescent sample

Sheerin Christina M., Vladimirov Vladimir, Williamson Vernell, Bountress Kaitlin, K. Danielson Carla, Ruggiero Kenneth, Amstadter Ananda B. · European Journal of Psychotraumatology · 2019 · Rare-variant gene-burden analysis; 707 tornado-exposed adolescents

Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

Rare variation in DOCK2 was associated with PTSD symptom burden in this preliminary genetic analysis, providing a distinct line of human evidence.

Recorded limitations

DNA sequence variation is different from methylation or RNA expression. Small numbers of rare-variant carriers and exploratory analyses limit inference. This does not test intervention-related DOCK2 change. Access: Relevant full-text methods and results reviewed.
