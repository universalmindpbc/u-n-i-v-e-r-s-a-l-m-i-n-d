Transcutaneous vagal nerve stimulation blocks stress-induced activation of Interleukin-6 and interferon-γ in posttraumatic stress disorder: A double-blind, randomized, sham-controlled trial.

Bremner JD, Gurel NZ, Jiao Y, Wittbrodt MT, Levantsevych OM, Huang M, Jung H, Shandhi MH, Beckwith J, Herring I, Rapaport MH, Murrah N, Driggers E, Ko YA, Alkhalaf ML, Soudan M, Song J, Ku BS, Shallenberger L, Hankus AN, Nye JA, Park J, Vaccarino V, Shah AJ, Inan OT, Pearce BD. · Brain, behavior, & immunity - health · 2020 · Randomized sham-controlled mechanistic trial

Original publication · Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

Active cervical vagus nerve stimulation blocked script-associated IL-6 and interferon-γ responses in the PTSD subgroup. IL-2, IL-1β and TNF showed no significant group differences.

Recorded limitations

Only ten participants had PTSD. Acute challenge physiology is distinct from sustained clinical benefit; related pilot reports are not independent replications. Access: Abstract and selected full-text passages reviewed.
