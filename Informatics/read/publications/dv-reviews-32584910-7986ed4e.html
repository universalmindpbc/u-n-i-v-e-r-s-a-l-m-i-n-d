A systematic review of intimate partner violence interventions focused on improving social support and/ mental health outcomes of survivors.

Ogbe E; Harmon S; Van den Bergh R; Degomme O · PloS one · 2020 · Systematic scoping review

Original publication · Original publication · Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

Most studies reported better support or mental-health outcomes; evidence for reducing IPV or increasing healthcare use was limited.

Recorded limitations

This is a mixed-methods scoping review, not a meta-analysis. Many interventions are service supports rather than talking therapy.

Chapter Trust Factor uses a minimum of three distinct qualifying studies. Publication records retain their own evidence and assessment.
