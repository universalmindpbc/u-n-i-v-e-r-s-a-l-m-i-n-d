Inflammation biomarkers in suicide attempts and their relation to abuse, global functioning and cognition.

Fernández-Sevillano J, González-Ortega I, MacDowell K, Zorrilla I, López MP, Courtet P, Gabilondo A, Martínez-Cengotitabengoa M, Leza JC, Sáiz P, González-Pinto A. · The world journal of biological psychiatry : the official journal of the World Federation of Societies of Biological Psychiatry · 2022 · Case-control study

Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

Recent and past suicide-attempt groups had higher IL-6 than depressed non-attempters. IL-6 also related to abuse history, functioning and attention.

Recorded limitations

Trauma and illness severity may explain part of the association. Cross-sectional attention associations do not establish a causal route to attempts. Access: Indexed abstract reviewed; full-text methods not comprehensively assessed.
