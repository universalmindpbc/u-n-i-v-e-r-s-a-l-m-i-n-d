Changes in inflammation with treatment for bipolar II depression: Pilot trial data on differential effects of psychotherapy and medication.

Fiedorowicz JG, Cyranowski JM, Liu Z, Swartz HA. · Neurology, psychiatry, and brain research · 2019 · Secondary biomarker analysis of randomized trial

Original publication · Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

Both bipolar II depression treatment groups improved, yet quetiapine plus psychotherapy increased IL-6 and TNF compared with psychotherapy plus placebo. Cytokine change did not correlate with symptom change.

Recorded limitations

Very small selected follow-up sample; useful counterexample to a simple model in which clinical improvement always requires lower IL-6. Access: Indexed abstract reviewed; full-text methods not comprehensively assessed.
