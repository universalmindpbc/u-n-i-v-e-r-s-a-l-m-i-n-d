Feasibility and Effectiveness of Parent-Child Interaction Therapy with Victims of Domestic Violence: A Pilot Study.

Herschell AD; Scudder AB; Schaffner KF; Slagel LA · Journal of child and family studies · 2017 · Uncontrolled feasibility pilot

Original publication · Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

Among treatment completers, PCIT was associated with better child behavior, parenting and mental-health symptoms.

Recorded limitations

Only 9/21 families completed treatment; uncontrolled completer results are highly vulnerable to attrition and natural change.
