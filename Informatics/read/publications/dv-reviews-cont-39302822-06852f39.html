Effectiveness of Father-Focused Interventions to Prevent or Reduce Intimate Partner Violence During Pregnancy and Early Parenthood: A Systematic Review.

Wynter K; Francis LM; Borgkvist A; Dixson B; D'Souza L; Duursma E; May C; Sher L; George JS · Trauma, violence & abuse · 2025 · Systematic review

Original publication · Original publication · Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

Only three of twelve publications with comparison groups reported more robust adjusted evidence of reduced IPV; all three were in lower- or middle-income settings. Other controlled findings were null, limited to particular outcomes, or no longer evident at later follow-up.

Recorded limitations

Poor-to-moderate reporting/method quality; English peer-reviewed literature only, search completed May 2021 despite later publication, mixed prevention populations and incompatible outcomes prevented meta-analysis.

Evidence and provenance

Chapter Trust Factor uses a minimum of three distinct qualifying studies. Publication records retain their own evidence and assessment.
