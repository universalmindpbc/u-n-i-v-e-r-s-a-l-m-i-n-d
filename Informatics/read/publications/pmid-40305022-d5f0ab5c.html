A Neuroimmune Modulator for Alcohol Use Disorder: A Randomized Clinical Trial.

Ray LA, Meredith LR, Grodin EN, Belnap MA, Nieto SJ, Baskerville WA, Donato S, Shoptaw SJ, Gillis AJ, Irwin MR, Miotto K, Enders CK. · JAMA network open · 2025 · Randomized placebo-controlled trial

Original publication · Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

Twelve weeks of ibudilast did not reduce heavy drinking relative to placebo and did not change peripheral inflammatory markers. The primary heavy-drinking estimate was 0.06 (95% CI −0.09 to 0.21).

Recorded limitations

This larger negative trial constrains promising pilot findings. Exploratory sex or depression interactions do not rescue the overall null result. Blood availability fell during pandemic follow-up. Access: Abstract and selected full-text passages reviewed.
