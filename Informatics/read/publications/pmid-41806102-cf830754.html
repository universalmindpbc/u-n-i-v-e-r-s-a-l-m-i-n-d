Identification of intranasal oxytocin plasma proteome signatures

Breithaupt L, Harshman SG, Botros PM, Zhang Z, Holman K, Boutin R, Galbiati F, Maso AA, Lo J, Soukas A, Sadreyev RI, McCormack SE, Lawson EA. · Endocrine · 2026 · Double-blind randomised crossover study in 25 men; proteomics in 19

Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

A single intranasal oxytocin dose altered a plasma proteomic signature over approximately one hour. Analyses covered 4,725 proteins and identified 198 differential proteins, including immune-related pathways.

Recorded limitations

Exploratory proteomic changes do not identify a validated anti-inflammatory clinical response. Small acute male-only sampling, assay multiplicity and the need for independent targeted replication limit translation. Access: Indexed abstract and bibliographic record reviewed.
