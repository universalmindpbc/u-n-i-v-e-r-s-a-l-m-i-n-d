Cytokine alterations in panic disorder: A systematic review.

Quagliato LA, Nardi AE. · Journal of affective disorders · 2018 · Systematic review

Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

Some panic-disorder studies reported increased IL-6 and other cytokines, but the review emphasized discrepant findings and assay/population differences.

Recorded limitations

Small heterogeneous literature and no pooled causal estimate. This earlier review overlaps later panic meta-analyses. Access: Indexed abstract reviewed; full-text methods not comprehensively assessed.
