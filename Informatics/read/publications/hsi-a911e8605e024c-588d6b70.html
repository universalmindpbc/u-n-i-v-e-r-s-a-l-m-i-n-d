Smartphone-based hyperspectral imaging for ice sheet and proglacial applications in South-West Greenland.

Stuart MB, Davies M, Fisk C, Allen E, Sole AJ, Ing R, Hobbs MJ, Willmott JR. · The Science of the total environment · 2024 · Journal publication

Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

A smartphone-based hyperspectral instrument was deployed at supraglacial and proglacial sites in Greenland to collect visible and near-infrared environmental data.

Recorded limitations

This is a field-imaging instrument study; its findings do not establish biological or clinical measurements from a standard phone camera.
