Psychological therapies for women who experience intimate partner violence.

Hameed M; O'Doherty L; Gilchrist G; Tirado-Muñoz J; Taft A; Chondros P; Feder G; Tan M; Hegarty K · The Cochrane database of systematic reviews · 2020 · Systematic review and meta-analysis

Original publication · Original publication · Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

Therapies probably modestly reduced depression at medium-term follow-up; evidence for PTSD, safety planning and repeat IPV remained uncertain.

Recorded limitations

Allocation concealment was adequately described in only 12 trials. Harms were poorly measured; dropout similarity is not proof of no harm.

Evidence and provenance

Chapter Trust Factor uses a minimum of three distinct qualifying studies. Publication records retain their own evidence and assessment.
