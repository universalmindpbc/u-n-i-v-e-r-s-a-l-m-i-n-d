Inflammatory and oxidative stress markers in post-traumatic stress disorder: a systematic review and meta-analysis.

Peruzzolo TL, Pinto JV, Roza TH, Shintani AO, Anzolin AP, Gnielka V, Kohmann AM, Marin AS, Lorenzon VR, Brunoni AR, Kapczinski F, Passos IC. · Molecular psychiatry · 2022 · Systematic review and meta-analysis

Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

IL-6 was elevated in PTSD (standardized difference 0.94; 95% CI 0.36–1.52), along with CRP and TNF; oxidative markers did not show consistent associations.

Recorded limitations

The total 8394 participants spans multiple markers. Pooled observational associations do not demonstrate that suppressing IL-6 improves PTSD. Access: Indexed abstract reviewed; full-text methods not comprehensively assessed.
