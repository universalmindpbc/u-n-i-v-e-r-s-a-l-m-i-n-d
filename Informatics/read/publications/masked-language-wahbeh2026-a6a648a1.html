Digital meditation at scale: a randomized citizen science trial comparing four meditation practices

BMC Psychology · 2026 · Randomized digital trial; 1,211 participants assigned to meditation or music conditions

Original publication

Recorded summary

Four weeks of practice were assessed with subjective, EEG and HRV measures. The preregistered primary findings did not survive multiplicity correction.

Recorded limitations

Active practices, including mantra, differ from masked playback; substantial attrition complicates interpretation.
