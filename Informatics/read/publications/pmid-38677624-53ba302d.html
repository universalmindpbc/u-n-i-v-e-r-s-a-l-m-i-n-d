The antidepressant effect of whole-body hyperthermia is associated with the classical interleukin-6 signaling pathway.

Mac Giollabhui N, Lowry CA, Nyer M, Foster SL, Liu RT, Smith DG, Cole SP, Mason AE, Mischoulon D, Raison CL. · Brain, behavior, and immunity · 2024 · Secondary biomarker analysis of randomized sham trial

Original publication · Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

An acute increase in the IL-6-to-soluble-IL-6-receptor ratio after whole-body hyperthermia was associated with later depression improvement, consistent with a proposed classical-signalling mechanism.

Recorded limitations

Small secondary analysis; a circulating ratio is not direct proof of cellular signalling. Illustrates context-dependent biology rather than a universal benefit of IL-6 suppression. Linked correction notice identified; its full content remains unresolved in this appraisal. Access: Indexed abstract reviewed; full-text methods not comprehensively assessed.
