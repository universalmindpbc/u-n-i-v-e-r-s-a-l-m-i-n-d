Phase-based treatment versus immediate trauma-focused treatment for post-traumatic stress disorder due to childhood abuse: randomised clinical trial

Noortje I. van Vliet; Rafaele J. C. Huntjens; Maarten K. van Dijk; Nathan Bachrach; Marie-Louise Meewisse; Ad de Jongh · BJPsych Open · 2021 · Single-blind randomized controlled trial

Original publication · Original publication · Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

Adding STAIR produced no significant advantage over immediate EMDR at post-treatment or follow-up.

Recorded limitations

No inactive control; null superiority is not proof of equivalence. Historical abuse findings do not establish care for ongoing IPV.

Evidence and provenance

Chapter Trust Factor uses a minimum of three distinct qualifying studies. Publication records retain their own evidence and assessment.
