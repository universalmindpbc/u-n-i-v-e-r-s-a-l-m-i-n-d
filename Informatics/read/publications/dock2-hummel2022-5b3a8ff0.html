No evidence for intervention-associated DNA methylation changes in monocytes of patients with posttraumatic stress disorder

Hummel Elisabeth, Elgizouli Magdeldin, Sicorello Maurizio, Leitão Elsa, Beygo Jasmin, Schröder Christopher, Zeschnigk Michael, Müller Svenja, Herpertz Stephan, Moser Dirk, Kessler Henrik, Horsthemke Bernhard, Kumsta Robert · Scientific Reports · 2022 · Pre/post treatment study; isolated monocytes from 60 women with PTSD

Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

Despite symptom improvement, the study found no major recurrent treatment-associated methylation changes in the tested regions of NR3C1, FKBP5, SLC6A4 or OXTR. It highlights the value of controlling blood-cell composition.

Recorded limitations

DOCK2 was not one of these four targeted genes. Only one cell type was examined; exploratory whole-genome sequencing used two patients. RNA expression may change independently of methylation. Access: Relevant full-text methods and results reviewed.
