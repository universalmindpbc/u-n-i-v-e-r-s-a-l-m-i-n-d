Meta-analysis of cytokine alterations in schizophrenia: clinical status and antipsychotic effects.

Miller BJ, Buckley P, Seabolt W, Mellor A, Kirkpatrick B. · Biological psychiatry · 2011 · Meta-analysis

Original publication · Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

IL-6 was elevated in acute relapse and first-episode psychosis and decreased after antipsychotic treatment; stable medicated outpatients did not differ from controls in this earlier synthesis.

Recorded limitations

BMI and smoking were often uncontrolled. Later larger syntheses report some persistent elevations, so the state-versus-trait distinction remains unsettled. Access: Indexed abstract reviewed; full-text methods not comprehensively assessed.
