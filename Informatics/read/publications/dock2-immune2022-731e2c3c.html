Central and Peripheral Immune Dysregulation in Posttraumatic Stress Disorder: Convergent Multi-Omics Evidence

Núñez-Rios Diana L., Martínez-Magaña José J., Nagamatsu Sheila T., Andrade-Brito Diego E., Forero Diego A., Orozco-Castaño Carlos A., Montalvo-Ortiz Janitza L. · Biomedicines · 2022 · Review with cross-study convergence analysis

Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

The authors identify DOCK2 among immune-related genes overlapping animal-model and human postmortem brain transcriptomic findings. This provides a research lead linking peripheral associations with central immune biology.

Recorded limitations

The DOCK2 convergence statement was reviewed in the synthesis; a DOCK2-specific effect estimate from each underlying brain dataset was not independently reconstructed. It supplies mechanistic context, not proof of OXTR mediation or a validated intervention endpoint. Access: Relevant full-text methods and results reviewed.
