Probiotic Lactobacillus Plantarum 299v decreases kynurenine concentration and improves cognitive functions in patients with major depression: A double-blind, randomized, placebo controlled study.

Rudzki L, Ostrowska L, Pawlak D, Małus A, Pawlak K, Waszkiewicz N, Szulc A. · Psychoneuroendocrinology · 2019 · Randomized placebo-controlled trial

Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

Probiotic augmentation improved selected cognitive measures and reduced kynurenine, without significant IL-6, TNF or IL-1β changes.

Recorded limitations

Attrition and multiple endpoints limit inference. Cognitive or gut–brain benefits cannot automatically be attributed to IL-6 suppression. Access: Indexed abstract reviewed; full-text methods not comprehensively assessed.
