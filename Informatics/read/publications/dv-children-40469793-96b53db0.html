Video Narrative Exposure Therapy (NET) with Children and Young People who Witnessed Domestic Violence: A Naturalistic Single Case Study Series.

Rocca F; Schröder T; Schröder T; Golijani-Moghaddam N; Wilde S · Journal of child & adolescent trauma · 2025 · Naturalistic AB single-case series

Original publication · Original publication · Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

Three of five adolescents showed reliable PTSD-symptom improvement, but only one met clinically significant change criteria. General distress improved reliably for one participant.

Recorded limitations

Five female adolescents, no comparison group, short follow-up and missing follow-up data limit causal and generalizable claims.

Evidence and provenance

Chapter Trust Factor uses a minimum of three distinct qualifying studies. Publication records retain their own evidence and assessment.
