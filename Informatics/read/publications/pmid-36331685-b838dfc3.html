A Pilot Study of a Three-Session Heart Rate Variability Biofeedback Intervention for Veterans with Posttraumatic Stress Disorder

Schuman Donna L., Lawrence Karen A., Boggero Ian, Naegele Peter, Ginsberg J. P., Casto Ashley, Moser Debra K. · Applied Psychophysiology and Biofeedback · 2022 · Randomised controlled study

Original publication

Recorded summary

A randomised waitlist pilot of three clinical HRV feedback sessions reported improvements in depression and an HRV measure within the intervention arm. Home-practice adherence was limited.

Recorded limitations

The veteran waitlist pilot used clinical HRV biofeedback and a smartphone with a sensor for home practice.
