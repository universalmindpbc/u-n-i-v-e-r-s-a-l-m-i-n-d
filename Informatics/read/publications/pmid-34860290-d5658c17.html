Virtual Reality Biofeedback in Health: A Scoping Review

Lüddecke Robin, Felnhofer Anna · Applied Psychophysiology and Biofeedback · 2021 · Review or conceptual paper

Original publication

Recorded summary

Eighteen studies, mainly in adults, reported outcomes including stress, anxiety and pain. The authors found little evidence for an efficacy advantage over conventional feedback.

Recorded limitations

Scoping review of VR biofeedback in health.
