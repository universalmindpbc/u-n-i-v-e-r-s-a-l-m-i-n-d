Original publication · Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

The review reported a large average effect and stronger results for IPV-tailored CBT.

Recorded limitations

The abstract does not establish whether the overall effect is based on controlled comparisons or pre/post change. Few rigorous studies; do not interpret the aggregate as a definitive comparative treatment effect.
