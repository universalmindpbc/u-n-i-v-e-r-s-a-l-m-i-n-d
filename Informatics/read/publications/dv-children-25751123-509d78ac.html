Mediators and Treatment Factors in Intervention for Children Exposed to Interparental Violence.

Overbeek MM; De Schipper JC; Willemen AM; Lamers-Winkelman F; Schuengel C · Journal of clinical child and adolescent psychology : the official journal for the Society of Clinical Child and Adolescent Psychology, American Psychological Association, Division 53 · 2017 · Secondary mediation analysis of a randomized trial

Original publication · Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

Improved parental mental health mediated associations between nonspecific treatment factors and reduced child posttraumatic symptoms. Trauma-specific child components had mixed associations.

Recorded limitations

Mediation analyses cannot isolate causal ingredients;134 completed assessments versus 155 in the original trial, and multiple correlated treatment factors complicate attribution.

Evidence and provenance
