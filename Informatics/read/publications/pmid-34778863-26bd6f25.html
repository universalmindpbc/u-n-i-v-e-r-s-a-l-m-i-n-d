Transcutaneous Cervical Vagal Nerve Stimulation in Patients with Posttraumatic Stress Disorder (PTSD): A Pilot Study of Effects on PTSD Symptoms and Interleukin-6 Response to Stress.

Bremner JD, Wittbrodt MT, Gurel NZ, Shandhi MH, Gazi AH, Jiao Y, Levantsevych OM, Huang M, Beckwith J, Herring I, Murrah N, Driggers EG, Ko YA, Alkhalaf ML, Soudan M, Shallenberger L, Hankus AN, Nye JA, Park J, Woodbury A, Mehta PK, Rapaport MH, Vaccarino V, Shah AJ, Pearce BD, Inan OT. · Journal of affective disorders reports · 2021 · Randomized sham-controlled pilot trial

Original publication · Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

Three months of cervical vagus nerve stimulation reduced self-reported PTSD symptoms and blocked IL-6 responses to traumatic scripts in this pilot.

Recorded limitations

Small trial, device-specific exposure and subsequent open-label phase; related acute-stress reports require trial-family caution. No OXTR mediation was tested. Access: Indexed abstract reviewed; full-text methods not comprehensively assessed.
