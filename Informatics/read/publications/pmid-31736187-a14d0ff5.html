Stress-related suppression of peripheral cytokines predicts future relapse in alcohol-dependent individuals with and without subclinical depression.

Fox HC, Milivojevic V, MacDougall A, LaVallee H, Simpson C, Angarita GA, Sinha R. · Addiction biology · 2020 · Counterbalanced stress experiment with prospective relapse follow-up

Original publication · Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

Alcohol dependence was associated with blunted IL-6 and IL-1RA responses to stress. Lower post-stress IL-6 predicted more subsequent drinking days.

Recorded limitations

Randomization concerned stress-task order, not diagnosis or IL-6 treatment. This counterexample distinguishes impaired stress reactivity from high resting inflammation. Access: Indexed abstract reviewed; full-text methods not comprehensively assessed.
