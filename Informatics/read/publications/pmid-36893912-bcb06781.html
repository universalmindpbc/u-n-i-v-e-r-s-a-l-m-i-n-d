Peripheral cytokine levels across psychiatric disorders: A systematic review and network meta-analysis.

Zhang Y, Wang J, Ye Y, Zou Y, Chen W, Wang Z, Zou Z. · Progress in neuro-psychopharmacology & biological psychiatry · 2023 · Systematic review and network meta-analysis

Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

IL-6 was elevated relative to healthy controls across the psychiatric evidence network, without clear IL-6 differences between the compared diagnoses. Other cytokines showed different patterns.

Recorded limitations

Indirect comparisons and heterogeneous primary studies cannot establish that every person or every disorder shares one causal pathway; no diagnostic specificity was established. Access: Indexed abstract reviewed; full-text methods not comprehensively assessed.
