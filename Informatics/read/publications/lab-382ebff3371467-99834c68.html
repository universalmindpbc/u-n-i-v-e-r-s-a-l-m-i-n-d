Virtual reality experiences for breathing and relaxation training: The effects of real vs. placebo biofeedback

Chittaro Luca, Serafini Marta, Vulcano Yvonne · International Journal of Human-Computer Studies · 2024 · Participant study

Original publication

Recorded summary

In a 35-person VR experiment, real-time breathing, cardiac and skin signals produced greater relaxation and presence than replayed signals from another participant.

Recorded limitations

The environment responded to the participant’s breathing, heart rate and skin conductance, or to another participant’s replay.
