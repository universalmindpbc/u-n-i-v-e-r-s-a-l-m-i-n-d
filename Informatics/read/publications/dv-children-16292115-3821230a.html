Toward evidence-based treatment: child-parent psychotherapy with preschoolers exposed to marital violence.

Lieberman AF; Van Horn P; Ippen CG · Journal of the American Academy of Child and Adolescent Psychiatry · 2005 · Randomized controlled trial

Original publication · Original publication · Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

CPP produced greater improvements in child behavior problems, traumatic-stress symptoms and diagnostic status than the comparison condition.

Recorded limitations

Small sample; inconsistent denominators; parent-reported outcomes; occasional assessor unmasking; maternal PTSD/general-distress differences were trends.
