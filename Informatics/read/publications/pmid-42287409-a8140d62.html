Inflammatory cytokine alterations in autism spectrum disorder and attention-deficit/hyperactivity disorder: a systematic review and network meta-analysis.

Chen Q, Sun Y, Pan N, Long Y, Cao Y, Luo Y, Xin K, Chen P, Wang Y, Shu M, Chen Y, Gong Q. · European child & adolescent psychiatry · 2026 · Systematic review and network meta-analysis

Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

The analysis found distinct immune patterns: elevated IL-6 was highlighted for autism, while ADHD findings centred on lower TNF, IL-2 and MCP-1.

Recorded limitations

The autism result cannot be transferred to ADHD. Most of the 74 studies were not ADHD studies, and the review does not validate an IL-6 diagnostic test. Access: Indexed abstract reviewed; full-text methods not comprehensively assessed.
