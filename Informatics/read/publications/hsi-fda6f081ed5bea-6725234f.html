Hyperspectral Information Extraction With Full Resolution From Arbitrary Photographs.

Kwon S, Mok Park S, Ji Y, Sakthivel H, Woo Leem J, Kim YL. · IEEE transactions on image processing : a publication of the IEEE Signal Processing Society · 2025 · Journal publication

Original publication · Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

Computational photography spectrometry combines a physical spectral colour chart with an algorithm to reconstruct visible-range spectra from RGB photographs. The study tests transmission and reflection modes.

Recorded limitations

The method depends on a reference chart and its optical acquisition conditions. It does not establish unrestricted infrared recovery from an uncalibrated photograph.
