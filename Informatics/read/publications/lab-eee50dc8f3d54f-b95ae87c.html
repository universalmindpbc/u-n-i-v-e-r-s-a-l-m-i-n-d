Ego-depletion and motor skill performance under pressure—experimental effects of a short term virtual-reality based mindfulness breathing meditation with integrated biofeedback

Wagner Matthias, Wieczorek Alissa · Scientific Reports · 2024 · Randomised controlled crossover study

Original publication

Recorded summary

Two small crossover experiments tested basketball free throws and football penalties. VR breathing meditation with feedback improved task performance against a rest break. It was not a test of mental-health treatment.

Recorded limitations

Abdominal movement during exhalation changed the virtual environment. The testing staff were masked to condition.
