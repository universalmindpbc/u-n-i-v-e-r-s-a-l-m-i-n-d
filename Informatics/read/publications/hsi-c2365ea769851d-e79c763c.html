Quantum dot-enabled infrared hyperspectral imaging with single-pixel detection.

Meng H, Gao Y, Wang X, Li X, Wang L, Zhao X, Sun B. · Light, science & applications · 2024 · Journal publication

Original publication · Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

The laboratory system combines quantum-dot filters, a digital micromirror device and a single-pixel detector to reconstruct near-infrared spectral images.

Recorded limitations

The demonstrated performance belongs to this optical hardware and reconstruction method; it is not an unmodified smartphone RGB camera.
