Genomewide DNA methylation analysis in combat veterans reveals a novel locus for PTSD

Mehta D, Mehta D, Bruenig D, Carrillo-Roa T, Lawford B, Harvey W, Morris CP, Smith AK, Binder EB, Young RM, Voisey J. · Acta psychiatrica Scandinavica · 2017 · Human blood methylation and expression; 96 veterans and 115 independent replication participants

Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

Lower methylation at DOCK2 cg16277944 was associated with greater PTSD symptom severity and replicated in a separate cohort (p = 0.0028). Post-hoc blood analyses found a modest inverse methylation–expression association (r = −0.21) and a group difference in DOCK2 expression.

Recorded limitations

Cross-sectional, male samples; discovery used a 10% false-discovery-rate threshold. Association does not establish intervention responsiveness. Genetic variation also influenced methylation. The 211 participants are the total across both cohorts, not the discovery sample alone.
