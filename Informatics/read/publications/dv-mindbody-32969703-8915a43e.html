Effects of mindfulness training on posttraumatic stress symptoms from a community-based pilot clinical trial among survivors of intimate partner violence.

Gallegos AM; Heffner KL; Cerulli C; Luck P; McGuinness S; Pigeon WR · Psychological trauma : theory, research, practice and policy · 2020 · Pilot RCT

Original publication · Original publication · Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

Comparative efficacy remains unproven; fuller methods reveal substantial differential follow-up loss and unequal intervention contact.

Recorded limitations

Small pilot, differential missingness, unblinded assessment, unmatched comparator, reporting inconsistencies and retrospective registration.

Evidence and provenance

Chapter Trust Factor uses a minimum of three distinct qualifying studies. Publication records retain their own evidence and assessment.
