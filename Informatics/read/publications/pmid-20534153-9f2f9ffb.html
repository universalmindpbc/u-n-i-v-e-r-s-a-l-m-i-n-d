Attention-deficit hyperactivity disorder (ADHD) and glial integrity: an exploration of associations of cytokines and kynurenine metabolites with symptoms and attention.

Oades RD, Myint AM, Dauvermann MR, Schimmelmann BG, Schwarz MJ. · Behavioral and brain functions : BBF · 2010 · Exploratory case-control study

Original publication · Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

Cytokine–symptom relationships were heterogeneous; IL-6 related to opposition ratings in controls, while other cytokines carried the main ADHD associations.

Recorded limitations

Small sample with medicated and unmedicated participants and many biomarkers. This is not evidence for a uniform IL-6 abnormality in ADHD. Access: Indexed abstract reviewed; full-text methods not comprehensively assessed.
