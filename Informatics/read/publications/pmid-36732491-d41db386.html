Acute stress responses of autonomous nervous system, HPA axis, and inflammatory system in posttraumatic stress disorder.

von Majewski K, Kraus O, Rhein C, Lieb M, Erim Y, Rohleder N. · Translational psychiatry · 2023 · Controlled laboratory stress study

Original publication · Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

PTSD patients had higher baseline IL-6 and altered autonomic/endocrine measures, but broadly similar acute IL-6 stress responses to controls.

Recorded limitations

Small nonrandomized comparison; baseline inflammation and challenge reactivity are distinct phenomena. Access: Indexed abstract reviewed; full-text methods not comprehensively assessed.
