Screening and counselling in the primary care setting for women who have experienced intimate partner violence (WEAVE): a cluster randomised controlled trial.

Hegarty K; O'Doherty L; Taft A; Chondros P; Brown S; Valpied J; Astbury J; Taket A; Gold L; Feder G; Gunn J · Lancet (London, England) · 2013 · Cluster randomized controlled trial

Original publication · Original publication · Original publication · Original publication · Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

Original abstract reports null 12-month primary outcomes and a secondary depression advantage (OR 0.3, 95% CI 0.1–0.7). Same-cohort 24-month follow-up did not preserve that advantage; no new replication is implied.

Recorded limitations

Original 2013 fulltext inaccessible; selected shared methods come from later primary report and harms qualification from a same-family abstract. Low uptake, unmasked self-report and loss to follow-up limit inference; secondary benefit was not durable at 24 months.

Evidence and provenance

Chapter Trust Factor uses a minimum of three distinct qualifying studies. Publication records retain their own evidence and assessment.
