Association of interleukin-6 with suicidal ideation in veterans: a longitudinal perspective.

Sun S, Wilson CM, Alter S, Ge Y, Hazlett EA, Goodman M, Yehuda R, Galfalvy H, Haghighi F. · Frontiers in psychiatry · 2023 · Longitudinal veteran cohort

Original publication · Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

IL-6 was associated with severity of suicidal ideation, but did not predict its presence (OR 1.34; 95% CI 0.77–2.33). Cytokines declined over time in depressed attempters.

Recorded limitations

The negative presence result limits screening claims. Not all groups received identical longitudinal follow-up; no validated clinical suicide-risk test resulted. Access: Indexed abstract reviewed; full-text methods not comprehensively assessed.
