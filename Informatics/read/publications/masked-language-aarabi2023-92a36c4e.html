Effect of Subliminal Auditory Stimulation on Components of Auditory Late Responses and Functional Magnetic Resonance Imaging Data in Adults with Normal Hearing

Auditory and Vestibular Research · 2023 · Small controlled exposure study; EEG and fMRI

Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

Words masked beneath instrumental music were presented repeatedly over ten days. The study reported changes in auditory evoked-potential amplitudes and some fMRI responses.

Recorded limitations

Small groups and incompletely clear allocation and participant flow. Responses to audible test words were measured after exposure; structural brain change and emotional benefit were not established.
