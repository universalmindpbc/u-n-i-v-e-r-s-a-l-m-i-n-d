Feeling-the-Beat: Enhancing Empathy and Engagement During Public Speaking Through Heart Rate Sharing

2024 · Participant study

Original publication

Recorded summary

In a 36-person experiment comparing real, fabricated and absent heart-rate sharing, audience members reported greater empathy towards the speaker with the system. This is a context-specific self-report.

Recorded limitations

The system shared heart rate between speakers and audience members. The comparison used fabricated or absent signals.
