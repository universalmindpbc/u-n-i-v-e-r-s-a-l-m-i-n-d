Aberrations in peripheral blood interleukin-6 levels in post-traumatic stress disorder: A systematic review and meta-analysis.

Yang L, Meng M, Tian M, Zhang C, Zou J, Cai L, Qian C, Jiang H, Li C, Liu Z, Nie C, Gao J, Chen X, Liu M. · Behavioural brain research · 2026 · Systematic review and meta-analysis

Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

Peripheral IL-6 was moderately higher in PTSD (g=0.493; 95% CI 0.275–0.710). The association persisted in sensitivity analyses despite detected publication bias.

Recorded limitations

Sex and control-trauma subgroups differed; the result is not a universal biomarker. Multiple studies within articles and overlapping earlier reviews require counting caution. Access: Indexed abstract reviewed; full-text methods not comprehensively assessed.
