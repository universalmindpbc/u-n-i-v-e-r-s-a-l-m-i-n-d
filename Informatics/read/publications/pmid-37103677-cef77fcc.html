Effects of IL-6/IL-6R axis alterations in serum, plasma and cerebrospinal fluid with the schizophrenia: an updated review and meta-analysis of 58 studies.

González-Castro TB, Tovilla-Zárate CA, Juárez-Rojop IE, Hernández-Díaz Y, López-Narváez ML, Ortiz-Ojeda RF. · Molecular and cellular biochemistry · 2024 · Systematic review and meta-analysis

Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

The synthesis reported increased IL-6 in blood and CSF, with lower serum IL-6 receptor levels in treated patients.

Recorded limitations

Different tissues, receptor measures and treatment states cannot be collapsed into one concentration or causal interpretation. Access: Indexed abstract reviewed; full-text methods not comprehensively assessed.
