Peripheral Inflammatory Markers in Autism Spectrum Disorder and Attention Deficit/Hyperactivity Disorder at Adolescent Age.

Ferencova N, Visnovcova Z, Ondrejka I, Hrtanek I, Bujnakova I, Kovacova V, Macejova A, Tonhajzerova I. · International journal of molecular sciences · 2023 · Case-control study

Original publication · Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

IL-6 and several other inflammatory markers were higher in ADHD adolescents; patterns varied by sex and differed from autism.

Recorded limitations

Only five girls per group and many comparisons; sex-specific estimates are especially unstable and require replication. Access: Indexed abstract reviewed; full-text methods not comprehensively assessed.
