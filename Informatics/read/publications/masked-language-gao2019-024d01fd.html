The neurophysiological correlates of religious chanting

Gao J, Leung HK, Wu BWY, Skouras S, Sik HH. · Scientific Reports · 2019 · EEG and ECG in experienced practitioners; supplementary single-person fMRI

Original publication

Recorded summary

Silent internal religious chanting was associated with neural and cardiac differences across comparison conditions.

Recorded limitations

Participants actively recited internally. This is silent mental mantra, a different intervention from an external masked recording.
