Is there an effect of subliminal messages in music on choice behavior?

Journal of Articles in Support of the Null Hypothesis · 2006 · Participant-blinded controlled experiments; 66 adults and 82 children

Original publication

Recorded summary

Messages embedded in music did not reliably change the tested word or drink choices.

Recorded limitations

Choice outcomes differ from neural encoding, autonomic response and longer-term learning.
