Peripheral levels of C-reactive protein, tumor necrosis factor-α, interleukin-6, and interleukin-1β across the mood spectrum in bipolar disorder: A meta-analysis of mean differences and variability.

Solmi M, Suresh Sharma M, Osimo EF, Fornaro M, Bortolato B, Croatto G, Miola A, Vieta E, Pariante CM, Smith L, Fusar-Poli P, Shin JI, Berk M, Carvalho AF. · Brain, behavior, and immunity · 2021 · Systematic review and meta-analysis

Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

IL-6 was elevated across manic, depressed and euthymic groups in this analysis, with an overall standardized difference of 0.81. CRP and TNF showed stronger episode dependence.

Recorded limitations

Between-study heterogeneity, medication and metabolic factors remain relevant. A group-level trait-like pattern does not validate an individual biomarker or treatment target. Access: Indexed abstract reviewed; full-text methods not comprehensively assessed.
