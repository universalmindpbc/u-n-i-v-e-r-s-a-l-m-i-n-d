Inflammatory markers changes following acceptance-based behavioral psychotherapy in generalized anxiety disorder patients: Evidence from a randomized controlled trial.

Gandarela L, de A Sampaio TP, Marçal L, Burdmann EA, Neto FL, Bernik MA. · Brain, behavior, & immunity - health · 2024 · Randomized active-control psychotherapy trial

Original publication · Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

Acceptance-based therapy improved symptoms but did not reduce the inflammatory profile relative to supportive therapy. IL-6 increased in the experimental group; most symptom–biomarker correlations were absent.

Recorded limitations

Small short trial with an active comparator. Results challenge an obligatory IL-6-lowering mechanism for anxiety improvement. Access: Abstract and selected full-text passages reviewed.
