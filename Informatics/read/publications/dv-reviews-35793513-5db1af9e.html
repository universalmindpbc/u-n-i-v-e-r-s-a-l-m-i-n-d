Motivational Interview Techniques and the Effectiveness of Intervention Programs With Perpetrators of Intimate Partner Violence: A Systematic Review.

Pinto E Silva T; Cunha O; Caridade S · Trauma, violence & abuse · 2023 · Systematic review

Original publication · Original publication · Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

The review reported improvements in engagement, adherence and motivation, especially for participants initially less ready to change.

Recorded limitations

Narrative findings on attendance and attitudes do not establish reduced partner harm or recidivism. Quantitative pooled effects were not provided.
