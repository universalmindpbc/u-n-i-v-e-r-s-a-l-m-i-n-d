Culturally adapted trauma-focused CBT-based guided self-help (CatCBT GSH) for female victims of domestic violence in Pakistan: feasibility randomized controlled trial.

Latif M; Husain MI; Gul M; Naz S; Irfan M; Aslam M; Awan F; Sharif A; Rathod S; Farooq S; Ayub M; Naeem F · Behavioural and cognitive psychotherapy · 2021 · Randomized controlled trial

Original publication · Original publication · Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

The guided self-help trial supported feasibility and acceptability, with improvements in secondary PTSD, depression, anxiety and functioning measures.

Recorded limitations

Small feasibility trial with a waitlist control; secondary symptom findings require a larger confirmatory trial. Masking and effect-size confidence intervals were not established from the abstract.

Evidence and provenance

Chapter Trust Factor uses a minimum of three distinct qualifying studies. Publication records retain their own evidence and assessment.
