Young children's experiences of participating in group treatment for children exposed to intimate partner violence: A qualitative study.

Pernebo K; Almqvist K · Clinical child psychology and psychiatry · 2016 · Qualitative child interview study

Original publication · Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

Children described positive feelings, safety, relationships, opportunities to discuss violence and new skills in group treatment.

Recorded limitations

Nine interviewed children provide experience data, not controlled evidence of symptom benefit; selection and interview interpretation may influence findings.
