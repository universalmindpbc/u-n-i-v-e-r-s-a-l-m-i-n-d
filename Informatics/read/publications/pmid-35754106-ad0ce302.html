Effects of ibudilast on central and peripheral markers of inflammation in alcohol use disorder: A randomized clinical trial.

Grodin EN, Nieto SJ, Meredith LR, Burnette E, O'Neill J, Alger J, London ED, Miotto K, Miotto K, Evans CJ, Irwin MR, Ray LA. · Addiction biology · 2022 · Randomized placebo-controlled biomarker trial

Original publication · Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

Two weeks of ibudilast produced differences in selected spectroscopy metabolites and nominal peripheral CRP and TNF-to-IL-10 findings. The abstract does not establish a specific IL-6 treatment effect.

Recorded limitations

Small exploratory study with multiple biomarkers; spectroscopy metabolites are indirect indicators of inflammation. Related drinking reports share the trial, and the later 102-person trial was negative. Access: Indexed abstract reviewed; full-text methods not comprehensively assessed.
