Oxidative Stress and Inflammatory Biomarkers in People with Methamphetamine Use Disorder.

Turan Ç, Şenormancı G, Neşelioğlu S, Budak Y, Erel Ö, Şenormancı Ö. · Clinical psychopharmacology and neuroscience : the official scientific journal of the Korean College of Neuropsychopharmacology · 2023 · Case-control study

Original publication · Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

IL-6 did not differ between groups despite differences in oxidative and blood-cell inflammatory indices. Duration of use related to IL-6 within the analysis.

Recorded limitations

Modest sample and cross-sectional design; retained null evidence demonstrates that other inflammatory abnormalities do not guarantee an IL-6 elevation. Access: Indexed abstract reviewed; full-text methods not comprehensively assessed.
