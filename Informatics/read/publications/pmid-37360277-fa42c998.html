Inaccuracies in plasma oxytocin extraction and enzyme immunoassay techniques

Gan HW, Leeson C, Aitkenhead H, Dattani M. · Comprehensive psychoneuroendocrinology · 2023 · Oxytocin extraction and enzyme-immunoassay validation experiments

Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

Extraction procedures produced substantial and variable analyte loss. The investigators evaluated a particular non-extracted assay approach, illustrating that analytical validity depends on the complete method.

Recorded limitations

The result does not validate all commercial assays or justify a universal extraction rule. Recovery, specificity, dilution behaviour, precision and matrix effects need evaluation for the selected platform. Access: Relevant full-text methods and results reviewed.
