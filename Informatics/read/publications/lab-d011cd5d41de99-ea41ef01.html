Pan Ziqi, Comley Enego, Cristea Alexandra I. · 2026 12th International Conference on Virtual Reality (ICVR) · 2026 · Participant study

Original publication

Recorded summary

An IEEE conference paper describes a preliminary usability evaluation of EEG-driven visual feedback. Clinical efficacy remains untested.

Recorded limitations

The authors describe a preliminary evaluation in which an EEG-derived relaxation estimate drove visual feedback. The accessible abstract gives limited detail about the calculation.
