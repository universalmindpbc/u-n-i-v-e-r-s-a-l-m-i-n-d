CD38 Deficiency Protects Mouse Retinal Ganglion Cells Through Activating the NAD+/Sirt1 Pathway in Ischemia-Reperfusion and Optic Nerve Crush Models

Pang Y, Hu H, Xu K, Cao T, Wang Z, Nie J, Zheng H, Luo H, Wang F, Xiong C, Deng KY, Xin HB, Zhang X. · Investigative ophthalmology & visual science · 2024 · Mouse retinal-injury and gene-deletion experiments

Original publication · Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

CD38 deletion reduced retinal injury and inflammatory responses, with evidence implicating the NAD+/Sirt1 pathway.

Recorded limitations

Gene deletion and retinal-injury models do not establish that visual exposure regulates CD38. NAD+ is a metabolite, not a gene.
