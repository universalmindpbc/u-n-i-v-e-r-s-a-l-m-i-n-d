Effects of SSRIs on peripheral inflammatory cytokines in patients with Generalized Anxiety Disorder.

Hou R, Ye G, Liu Y, Chen X, Pan M, Zhu F, Fu J, Fu T, Liu Q, Gao Z, Baldwin DS, Tang Z. · Brain, behavior, and immunity · 2019 · Prospective treatment cohort

Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

After 12 weeks of escitalopram or sertraline, anxiety and several cytokines including IL-6 declined. Baseline IL-6 and CRP predicted response in the cohort.

Recorded limitations

No randomized placebo comparison; time, regression to the mean and other treatment effects remain alternative explanations. Prediction requires external validation. Access: Indexed abstract reviewed; full-text methods not comprehensively assessed.
