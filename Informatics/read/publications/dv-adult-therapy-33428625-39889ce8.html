Interpersonal psychotherapy delivered by nonspecialists for depression and posttraumatic stress disorder among Kenyan HIV-positive women affected by gender-based violence: Randomized controlled trial.

Meffert SM; Neylan TC; McCulloch CE; Blum K; Cohen CR; Bukusi EA; Verdeli H; Markowitz JC; Kahn JG; Bukusi D; Thirumurthy H; Rota G; Rota R; Oketch G; Opiyo E; Ongeri L · PLoS medicine · 2021 · Randomized controlled trial

Original publication · Original publication · Original publication · Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

At three months, IPT reduced odds of major depression and PTSD in the mixed-GBV cohort. Partnered-subgroup IPV findings were exploratory and vulnerable to attrition/relationship-status changes.

Recorded limitations

Sequence visible to coordinator; differential attrition and mixed GBV exposure. Later follow-up lacks an untreated comparison and cannot establish persistent comparative benefit.

Chapter Trust Factor uses a minimum of three distinct qualifying studies. Publication records retain their own evidence and assessment.
