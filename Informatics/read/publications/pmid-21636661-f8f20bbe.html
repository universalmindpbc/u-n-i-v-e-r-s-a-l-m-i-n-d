Evaluation of enzyme immunoassay and radioimmunoassay methods for the measurement of plasma oxytocin

Szeto A, McCabe PM, Nation DA, Tabak BA, Rossetti MA, McCullough ME, Schneiderman N, Mendez AJ. · Psychosomatic medicine · 2011 · Human plasma oxytocin immunoassay comparison

Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

Measurements differed substantially between enzyme immunoassay and radioimmunoassay and between extraction conditions. The paper demonstrates how analytical procedure changes the reported oxytocin concentration.

Recorded limitations

A plasma concentration is not direct receptor occupancy or central target engagement. Assay results require platform-specific validation rather than comparison across incompatible procedures. Access: Relevant full-text methods and results reviewed.
