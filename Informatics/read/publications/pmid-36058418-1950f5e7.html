Effects of intranasal oxytocin and positive couple interaction on immune factors in skin wounds

Ditzen B, Aguilar-Raab C, Winter F, Hernández C, Schneider E, Bodenmann G, Heinrichs M, Ehlert U, Läuchli S. · Brain, behavior, and immunity · 2023 · Randomised 2 × 2 intranasal oxytocin and couple-interaction study; 80 couples

Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

Wound-fluid cytokines depended on sex and interaction context. Among women, oxytocin combined with a positive couple interaction was associated with lower IL-1β and higher IL-6; TNF did not show the same assignment effect.

Recorded limitations

Local wound IL-6 can participate in repair and is not equivalent to systemic inflammatory burden. Many early IL-6 measurements were below detection. This is the same trial population later analysed for wound healing by Schneider and colleagues. Access: Relevant full-text methods and results reviewed.
