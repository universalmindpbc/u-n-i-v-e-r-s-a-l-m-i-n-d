Comparing Fathers for Change Individual Therapy for IPV Use to the Duluth Model Intervention in Individual and Group Formats.

Stover CS; Shayani DR; Ramos KL; Sullivan TP; Hayes A · Journal of interpersonal violence · 2025 · Randomized pilot trial

Original publication · Original publication · Original publication · Original publication · Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

Individual-treatment participants attended more sessions, reported stronger alliances and less IPV; only Fathers for Change improved emotion regulation pre/post.

Recorded limitations

Pilot sample and self-reported outcomes. The emotion-regulation finding is within-group and does not by itself prove superiority.
