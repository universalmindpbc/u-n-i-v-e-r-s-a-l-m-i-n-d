Berberine improves negative symptoms and cognitive function in patients with chronic schizophrenia via anti-inflammatory effect: a randomized clinical trial.

Pu Z, Wen H, Jiang H, Hou Q, Yan H. · Chinese medicine · 2023 · Randomized placebo-controlled trial

Original publication · Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

Berberine recipients showed improvements in negative symptoms and cognitive tests alongside lower IL-1β, IL-6 and TNF compared with placebo over three months.

Recorded limitations

Open-label trial with per-protocol analysis, attrition and multiple outcomes; cytokine associations do not establish mediation. Background antipsychotic treatment varied. Access: Abstract and selected full-text passages reviewed.
