Effects of celecoxib on inflammatory markers in bipolar patients undergoing electroconvulsive therapy: a placebo-controlled, double-blind, randomised study.

Kargar M, Yousefi A, Mojtahedzadeh M, Akhondzadeh S, Artounian V, Abdollahi A, Ahmadvand A, Ghaeli P. · Swiss medical weekly · 2014 · Randomized placebo-controlled trial

Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

Celecoxib during electroconvulsive therapy reduced TNF at the final assessment; IL-6, IL-1β and hs-CRP did not differ significantly between groups.

Recorded limitations

Short adjunctive biomarker trial during ECT. A TNF result is not an IL-6 result and does not isolate a mechanism for mood benefit. Access: Indexed abstract reviewed; full-text methods not comprehensively assessed.
