Serum Interleukin-6 Level in Children With Attention-Deficit Hyperactivity Disorder (ADHD).

Darwish AH, Elgohary TM, Nosair NA. · Journal of child neurology · 2019 · Case-control study

Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

ADHD children had higher serum IL-6 than controls, without a significant relationship to parent-rated severity or IQ.

Recorded limitations

Observational design and limited sample; examine cohort independence from later similar reports before treating it as replication. Access: Indexed abstract reviewed; full-text methods not comprehensively assessed.
