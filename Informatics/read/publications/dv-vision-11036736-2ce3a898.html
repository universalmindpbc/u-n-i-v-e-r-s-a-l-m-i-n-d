The relative effectiveness of EMDR versus relaxation training with battered women prisoners.

Sharman D. Colosetti; Bruce A. Thyer · Behavior Modification · 2000 · Repeated-measures case series

Original publication · Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

Four of five women showed no additional improvement after EMDR; one improved in anxiety and intrusive thoughts.

Recorded limitations

Five cases and fixed treatment order cannot establish comparative efficacy or general ineffectiveness.
