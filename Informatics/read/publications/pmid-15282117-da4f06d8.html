Serum cytokine concentrations in alcohol-dependent individuals without liver disease.

Nicolaou C, Chatzipanagiotou S, Tzivos D, Tzavellas EO, Boufidou F, Liappas IA. · Alcohol (Fayetteville, N.Y.) · 2004 · Case-control study

Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

Alcohol dependence without liver disease was associated with higher serum IL-6; several other cytokines were similar to controls.

Recorded limitations

Excluding liver disease addresses one confounder, but detoxification timing, exposure and other group differences remain. Sample size requires full text. Access: Indexed abstract reviewed; full-text methods not comprehensively assessed.
