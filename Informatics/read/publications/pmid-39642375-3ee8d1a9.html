Neurological Evidence of Diverse Self-Help Breathing Training With Virtual Reality and Biofeedback Assistance: Extensive Exploration Study of Electroencephalography Markers

Ng Hei-Yin Hydra, Wu Changwei W, Hsu Hao-Che, Huang Chih-Mao, Hsu Ai-Ling, Chao Yi-Ping, Jung Tzyy-Ping, Chuang Chun-Hsiang · JMIR Formative Research · 2024 · Participant study

Original publication

Recorded summary

Brain recordings from 51 participants varied across breathing conditions. Task-related EEG findings address attention and physiology during training. They provide no direct measure of lasting clinical improvement.

Recorded limitations

A respiration belt controlled the meadow. The investigators recorded EEG to compare the practice conditions.
