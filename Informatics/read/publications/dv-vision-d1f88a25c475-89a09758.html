The effectiveness of EMDR with adult female survivors of childhood sexual abuse

T. Edmond; A. Rubin; K. G. Wambach · Social Work Research · 1999 · Randomized controlled trial

Original publication · Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

EMDR outperformed delayed treatment on four measures post-treatment; at three months it outperformed routine therapy on two of four measures.

Recorded limitations

Small trial; access restricted to abstract; not an IPV-specific cohort.
