Effect of the GLP-1 receptor agonist exenatide on pro-inflammatory and metabolic biomarkers in individuals with alcohol use disorder: Post hoc results from a randomized, double-blinded, placebo-controlled clinical trial.

Hviid MEB, Christoffersen LAN, Klausen MK, Brodersen T, Pedersen OB, Ostrowski SR, Larsen MH, Kongstad M, Jensen ME, Vilsbøll T, Fink-Jensen A. · Alcohol, clinical & experimental research · 2025 · Post hoc biomarker analysis of randomized trial

Original publication · Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

AUD participants had higher IL-6 and hs-CRP than controls. After 26 weeks, exenatide did not significantly change the measured inflammatory or metabolic biomarkers relative to placebo.

Recorded limitations

Post hoc analysis, attrition and small non-AUD comparison group; a baseline association does not establish treatment responsiveness. Access: Indexed abstract reviewed; full-text methods not comprehensively assessed.
