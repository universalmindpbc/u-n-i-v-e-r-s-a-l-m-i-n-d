The Effect of Binaural Beat Technology on the Cardiovascular Stress Response in Military Service Members With Postdeployment Stress

Gantt MA, Dadds S, Burns DS, Glaser D, Moore AD. · Journal of nursing scholarship : an official publication of Sigma Theta Tau International Honor Society of Nursing · 2017 · Double-blind randomized controlled trial

Original publication · Original publication

Attributed extracts from the research record

“The use of binaural beat technology in the theta brainwave frequency embedded into music decreases physical and psychological indications of stress. Binaural beat technology embedded with beta and delta frequencies may improve cognitive functioning and sleep quality, respectively.”
