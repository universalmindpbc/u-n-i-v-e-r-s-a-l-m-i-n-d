Estimation of IL-6, INFγ, and Lipid Profile in Suicidal and Nonsuicidal Adults with Major Depressive Disorder.

Eidan AJ, Al-Harmoosh RA, Al-Amarei HM. · Journal of interferon & cytokine research : the official journal of the International Society for Interferon and Cytokine Research · 2019 · Case-control study

Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

IL-6 was higher in depressed groups than healthy controls, but did not independently distinguish suicide attempters from non-attempters after confounder adjustment.

Recorded limitations

Small observational study; distinguishes depression-associated inflammation from a suicide-specific signal. Access: Indexed abstract reviewed; full-text methods not comprehensively assessed.
