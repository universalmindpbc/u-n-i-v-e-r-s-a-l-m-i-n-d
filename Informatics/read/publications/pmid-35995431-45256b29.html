Inflammatory cytokines and alcohol use disorder: systematic review and meta-analysis.

Moura HF, Hansen F, Galland F, Silvelo D, Rebelatto FP, Ornell F, Massuda R, Scherer JN, Schuch F, Kessler FH, von Diemen L. · Revista brasileira de psiquiatria (Sao Paulo, Brazil : 1999) · 2022 · Systematic review and meta-analysis

Original publication · Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

AUD was associated with higher IL-6 (SMD 0.523; 95% CI 0.136–0.909), while the other thirteen assessed cytokines did not show significant pooled differences.

Recorded limitations

Active drinking, abstinence, liver and other illnesses, and small marker-specific evidence bases limit causal interpretation. Access: Indexed abstract reviewed; full-text methods not comprehensively assessed.
