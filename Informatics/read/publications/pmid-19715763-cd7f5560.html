Cerebral bases of subliminal speech priming

Kouider S, de Gardelle V, Dehaene S, Dupoux E, Pallier C. · NeuroImage · 2010 · Behavioural priming and fMRI; 16 participants

Original publication · Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

Masked, compressed spoken words influenced subsequent word processing. Repetition-related responses in temporal and insular regions occurred with prime identification near chance.

Recorded limitations

Short, tightly controlled primes; repetition suppression indexes altered processing, not a measured emotional or therapeutic response.
