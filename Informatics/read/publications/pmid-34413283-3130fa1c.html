Cortisol and inflammatory biomarker levels in youths with attention deficit hyperactivity disorder (ADHD): evidence from a systematic review with meta-analysis.

Chang JP, Su KP, Mondelli V, Pariante CM. · Translational psychiatry · 2021 · Systematic review and meta-analysis

Original publication · Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

The pooled inflammatory evidence did not show a significant IL-6 difference (p=0.09), despite a lower TNF signal. Most studies in the broader review concerned cortisol, not cytokines.

Recorded limitations

Very small cytokine evidence base; a nonsignificant result is not proof of equivalence. Cortisol study counts cannot be assigned to IL-6. Access: Indexed abstract reviewed; full-text methods not comprehensively assessed.
