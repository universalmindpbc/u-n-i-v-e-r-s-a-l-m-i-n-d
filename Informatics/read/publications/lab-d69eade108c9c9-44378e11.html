Virtual reality improves emotional but not cognitive empathy: A meta-analysis

Martingano Alison Jane, Hererra Fernanda, Konrath Sara · Technology, Mind, and Behavior · 2021 · Review or conceptual paper

Original publication

Recorded summary

Across 43 VR studies involving 5,644 participants, immersion supported emotional empathy. Cognitive perspective-taking and the added value of VR over simpler storytelling were less convincing.

Recorded limitations

Meta-analysis of VR and empathy.
