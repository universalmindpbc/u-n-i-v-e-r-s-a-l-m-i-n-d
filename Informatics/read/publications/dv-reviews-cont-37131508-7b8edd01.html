The promotion of well-being among children exposed to intimate partner violence: A systematic review of interventions.

Latzman NE; Casanueva C; Brinton J; Forman-Hoffman VL · Campbell systematic reviews · 2019 · Systematic review with limited meta-analysis

Original publication · Original publication · Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

Two intensive home-based parenting studies supported reduced child externalizing problems immediately after treatment and at eight months, but not four months. Most other outcomes could not be pooled and overall effectiveness remains uncertain.

Recorded limitations

Only eight eligible trials; heterogeneity prevented most meta-analyses. Restriction to low/moderate-risk studies was a protocol deviation. Most studies inferred child exposure from parental IPV reports; one study tracked adverse events.

Evidence and provenance

Chapter Trust Factor uses a minimum of three distinct qualifying studies. Publication records retain their own evidence and assessment.
