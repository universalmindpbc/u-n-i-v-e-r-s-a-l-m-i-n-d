Benefits of Yoga on IL-6: Findings from a Randomized Controlled Trial of Yoga for Depression.

Nugent NR, Brick L, Armey MF, Tyrka AR, Ridout KK, Uebelacker LA. · Behavioral medicine (Washington, D.C.) · 2021 · Biomarker analysis of randomized active-control trial

Original publication · Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

Hatha yoga reduced IL-6 relative to health education; TNF and CRP did not show the same treatment-related trajectory.

Recorded limitations

Biomarker subset of a larger depression trial; behavioural interventions have multiple pathways and cannot establish OXTR or IL-6 mediation. Access: Indexed abstract reviewed; full-text methods not comprehensively assessed.
