Facilitators to Engagement in a Mother-Child Therapeutic Intervention Following Intimate Partner Violence.

Fogarty A; Treyvaud K; Savopoulos P; Jones A; Cox A; Toone E; Giallo R · Journal of interpersonal violence · 2022 · Qualitative interview study

Original publication · Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

Mothers identified clear referrals, flexible delivery and direct clinician communication as engagement facilitators; mistrust, blame and ongoing father contact could obstruct engagement.

Recorded limitations

Interviews involved 16 mothers who completed BRISC, excluding noncompleters’ perspectives; findings concern acceptability and access, not efficacy.

Evidence and provenance

Chapter Trust Factor uses a minimum of three distinct qualifying studies. Publication records retain their own evidence and assessment.
