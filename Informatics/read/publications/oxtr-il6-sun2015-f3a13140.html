The Oxytocin Receptor as an Acute Phase Protein and its Signaling Pathways in Response to Inflammation

Ni Sun-Suslow · University of Miami thesis repository · 2015 · University of Miami master’s thesis; repository abstract

Original publication

Recorded summary

The abstract reports inflammatory induction of OXTR in THP-1 cells and investigates NF-κB-associated regulation. IL-6 alone did not reproduce the LPS-related receptor increase in the reported experiment.

Recorded limitations

Full text was restricted at review. This work comes from the research lineage of the later Szeto macrophage paper; it is not counted as an independent replication. The exact dataset overlap remains unresolved. Access: Repository abstract and bibliographic record reviewed.
