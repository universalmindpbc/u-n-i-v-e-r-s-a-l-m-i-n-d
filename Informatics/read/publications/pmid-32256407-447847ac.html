A Pilot Randomized Control Trial With the Probiotic Strain Lactobacillus rhamnosus GG (LGG) in ADHD: Children and Adolescents Report Better Health-Related Quality of Life.

Kumperscak HG, Gricar A, Ülen I, Micetic-Turk D. · Frontiers in psychiatry · 2020 · Randomized placebo-controlled pilot trial

Original publication · Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

Probiotic supplementation improved child-reported quality of life, while parent/teacher findings were less consistent. IL-6 decreased in both probiotic and placebo groups.

Recorded limitations

A change in both arms is not evidence of a probiotic-specific IL-6 effect. Small sample and multiple endpoints limit inference. Access: Abstract and selected full-text passages reviewed.
