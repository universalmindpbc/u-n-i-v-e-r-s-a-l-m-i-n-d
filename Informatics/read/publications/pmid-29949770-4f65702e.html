The Molecular Motor KIF21B Mediates Synaptic Plasticity and Fear Extinction by Terminating Rac1 Activation

Morikawa Momo, Tanaka Yosuke, Cho Hyun-Soo, Yoshihara Masaharu, Hirokawa Nobutaka · Cell Reports · 2018 · Preclinical mouse and cellular fear-extinction experiments

Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

The experiments connect KIF21B, Rac1/ELMO1/Dock signalling and fear extinction, supplying adjacent mechanistic context.

Recorded limitations

The work concerns a Dock signalling complex including Dock180/DOCK1. It is not a human DOCK2 methylation or expression validation study; inhibitor-related DOCK2 citations do not establish DOCK2-specific treatment efficacy. Access: Publisher abstract and publicly available author manuscript passages reviewed.
