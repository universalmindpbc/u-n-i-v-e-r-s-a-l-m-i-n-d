SAT-059 Randomized Controlled Trial Examining Effects of 8 weeks of Intranasal Oxytocin on the Circulating Inflammatory Proteome in Adults with Obesity

Boutin R and colleagues · Journal of the Endocrine Society · 2025 · Conference abstract: eight-week placebo-controlled trial in 61 adults with obesity

Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

The inflammatory proteome analysis reported no treatment effects that survived Benjamini–Hochberg correction. Some nominal findings pointed toward increased inflammatory proteins rather than a consistent reduction.

Recorded limitations

This is a conference abstract, with limited methodological detail, rather than a complete peer-reviewed clinical report. The corrected null result remains relevant and does not prove exact equivalence or absence of every possible effect. Access: Indexed abstract and bibliographic record reviewed.
