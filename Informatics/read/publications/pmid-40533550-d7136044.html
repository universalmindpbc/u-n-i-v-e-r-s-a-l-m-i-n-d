Recorded summary

The study identifies cell-specific PTSD-associated molecular changes, including neuronal and immune-related pathways. It illustrates why tissue and cell type matter when interpreting a blood-based candidate.

Recorded limitations

The main text does not establish DOCK2 as an intervention-response marker. This is broader mechanistic context, not a clinical trial or a DOCK2 replication. Access: Main article and DOCK2 relevance screened.
