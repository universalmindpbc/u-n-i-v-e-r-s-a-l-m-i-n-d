Effects of zinc supplementation on efficacy of antidepressant therapy, inflammatory cytokines, and brain-derived neurotrophic factor in patients with major depression.

Ranjbar E, Shams J, Sabetkasaei M, M-Shirazi M, Rashidkhani B, Mostafavi A, Bornak E, Nasrollahzadeh J. · Nutritional neuroscience · 2014 · Randomized placebo-controlled trial

Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

Adjunctive zinc improved depression ratings but did not significantly change IL-6, TNF or BDNF relative to placebo.

Recorded limitations

Small single-centre adjunctive study; clinical improvement occurred without a demonstrated IL-6 effect. Access: Indexed abstract reviewed; full-text methods not comprehensively assessed.
