Original publication · Original publication · Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

Most statistical tests were nonsignificant; descriptive gains among nine completers do not establish efficacy.

Recorded limitations

71% attrition, tiny sequence groups and no long-term follow-up; not independent replication of the 2024 report.
