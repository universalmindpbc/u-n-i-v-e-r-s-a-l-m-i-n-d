Oxytocin Modulates Microglial IL-17-Linked Inflammatory Pathways Through the IL-6/COX-2

Hwang W, Jang YH, Hong J, Kang S, Hur JK, Lee HJ. · Life (Basel, Switzerland) · 2026 · BV-2 mouse microglial RNA sequencing and gene-expression experiments

Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

Oxytocin pretreatment reduced IL6 and COX2 transcripts after LPS challenge. Transcriptomic enrichment implicated IL-17-linked inflammatory pathways; TNF and IBA1 transcript changes were not significant in the reported targeted comparison.

Recorded limitations

Pathway enrichment is not direct evidence of IL-17 blockade. This cell study does not establish reduced secreted IL-6, human brain exposure or clinical benefit; receptor-specific causality was not established by a knockout or antagonist experiment.
