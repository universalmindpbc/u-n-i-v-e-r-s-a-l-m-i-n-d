Subliminal speech priming

Kouider S, Dupoux E. · Psychological Science · 2005 · Controlled experiments with compressed and masked speech

Original publication

Recorded summary

Masked speech produced repetition priming, including effects across different voices. Evidence for semantic overlap was more limited.

Recorded limitations

Word identity, phonological processing and sentence meaning are distinct levels of processing.
