The Effectiveness of Culturally Specific Male Domestic Violence Offender Intervention Programs on Behavior Changes and Mental Health: A Systematic Review.

Satyen L; Hansen A; Green JL; Zark L · International journal of environmental research and public health · 2022 · Mixed-methods systematic review

Original publication · Original publication · Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

Most publications described improvement, but predominantly uncontrolled designs cannot establish that cultural adaptation caused benefit. One controlled study found higher violent recidivism in treatment and no IPV recidivism advantage.

Recorded limitations

Small and culturally restricted evidence base, inconsistent outcomes and heavy reliance on perpetrator reports. The review text also contains a cross-reference mismatch when attributing one study’s success rate; no exact success percentage is carried forward.

Evidence and provenance

Chapter Trust Factor uses a minimum of three distinct qualifying studies. Publication records retain their own evidence and assessment.
