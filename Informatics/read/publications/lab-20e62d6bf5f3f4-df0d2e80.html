Integrating Cognitive Behavioral Therapy and Heart Rate Variability Biofeedback in Virtual Reality, Augmented Reality, and Mixed Reality as a Mental Health Intervention

Original publication

Recorded summary

A workshop demonstration combines CBT and simulated HRV feedback in VR, AR and mixed reality. Controlled outcomes were proposed for evaluation.

Recorded limitations

The workshop abstract describes simulated HRV feedback and a planned comparison. Live sensing in participants is unverified.
