Evocation of positive memories as complement to trauma-focused cognitive-behavioural therapy for intimate partner violence against women.

Crespo M; Hernández-Lloreda MJ; Hornillos C; Miguel-Alvaro A; Sánchez-Ferrer S; Antón AA · European journal of psychotraumatology · 2024 · Therapy-group randomized active comparison with nonrandomized waitlist

Original publication · Original publication · Original publication · Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

Both active treatments improved several outcomes; direct comparisons did not establish superiority of adding positive memories.

Recorded limitations

Waitlist assignment was by availability; only active therapy groups randomized. High dropout and per-protocol emphasis limit inference; clustering adjustment not established.

Evidence and provenance

Chapter Trust Factor uses a minimum of three distinct qualifying studies. Publication records retain their own evidence and assessment.
