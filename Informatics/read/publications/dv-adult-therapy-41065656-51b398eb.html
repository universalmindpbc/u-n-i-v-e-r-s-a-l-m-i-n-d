Long-Term Effectiveness of Trauma-Focused Therapy for Intimate Partner Violence Against Women: Effects on Posttraumatic Symptoms and Revictimization.

Crespo M; Hernández-Lloreda MJ; Hornillos C · Journal of interpersonal violence · 2025 · Randomized controlled trial

Original publication · Original publication · Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

Symptoms and reported violence decreased over follow-up in both groups, with no significant group or time-by-group effects.

Recorded limitations

45.05% dropout and no untreated long-term comparator; within-group reductions in revictimization do not establish a comparative preventive effect.
