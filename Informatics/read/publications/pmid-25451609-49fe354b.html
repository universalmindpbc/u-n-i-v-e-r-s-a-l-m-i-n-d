Elevated levels of IL-6 and IL-18 in manic and hypomanic states in rapid cycling bipolar disorder patients.

Munkholm K, Weikop P, Kessing LV, Vinberg M. · Brain, behavior, and immunity · 2015 · Repeated-measures longitudinal study

Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

IL-6 and IL-18 were higher during pooled manic/hypomanic states than during depressed or euthymic states after covariate adjustment.

Recorded limitations

Mania and hypomania were pooled, so this is not isolated proof for hypomania. Several other cytokines had detection or assay-variability problems. Access: Indexed abstract reviewed; full-text methods not comprehensively assessed.
