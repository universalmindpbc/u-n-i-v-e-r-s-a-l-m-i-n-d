Individualized motivational plans in batterer intervention programs: A randomized clinical trial.

Lila M; Gracia E; Catalá-Miñana A · Journal of consulting and clinical psychology · 2018 · Randomized controlled trial

Original publication · Original publication · Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

Adding the motivational plan improved attendance dose and readiness to change and reduced self-reported physical violence.

Recorded limitations

Self-report and therapist risk ratings are not equivalent to verified recidivism. Masking and allocation concealment are not established from abstract.
