Pro- and anti-inflammatory cytokines Interleukin-6 and Interleukin-10 predict therapy outcome of female patients with posttraumatic stress disorder.

Renner V, Joraschky P, Kirschbaum C, Schellong J, Petrowski K. · Translational psychiatry · 2022 · Prospective stress-challenge treatment cohort

Original publication · Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

IL-6 and IL-10 stress-response measures contributed to models of later general distress and depressive symptoms after inpatient psychotherapy.

Recorded limitations

Very small sample with several predictors risks overfitting. Cohort overlap with related Dresden work is unresolved, and neither marker is a validated clinical predictor. Access: Indexed abstract reviewed; full-text methods not comprehensively assessed.
