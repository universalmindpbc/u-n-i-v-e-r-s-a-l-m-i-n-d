Association of inflammation with depression and anxiety: evidence for symptom-specificity and potential causality from UK Biobank and NESDA cohorts.

Milaneschi Y, Kappelmann N, Ye Z, Lamers F, Moser S, Jones PB, Burgess S, Penninx BWJH, Khandaker GM. · Molecular psychiatry · 2021 · Cohort and Mendelian-randomization analysis

Original publication · Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

Inflammation related particularly to selected depressive and neurovegetative symptoms. IL-6 genetic analyses supported fatigue and sleep associations; anxiety findings were less consistent.

Recorded limitations

Overlapping cohorts with other genetic reports; symptom specificity, metabolic confounding and instrument assumptions matter. No validated treatment-selection rule. Linked correction notice identified; its full content remains unresolved in this appraisal. Access: Indexed abstract reviewed; full-text methods not comprehensively assessed.
