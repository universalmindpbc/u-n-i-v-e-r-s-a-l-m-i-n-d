DNA methylation from germline cells in veterans with PTSD

Mehta Divya, Pelzer Elise S., Bruenig Dagmar, Lawford Bruce, McLeay Sarah, Morris Charles P., Gibson John N., Young Ross McD., Voisey Joanne, McLeay Sarah, Harvey Wendy, Romaniuk Madeline, Crawford Darrell, Colquhoun David, Young Ross McD., Dwyer Miriam, Gibson John, O'Sullivan Robyn, Cooksley Graham, Strakosch Christopher, Thomson Rachel, Voisey Joanne, Lawford Bruce · Journal of Psychiatric Research · 2019 · Exploratory sperm and blood methylation; 38 sperm samples

Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

DOCK2 appeared among nominally associated loci shared between sperm and blood. This extends the tissue question raised by the earlier veteran study.

Recorded limitations

The veteran cohort overlaps earlier work. DOCK2 was not one of the genome-wide significant sperm findings; cross-tissue overlap was not significant enrichment. No offspring molecular data established transmission of DOCK2 methylation or trauma. Access: Relevant full-text methods and results reviewed.
