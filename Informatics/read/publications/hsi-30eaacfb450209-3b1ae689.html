Assessment of hyperspectral imaging and CycleGAN-simulated narrowband techniques to detect early esophageal cancer.

Yang KY, Mukundan A, Tsao YM, Shi XH, Huang CW, Wang HC. · Scientific reports · 2023 · Journal publication

Original publication · Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

The study compares white-light, narrowband and reconstructed endoscopic images in an esophageal-cancer image dataset using colour-difference, entropy and structural-similarity measures.

Recorded limitations

Image-quality comparisons do not by themselves establish prospective patient-level diagnostic accuracy. The setting is endoscopy, with distinct acquisition and validation requirements.
