Cytokine alterations in bipolar disorder: a meta-analysis of 30 studies.

Modabbernia A, Taslimi S, Brietzke E, Ashrafi M. · Biological psychiatry · 2013 · Meta-analysis

Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

Several inflammatory and regulatory markers, including soluble IL-6 receptor, were elevated. The abstract gives IL-6 p=0.073 while also placing it in an elevated-marker list.

Recorded limitations

Internal wording inconsistency: IL-6 is treated here as a nonsignificant trend at the conventional threshold, not a confirmed positive pooled result. Receptor and ligand results remain separate. Access: Indexed abstract reviewed; full-text methods not comprehensively assessed.
