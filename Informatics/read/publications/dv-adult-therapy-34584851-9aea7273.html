Individually tailored Internet-delivered cognitive-behavioral therapy for survivors of intimate partner violence: A randomized controlled pilot trial.

Andersson G; Olsson E; Ringsgård E; Sandgren T; Viklund I; Andersson C; Hesselman Y; Johansson R; Nordgren LB; Bohman B · Internet interventions · 2021 · Randomized controlled trial

Original publication · Original publication · Original publication · Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

Some PTSD/depression measures favored guided internet CBT, while others did not. Follow-up improvements after both arms received treatment are within-group findings.

Recorded limitations

Small feasibility study, waitlist comparison, moderate adherence and substantial long-term attrition. Ongoing IPV excluded; later follow-up lacked an untreated comparator.

Evidence and provenance

Chapter Trust Factor uses a minimum of three distinct qualifying studies. Publication records retain their own evidence and assessment.
