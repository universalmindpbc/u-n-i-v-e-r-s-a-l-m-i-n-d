Inflammatory markers in post-traumatic stress disorder: a systematic review, meta-analysis, and meta-regression.

Passos IC, Vasconcelos-Moreno MP, Costa LG, Kunz M, Brietzke E, Quevedo J, Salum G, Magalhães PV, Kapczinski F, Kauer-Sant'Anna M. · The lancet. Psychiatry · 2015 · Systematic review and meta-analysis

Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

PTSD was associated with higher peripheral IL-6, including analyses excluding comorbid depression. Symptom severity related to IL-6 in meta-regression.

Recorded limitations

High heterogeneity; medication, depression, assay and sampling time explained substantial variation. Healthy-control contrasts do not isolate effects of trauma exposure. Access: Indexed abstract reviewed; full-text methods not comprehensively assessed.
