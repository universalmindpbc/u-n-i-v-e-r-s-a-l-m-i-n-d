Community treatment of posttraumatic stress disorder for children exposed to intimate partner violence: a randomized controlled trial.

Cohen JA; Mannarino AP; Iyengar S · Archives of pediatrics & adolescent medicine · 2011 · Randomized controlled trial with blinded outcome evaluators

Original publication · Original publication · Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

TF-CBT improved PTSD and anxiety more than child-centered therapy; benefits did not extend clearly to depression or general behavior problems.

Recorded limitations

Only 75 of 124 completed treatment; missing outcomes required imputation. Siblings shared allocation. No long-term outcome is established here.
