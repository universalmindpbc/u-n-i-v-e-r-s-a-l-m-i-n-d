Hypothalamic Neuropeptide Brain Protection: Focus on Oxytocin

Panaro MA, Benameur T, Porro C. · Journal of clinical medicine · 2020 · Review of hypothalamic oxytocin and brain-protection mechanisms

Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

Surveys proposed neuroprotective pathways, including interactions with microglia, oxidative stress and inflammatory signalling.

Recorded limitations

The reviewed mechanisms do not establish that peripheral oxytocin concentrations measure brain exposure or that clinical neuroprotection has been demonstrated. Access: Indexed abstract and bibliographic record reviewed.
