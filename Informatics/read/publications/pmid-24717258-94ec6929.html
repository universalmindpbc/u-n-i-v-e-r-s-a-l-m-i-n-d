The effects of add-on low-dose memantine on cytokine levels in bipolar II depression: a 12-week double-blind, randomized controlled trial.

Lee SY, Chen SL, Chang YH, Chen PS, Huang SY, Tzeng NS, Wang YS, Wang LJ, Lee IH, Wang TY, Yeh TL, Yang YK, Hong JS, Lu RB. · Journal of clinical psychopharmacology · 2014 · Randomized placebo-controlled trial

Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

Adjunctive memantine reduced TNF but did not improve symptoms or IL-6 relative to placebo during valproate treatment for bipolar II depression. Changes in depression ratings correlated with IL-6 change.

Recorded limitations

A within-person association despite a null between-group effect cannot establish IL-6-mediated efficacy. Bipolar II depression is distinct from hypomania. Access: Indexed abstract reviewed; full-text methods not comprehensively assessed.
