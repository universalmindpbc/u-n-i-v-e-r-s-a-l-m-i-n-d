Randomized clinical trial examining the incremental efficacy of a 90-minute motivational alcohol intervention as an adjunct to standard batterer intervention for men.

Stuart GL; Shorey RC; Moore TM; Ramsey SE; Kahler CW; O'Farrell TJ; Strong DR; Temple JR; Monti PM · Addiction (Abingdon, England) · 2013 · Randomized controlled trial

Original publication · Original publication · Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

The primary physical-IPV outcome did not differ. Early alcohol and some secondary violence benefits faded by 12 months.

Recorded limitations

Secondary short-term findings should not replace the null primary physical-IPV result. Follow-up fell from 95% at three months to 82% at 12 months.
