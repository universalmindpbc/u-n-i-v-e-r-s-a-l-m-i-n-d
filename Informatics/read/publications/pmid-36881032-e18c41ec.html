Effect of Induction Therapy With Olamkicept vs Placebo on Clinical Response in Patients With Active Ulcerative Colitis: A Randomized Clinical Trial

Zhang S, Chen B, Wang B, Chen H, Li Y, Cao Q, Zhong J, Shieh MJ, Ran Z, Tang T, Yang M, Xu B, Wang Q, Liu Y, Ma L, Wang X, Zhang N, Zhang S, Guo W, Huang L, Schreiber S, Chen M. · JAMA · 2023 · Phase 2 randomised trial of olamkicept in 91 adults with active ulcerative colitis

Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

The higher olamkicept dose improved clinical response relative to placebo, supporting the clinical relevance of targeting IL-6 trans-signalling in this disease context. The lower-dose comparison was not significant.

Recorded limitations

Olamkicept is a soluble-gp130-based agent, not oxytocin. Its efficacy cannot be transferred to OXTR interventions or interpreted as proof of an OXTR–IL-6 mechanism. Access: Relevant full-text methods and results reviewed.
