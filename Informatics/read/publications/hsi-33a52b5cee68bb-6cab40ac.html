"Medicine — Guiding machine vision with hyperspectral images." hitspectra.com/en/application.php?CNo=1 — Commercial HSI integration with standard medical lenses: dermatoscope (85% accuracy for skin pigment and haemoglobin abnormalities), ophthalmoscope (fundus layer analysis for retinal disease, glaucoma, optic nerve pathology), odontoscope (oral cancer staging, accuracy expected to reach 99.9%); SAVE HSI engine expanding 400+ analysable spectral channels from a single pixel of standard camera input; AI-assisted pathological image analysis; non-invasive disease identification without biopsy.
