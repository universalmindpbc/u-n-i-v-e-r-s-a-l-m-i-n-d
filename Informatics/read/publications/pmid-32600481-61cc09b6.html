Interleukin-6 and total antioxidant capacity levels following N-acetylcysteine and a combination nutraceutical intervention in a randomised controlled trial for bipolar disorder.

Bortolasci CC, Voigt C, Turner A, Mohebbi M, Gray L, Dodd S, Walder K, Berk M, Cotton SM, Malhi GS, Ng CH, Dowling N, Sarris J, Dean OM. · Acta neuropsychiatrica · 2020 · Secondary biomarker analysis of randomized trial

Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

N-acetylcysteine and a mitochondrial nutraceutical combination did not change serum IL-6 at 16 weeks. Lower baseline IL-6 moderated response to the combination on selected outcomes.

Recorded limitations

Secondary moderator analyses are exploratory; biomarker sample size and multiplicity require full trial context. Not evidence that lowering IL-6 caused recovery. Access: Indexed abstract reviewed; full-text methods not comprehensively assessed.
