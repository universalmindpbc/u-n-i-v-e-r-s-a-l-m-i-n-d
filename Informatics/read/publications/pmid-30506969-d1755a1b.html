Oxytocin receptor agonist reduces perinatal brain damage by targeting microglia

Mairesse J, Zinni M, Pansiot J, Hassan-Abdi R, Demene C, Colella M, Charriaut-Marlangue C, Rideau Batista Novais A, Tanter M, Maccari S, Gressens P, Vaiman D, Soussi-Yanicostas N, Baud O. · Glia · 2019 · Perinatal brain-injury models in rats, zebrafish and microglia

Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

The OXTR agonist carbetocin reduced microglial inflammatory responses and improved aspects of myelination, brain connectivity and later behaviour in experimental perinatal injury.

Recorded limitations

Carbetocin is a related pharmacological agonist, with its own exposure and receptor profile. Developmental animal injury models do not establish efficacy or safety for human infants or equivalence to a behavioural intervention. Access: Indexed abstract and bibliographic record reviewed.
