Inducing unconscious stress: Cardiovascular activity in response to subliminal presentation of threatening and neutral words

van der Ploeg MM, Brosschot JF, Verkuil B, Gillie BL, Williams DP, Koenig J, Vasey MW, Thayer JF. · Psychophysiology · 2017 · Randomized subliminal visual-word experiment; 116 participants

Original publication

Recorded summary

Threatening versus neutral masked words increased total peripheral resistance significantly. Mean arterial pressure and HRV followed the predicted directions without statistically significant differences.

Recorded limitations

Visual presentation; the cardiovascular endpoints differed in statistical support. Transfer to auditory masking remains a research question.
