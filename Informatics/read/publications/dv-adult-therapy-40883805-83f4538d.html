Original publication · Original publication · Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

Self-esteem, marital adjustment/satisfaction and pregnancy-related anxiety favored training. Violence reduction was not established by the reported outcomes.

Recorded limitations

Self-reported relationship and psychological outcomes should not be interpreted as proof of reduced violence or safer relationships.
