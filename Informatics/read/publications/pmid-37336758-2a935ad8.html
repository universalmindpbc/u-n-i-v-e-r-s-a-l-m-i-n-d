Neurophysiological Evidence for Semantic Processing of Irrelevant Speech and Own-Name Detection in a Virtual Café

Brown A, Pinto D, Burgart K, Zvilichovsky Y, Zion-Golumbic E. · Journal of Neuroscience · 2023 · EEG and skin conductance; 50 recruited participants

Original publication

Recorded summary

Unattended café speech elicited responses related to semantic violations and personally significant own-name information.

Recorded limitations

Irrelevant speech was audible; own-name effects do not establish an effect for every familiar phrase.
