Peripheral proinflammatory markers are upregulated in abstinent alcohol-dependent patients but are not affected by cognitive bias modification: Preliminary findings.

Portelli J, Wiers CE, Li X, Deschaine SL, McDiarmid GR, Bermpohl F, Leggio L. · Drug and alcohol dependence · 2019 · Randomized sham-training trial

Original publication · Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

CBM training did not alter the analysed inflammatory markers. IL-6 and IL-1β were below the assay detection limit and excluded from final analyses.

Recorded limitations

This is an assay-limited IL-6 result, not evidence of no biological IL-6 or a treatment-induced reduction. Small male-only sample. Access: Indexed abstract reviewed; full-text methods not comprehensively assessed.
