Biomarker Response to Mindfulness Intervention in Veterans Diagnosed with Post-traumatic Stress Disorder.

Shapira I, Richman J, Pace TWW, Lim KO, Polusny MA, Hamner MB, Bremner JD, Mumba MN, Jacobs ML, Pilkinton P, Davis LL. · Mindfulness · 2022 · Pooled secondary analysis of two randomized trials

Original publication · Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

Mindfulness and active-control psychotherapy did not differ in IL-6 or CRP change. Improved PTSD symptoms were associated with increased, rather than decreased, IL-6 and CRP in exploratory analyses.

Recorded limitations

Post hoc pooling and correlations do not establish mechanism. Clinical improvement and inflammatory change can diverge. Access: Indexed abstract reviewed; full-text methods not comprehensively assessed.
