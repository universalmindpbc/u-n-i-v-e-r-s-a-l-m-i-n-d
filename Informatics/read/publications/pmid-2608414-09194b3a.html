Psychophysiological responses to subliminal auditory suggestions for activation

Borgeat F, Boissonneault J, Chaloult L, Elie R. · Perceptual and Motor Skills · 1989 · Small psychophysiological experiment; 20 participants

Original publication

Recorded summary

Masked activating suggestions were associated with a heart-rate response during a stress task; skin conductance did not show the same effect.

Recorded limitations

Small, older study; heart rate is not heart-rate variability and the measures are not interchangeable.
