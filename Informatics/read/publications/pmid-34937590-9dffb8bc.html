Exploring interleukin-6, lipopolysaccharide-binding protein and brain-derived neurotrophic factor following 12 weeks of adjunctive minocycline treatment for depression.

Hasebe K, Mohebbi M, Gray L, Walker AJ, Bortolasci CC, Turner A, Berk M, Walder K, Maes M, Kanchanatawan B, Ashton MM, Berk L, Ng CH, Malhi GS, Singh AB, Dean OM. · Acta neuropsychiatrica · 2022 · Secondary biomarker analysis of randomized trial

Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

Adjunctive minocycline did not change IL-6, LBP or BDNF relative to placebo. Baseline IL-6 and selected clinical outcomes showed exploratory associations.

Recorded limitations

Multiple moderator and mediation analyses with modest sample size; no overall IL-6 treatment effect. Access: Indexed abstract reviewed; full-text methods not comprehensively assessed.
