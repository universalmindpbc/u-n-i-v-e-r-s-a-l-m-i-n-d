Take a Breath, Break The Silence: The Effects of Yogic Breathing and Testimony About Battering on Feelings of Self-Efficacy in Battered Women

Susan H. Franzblau; Michelle Smith; Sonia Echevarria; Thomas E. Van Cantfort · International Journal of Yoga Therapy · 2006 · Four-arm randomized experiment

Original publication · Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

Very short exploratory trial supports only cautious consideration of combined testimony/breathing; durable benefit and breathing-specific efficacy remain unproven.

Recorded limitations

Four-day endpoint; unclear allocation concealment/masking; one missing baseline; multiple tests and inconsistent reporting.
