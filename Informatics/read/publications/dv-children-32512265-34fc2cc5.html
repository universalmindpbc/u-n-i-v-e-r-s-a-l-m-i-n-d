Trauma-informed art and play therapy: Pilot study outcomes for children and mothers in domestic violence shelters in the United States and South Africa.

Woollett N; Bandeira M; Hatcher A · Child abuse & neglect · 2020 · Uncontrolled mixed-methods pilot

Original publication · Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

Child depressive symptoms decreased; the change in PTSD symptoms was not statistically significant. Interviews suggested improved emotional expression.

Recorded limitations

Small uncontrolled pilot;21 child self-reports and 16 mother reports. Country comparison cannot establish equivalent efficacy.
