Attention Regulation Framework

Niksirat Kavous Salehzadeh, Silpasuwanchai Chaklam, Cheng Peng, Ren Xiangshi · ACM Transactions on Computer-Human Interaction · 2019 · Participant study

Original publication

Recorded summary

Four user evaluations explored interfaces that use subtle movements to guide attention. Relevant to design in busy spaces and to giving participants control over the pace.

Recorded limitations

The evaluated interfaces tracked deliberate physical movements to support attention.
