Current Research on the Relationships Between Oxytocin and the Immune System: An Updated Study

Naeem MY, Alp Arici EC, Abbas S, Selamoglu Z · Archives of Razi Institute · 2026 · Narrative review; source appraisal

Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

A broad review from the supplied search collection maps proposed relationships between oxytocin and immunity. It is retained as a reading lead and provenance record.

Recorded limitations

Section 3.2 incorrectly describes OXTR as encoding oxytocin. OXT encodes the hormone precursor; OXTR encodes its receptor. This chapter uses the official gene records for that distinction and does not rely on this review for gene identity. Access: Publisher text reviewed; DOI not retrieved in Europe PMC.
