Plasticity and language in the anaesthetized human hippocampus

Katlowitz KA, Cole ER, Mickiewicz EA, Shah S, Franch M, Adkinson JA, Belanger JL, Mathura RK, Meszéna D, McGinley M, Muñoz W, Banks GP, Cash SS, Hsu CW, Paulk AC, Provenza NR, Watrous AJ, Williams Z, Goldman AM, Krishnan V, Maheshwari A, Heilbronner SR, Kim R, Rungratsameetaweemana N, Hayden BY, Sheth SA. · Nature · 2026 · Human hippocampal single-unit recordings during anaesthesia

Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

Hippocampal recordings during propofol anaesthesia showed responses to language features and learning-related patterns. Natural-speech analyses included four participants and 375 units.

Recorded limitations

Small neurosurgical sample; intact speech under anaesthesia is distinct from speech masked beneath music.
