A Randomized, Double-Blind, Placebo-Controlled Clinical Trial of Tocilizumab, An Interleukin-6 Receptor Antibody, For Residual Symptoms in Schizophrenia.

Girgis RR, Ciarleglio A, Choo T, Haynes G, Bathon JM, Cremers S, Kantrowitz JT, Lieberman JA, Brown AS. · Neuropsychopharmacology : official publication of the American College of Neuropsychopharmacology · 2018 · Randomized placebo-controlled trial

Original publication · Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

Three monthly tocilizumab infusions did not improve PANSS or cognitive outcomes. CRP fell while circulating IL-6 increased, illustrating that receptor blockade and measured ligand concentration are different endpoints.

Recorded limitations

Small trial in stable, chronically symptomatic schizophrenia; does not settle effects in early or inflammation-selected psychosis. No clinical efficacy signal. Access: Indexed abstract reviewed; full-text methods not comprehensively assessed.
