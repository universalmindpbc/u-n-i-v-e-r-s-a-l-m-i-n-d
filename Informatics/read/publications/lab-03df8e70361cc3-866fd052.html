Resident Thrum: Designing for Group Cardiac Synchronization through Multimodal Biofeedback

Perry Phoenix · Proceedings of the 2026 Conference on Creativity and Cognition · 2026 · Design or demonstration

Original publication

Recorded summary

Resident Thrum translates heart rhythms into light, sound and vibration for groups of two to six. It is an installation and design precedent.

Recorded limitations

The installation returned heartbeats as sound, vibration and light, and changed the environment with group cardiac synchrony.
