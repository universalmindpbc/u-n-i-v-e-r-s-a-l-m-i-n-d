Oxytocin does not attenuate the ex vivo production of inflammatory cytokines by lipopolysaccharide-activated monocytes and macrophages from healthy male and female donors

Ross KM, McDonald-Jones G, Miller GE. · Neuroimmunomodulation · 2013 · Ex vivo human donor monocyte and macrophage experiments

Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

Oxytocin did not attenuate LPS-induced inflammatory cytokine production across the tested conditions. The investigators examined donor sex, cell preparation, doses and pre-incubation as potential explanations for differences from earlier work.

Recorded limitations

This is a meaningful negative result in human-derived cells. Cell source, differentiation, receptor availability and challenge conditions may change the response; a universal direct macrophage effect is not established. Access: Relevant full-text methods and results reviewed.
