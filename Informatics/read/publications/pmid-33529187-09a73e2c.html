Development of a modular stress management platform (Performance Edge VR) and a pilot efficacy trial of a bio-feedback enhanced training module for controlled breathing

Kluge Murielle G., Maltby Steven, Walker Nicole, Bennett Neanne, Aidman Eugene, Nalivaiko Eugene, Walker Frederick Rohan · PLOS ONE · 2021 · Participant study

Original publication

Recorded summary

Thirty healthy volunteers recruited at the University of Newcastle practised across three sessions. Controlled breathing improved. The ADF co-developed this platform. The later on-base study provides direct evidence from serving trainees.

Recorded limitations

A respiratory belt provided controlled-breathing feedback.
