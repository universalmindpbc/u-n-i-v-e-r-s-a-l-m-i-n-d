Cognitive behavioral treatment of PTSD in residents of battered women's shelters: results of a randomized clinical trial.

Johnson DM; Zlotnick C; Perez S · Journal of consulting and clinical psychology · 2011 · Randomized controlled trial

Original publication · Original publication · Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

The trial found lower reabuse risk and improvement in selected PTSD symptom clusters. It did not establish a clear overall PTSD-severity benefit.

Recorded limitations

Small Phase I trial; interviewers not assured masked and depression imbalance required adjustment. Most women left shelter before completing therapy.
