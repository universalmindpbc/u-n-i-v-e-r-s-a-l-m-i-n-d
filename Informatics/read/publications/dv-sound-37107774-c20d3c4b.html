Evaluation of Integrative Community Therapy with Domestic Violence Survivors in Quito, Ecuador.

Chiara Sabina; Diego Perez-Figueroa; Laurent Reyes; Eduardo Campaña Medina; Eluzinete Pereira de Souza; Lisa Markovits; Andrea Carolina Oña Jacho; Gissel Katherine Rojas Bohorquez · International journal of environmental research and public health · 2023 · Self-selected pre/post comparison-group study

Original publication · Original publication · Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

Health, social support and self-esteem improved more in intervention; resilience and dating-violence-attitude interactions were nonsignificant.

Recorded limitations

Self-selection, baseline imbalances, attrition and unequal incentives limit causal inference.
