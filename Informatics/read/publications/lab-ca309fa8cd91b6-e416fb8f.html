Realidade virtual na prática de mindfulness em psicoterapia: uma revisão narrativa

Bengel de Paula Dirce Maria, Fernández Moretti Luciana · Psicologia em Estudo · 2021 · Review or conceptual paper

Original publication

Recorded summary

The authors examined 23 reports. Presence, usability and access featured in the designs. They called for controlled studies to isolate which features have therapeutic value.

Recorded limitations
