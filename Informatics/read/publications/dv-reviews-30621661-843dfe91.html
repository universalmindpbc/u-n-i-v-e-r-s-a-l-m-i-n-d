Cognitive behavioural group therapy for male perpetrators of intimate partner violence: a systematic review.

Nesset MB; Lara-Cabrera ML; Dalsbø TK; Pedersen SA; Bjørngaard JH; Palmstierna T · BMC psychiatry · 2019 · Systematic review

Original publication · Original publication · Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

Several RCTs reported improvement, but the review judged evidence insufficient to confirm benefit; the nonrandomized studies found no detectable effect.

Recorded limitations

Few low-quality studies; self-report and partner-report findings differed. A narrative synthesis does not establish a pooled effect.

Evidence and provenance

Chapter Trust Factor uses a minimum of three distinct qualifying studies. Publication records retain their own evidence and assessment.
