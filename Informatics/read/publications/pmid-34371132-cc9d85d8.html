Attention-deficit/hyperactivity disorder has a state-dependent association with asthma: The role of systemic inflammation in a population-based birth cohort followed from childhood to adulthood.

Leffa DT, Caye A, Santos I, Matijasevich A, Menezes A, Wehrmeister FC, Oliveira I, Vitola E, Bau CHD, Grevet EH, Tovo-Rodrigues L, Rohde LA. · Brain, behavior, and immunity · 2021 · Longitudinal birth-cohort study

Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

ADHD and asthma showed linked developmental patterns. Participants with both conditions had higher IL-6 than those with neither at ages 18 and 22.

Recorded limitations

Asthma and its treatment can influence inflammation; the comorbidity comparison does not isolate an ADHD-specific IL-6 effect. Access: Indexed abstract reviewed; full-text methods not comprehensively assessed.
