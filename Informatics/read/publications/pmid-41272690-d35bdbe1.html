Pro-inflammatory cytokines level in depressed patients with suicidal behaviour: a systematic review and meta-analysis.

Moshfeghinia R, Ghahramani M, Naderian R, Mostafavi S, Arman A, Mohagheghzadeh H, Haghighifard E, Tahmasebi S, Kanannejad Z, Javanbakht A, Ahmadi J. · Annals of general psychiatry · 2025 · Systematic review and meta-analysis

Original publication · Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

IL-6 was higher in depressed people with suicidal behaviour than healthy controls, but did not significantly distinguish depressed people with versus without suicidal behaviour (SMD 0.42; 95% CI −0.58 to 1.42).

Recorded limitations

Extreme heterogeneity, approximately 98.5% in the psychiatric comparison. The contrast with healthy controls cannot establish suicide-specific inflammation. Access: Indexed abstract reviewed; full-text methods not comprehensively assessed.
