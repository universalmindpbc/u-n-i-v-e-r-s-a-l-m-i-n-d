Efficacy of therapeutic suggestions under general anesthesia: a systematic review and meta-analysis of randomized controlled trials

Rosendahl J, Koranyi S, Jacob D, Zech N, Hansen E. · BMC Anesthesiology · 2016 · Systematic review and meta-analysis; 32 randomized trials, 2,102 patients

Original publication

Recorded summary

Pooled results found small effects for medication use and recovery, with little or no overall effect for pain and distress.

Recorded limitations

Trials varied in interventions and methods. A review of trials is not itself a double-blind trial.
