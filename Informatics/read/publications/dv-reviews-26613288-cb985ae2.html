A Randomized Controlled Clinical Trial of the Strength at Home Men's Program for Partner Violence in Military Veterans.

Taft CT; Macdonald A; Creech SK; Monson CM; Murphy CM · The Journal of clinical psychiatry · 2016 · Randomized controlled trial

Original publication · Original publication · Original publication · Original publication · Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

Modeled physical and psychological IPV trajectories favored SAH-M. Immediate post-treatment benefit was clearer; the later physical-recidivism comparison was not statistically significant.

Recorded limitations

Selected male military/veteran population; incomplete follow-up, limited collateral coverage, and referral comparator. The later physical-violence comparison was inconclusive.

Chapter Trust Factor uses a minimum of three distinct qualifying studies. Publication records retain their own evidence and assessment.
