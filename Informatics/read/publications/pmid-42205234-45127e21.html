Brief Virtual Reality and Mixed Reality Mindfulness Breathing Exercise for Emotional Well-Being and Cognitive Functions in University Students: Within-Subjects Experimental Design Study

Eun Zoey K Y, Koh Charmaine Jiali, Yang Hwajin, Goh Adalia Y H, Hu Meilan, Kasturiratna K T A Sandeeshwara, Hartanto Andree · JMIR XR and Spatial Computing · 2026 · Context or measurement study

Original publication

Recorded summary

In 102 Singapore undergraduates (mean age 20.87), VR and mixed-reality breathing improved immediate affect and self-reported self-control against a mind-wandering audio comparison. Working memory showed no added benefit.

Recorded limitations

VR and MR guided the breathing exercises. A live physiological input controlling the exercise was unverified.
