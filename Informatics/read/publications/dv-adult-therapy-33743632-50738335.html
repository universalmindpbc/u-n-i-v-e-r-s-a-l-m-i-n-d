The effect of solution-focused counseling on violence rate and quality of life of pregnant women at risk of domestic violence: a randomized controlled trial.

Dinmohammadi S; Dadashi M; Ahmadnia E; Janani L; Kharaghani R · BMC pregnancy and childbirth · 2021 · Randomized controlled trial

Original publication · Original publication · Original publication · Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

Several violence subscales and adjusted overall quality of life favored counselling; severe physical violence and several quality-of-life subscales showed no benefit.

Recorded limitations

Small single-center, complete-case trial with unmasked self-report and short follow-up; baseline quality of life differed numerically.
