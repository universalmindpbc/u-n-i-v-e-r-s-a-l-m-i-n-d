Peripheral blood inflammatory markers in patients with attention deficit/hyperactivity disorder (ADHD): A systematic review and meta-analysis.

Misiak B, Wójta-Kempa M, Samochowiec J, Schiweck C, Aichholzer M, Reif A, Samochowiec A, Stańczykiewicz B. · Progress in neuro-psychopharmacology & biological psychiatry · 2022 · Systematic review and meta-analysis

Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

ADHD was associated with higher peripheral IL-6 (pooled g about 0.7), but removal of individual studies made the pooled result nonsignificant. Other cytokines had different or absent associations.

Recorded limitations

Sensitivity to single studies, heterogeneous samples and limited confounder control make the positive pooled result fragile. Access: Indexed abstract reviewed; full-text methods not comprehensively assessed.
