The benefit of minocycline on negative symptoms of schizophrenia in patients with recent-onset psychosis (BeneMin): a randomised, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial.

Deakin B, Suckling J, Barnes TRE, Byrne K, Chaudhry IB, Dazzan P, Drake RJ, Giordano A, Husain N, Jones PB, Joyce E, Knox E, Krynicki C, Lawrie SM, Lewis S, Lisiecka-Ford DM, Nikkheslat N, Pariante CM, Smallman R, Watson A, Williams SCR, Upthegrove R, Dunn G, BeneMin Study team. · The lancet. Psychiatry · 2018 · Randomized placebo-controlled trial

Original publication · Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

The 12-month BeneMin trial found no benefit of adjunctive minocycline on negative symptoms (difference −0.19; 95% CI −1.23 to 0.85), plasma IL-6 or its other primary biomarkers.

Recorded limitations

This larger negative trial constrains smaller positive minocycline studies. It did not select participants for demonstrated inflammation. Linked correction notice identified; its full content remains unresolved in this appraisal. Access: Abstract and selected full-text passages reviewed.
