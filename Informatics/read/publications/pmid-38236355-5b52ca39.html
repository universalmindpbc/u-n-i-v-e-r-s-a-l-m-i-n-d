Virtual Reality Breathing Interventions for Mental Health: A Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis of Randomized Controlled Trials

Cortez-Vázquez Gabriela, Adriaanse Marcel, Burchell George Louis, Ostelo Raymond, Panayiotou Georgia, Vlemincx Elke · Applied Psychophysiology and Biofeedback · 2024 · Review or conceptual paper

Original publication

Recorded summary

Six randomised trials compared VR and non-VR breathing interventions. Pooled findings showed no reliable additional mental-health benefit from immersive delivery.

Recorded limitations

Systematic review of VR versus other methods of delivering breathing exercises.
