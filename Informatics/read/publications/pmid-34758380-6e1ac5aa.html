The immune-kynurenine pathway in social anxiety disorder.

Butler MI, Long-Smith C, Moloney GM, Morkl S, O'Mahony SM, Cryan JF, Clarke G, Dinan TG. · Brain, behavior, and immunity · 2022 · Case-control immune and kynurenine study

Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

Basal and stimulated pro-inflammatory cytokines, including IL-6, did not differ. Lower unstimulated IL-10 and altered kynurenine metabolites were reported.

Recorded limitations

Small cross-sectional study; supports examining regulatory and metabolic pathways without presuming an IL-6 elevation. Access: Indexed abstract reviewed; full-text methods not comprehensively assessed.
