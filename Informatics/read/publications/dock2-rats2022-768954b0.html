Epigenetic Approach to PTSD: In the Aspects of Rat Models

Aykac Asli, Kalkan Rasime · Global Medical Genetics · 2022 · Review of animal models and human epigenetic findings

Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

Discusses DOCK2 among human PTSD methylation candidates while reviewing experimental stress models.

Recorded limitations

Its table labels DOCK2 hypermethylation, whereas the original cg16277944 finding links lower methylation with greater symptom severity. This chapter follows the primary study and retains site-specific directions. Access: Full-text DOCK2 passages and table reviewed.
