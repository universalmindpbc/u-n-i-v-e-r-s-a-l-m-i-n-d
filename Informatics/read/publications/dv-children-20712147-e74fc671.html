The effectiveness of parent-child interaction therapy for victims of interparental violence.

Timmer SG; Ware LM; Urquiza AJ; Zebell NM · Violence and victims · 2010 · Nonrandomized comparative treatment cohort

Original publication · Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

Child behavior problems and caregivers’ psychological distress decreased during PCIT in both exposure groups. The separate parental-distress measure did not significantly decrease. Improvement did not differ significantly by IPV exposure.

Recorded limitations

Both groups received PCIT and there was no randomized treatment or untreated comparison. Changes cannot be attributed causally to PCIT; psychological distress and parental distress are distinct measures.
