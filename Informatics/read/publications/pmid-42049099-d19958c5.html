Oxytocin alleviates the cognitive and memory dysfunction caused by neuroinflammation through blocking the TLR4/NLRP3/NF-κB signaling pathway

Gong H, Wang Y, Ou Y, Zhou M, Wu G, Che M, Li K, Chen R, Xiong Z, Ling X, Wang H, Wang X, Ye X, Feng Z, Qi S. · Brain research bulletin · 2026 · LPS neuroinflammation and memory experiments in adult male mice

Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

Oxytocin improved cognitive and synaptic measures and reduced inflammatory markers including IL-6 in the reported mouse model. Atosiban reversed effects; NLRP3 inhibition mimicked benefits and inflammasome activation counteracted them.

Recorded limitations

Assessment here relies on the indexed abstract. Atosiban has vasopressin-receptor activity as well as OXTR antagonism, so reversal alone does not isolate OXTR with perfect specificity. Acute male-mouse results require independent translation. Access: Indexed abstract and bibliographic record reviewed.
