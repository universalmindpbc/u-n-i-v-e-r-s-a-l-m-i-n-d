Exploring cannabidiol effects on inflammatory markers in individuals with cocaine use disorder: a randomized controlled trial.

Morissette F, Mongeau-Pérusse V, Rizkallah E, Thébault P, Lepage S, Brissette S, Bruneau J, Dubreucq S, Stip E, Cailhier JF, Jutras-Aswad D. · Neuropsychopharmacology : official publication of the American College of Neuropsychopharmacology · 2021 · Secondary biomarker analysis of randomized trial

Original publication · Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

CBD recipients had lower IL-6 and changes in selected immune-cell populations than placebo recipients during a 92-day cocaine-use-disorder trial.

Recorded limitations

Exploratory biomarker analysis with many endpoints. Reduced IL-6 does not establish reduced cocaine use, relapse prevention or IL-6-mediated clinical efficacy. Access: Indexed abstract reviewed; full-text methods not comprehensively assessed.
