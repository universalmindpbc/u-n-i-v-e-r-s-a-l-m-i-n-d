Effect of 528 Hz Music on the Endocrine System and Autonomic Nervous System

Akimoto K, Hu A, Yamaguchi T, Kobayashi H. · Health · 2018 · Small participant-masked crossover study

Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

Nine adults heard the same piano music at standard tuning and A4 = 444 Hz on separate days. The latter places C5 near 528 Hz. Cortisol, oxytocin and some mood measures changed within that condition; chromogranin A did not change significantly.

Recorded limitations

The sample was nine, with eight usable ECG recordings. Randomized condition order was not reported. Significance within one condition and not another does not establish a statistically significant difference between conditions.
