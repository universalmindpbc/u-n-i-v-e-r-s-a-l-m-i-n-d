Vagal Nerve Biofeedback Intervention for Improving Health Outcomes Among Ukrainian Forced Migrants: A Proof-of-Concept Study

Gidron Yori, Levy Einav, Ryder Chen Hanna, Shaul Sharon, Sirota Rita, Atias Drorit · International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health · 2025 · Methods verification incomplete

Original publication

Recorded summary

Twenty-one Ukrainian forced migrants practised paced breathing. Stress and several physiological measures improved in a before–after comparison. The paper leaves live HRV display during practice unclear.

Recorded limitations

The authors describe live HRV feedback as a technique. Their procedure specifies resting HRV measurements before and after practice. They leave the participant’s access to a live display during the exercise unclear.
