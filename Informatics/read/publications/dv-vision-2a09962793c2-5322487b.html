Eye movement desensitization and reprocessing (EMDR) therapy in the treatment of victims of domestic violence: A pilot study

C. Tarquinio; M.-J. Brennstuhl; J.A. Rydberg; A. Schmitt; F. Mouda; M. Lourel; P. Tarquinio · Revue européenne de psychologie appliquée · 2012 · Nonrandomized controlled pilot

Original publication · Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

EMDR showed larger PTSD/anxiety reductions than eclectic therapy; both treated groups improved versus untreated controls, with gains reported at six months.

Recorded limitations

Self-selection, 12-person groups and restrictive trauma-history eligibility limit causal interpretation and generalization.

Evidence and provenance

Chapter Trust Factor uses a minimum of three distinct qualifying studies. Publication records retain their own evidence and assessment.
