HeartBit: mindful control of heart rate using haptic biofeedback

2020 · Participant study

Original publication

Recorded summary

A handheld device reproduced a heartbeat through touch. In the 25-person empathy experiment, feedback narrowed differences between some self-ratings and participants’ estimates of another person’s feelings. This is a small experimental thesis with an indirect empathy measure.

Recorded limitations

The handheld device reproduced a measured heartbeat through touch.
