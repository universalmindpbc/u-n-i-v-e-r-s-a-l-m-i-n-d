Skills training followed by either EMDR or narrative therapy for posttraumatic stress disorder in adult survivors of childhood abuse: a randomized controlled trial.

I. Wigard; K. Meyerbröker; T. Ehring; M. Topper; A. Arntz; P. Emmelkamp · European Journal of Psychotraumatology · 2024 · Assessor-blinded randomized controlled trial

Original publication · Original publication · Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

Both phase-based treatments improved symptoms; no significant between-treatment differences were found.

Recorded limitations

Substantial late missingness and limited power mean no detected difference does not establish equivalence.
