Mindful Garden: Supporting Reflection on Biosignals in a Co-Located Augmented Reality Mindfulness Experience

Liu Lika Haizhou, Lu Xi, Martinez Richard, Wang Dennis, Liu Fannie, Monroy-Hernández Andrés, Epstein Daniel A. · Companion Publication of the 2022 Conference on Computer Supported Cooperative Work and Social Computing · 2022 · Design or demonstration

Original publication

Recorded summary

Two people share an AR garden. One guides meditation and the other person’s biosignals shape the flowers. This is a demonstration using smart glasses and a headband.

Recorded limitations

The headband-based demonstration converted EEG into a flower display for a pair. This entry refers to the smart-glasses version.
