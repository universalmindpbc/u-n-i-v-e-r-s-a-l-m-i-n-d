Using Monozygotic Twins to Dissect Common Genes in Posttraumatic Stress Disorder and Migraine

Bainomugisa C et al. · Frontiers in Neuroscience · 2021 · Blood methylation; six identical-twin pairs discordant for PTSD

Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

DOCK2 was among candidate genes with methylation associations surviving gene-wise Bonferroni correction. The design matches inherited sequence and many demographic factors within twin pairs. Separate migraine analyses also implicated DOCK2.

Recorded limitations

Only 12 PTSD-study participants. This is support at the gene level, without confirmed replication here of cg16277944. Different CpG sites changed in different directions. Gene-wise correction is distinct from genome-wide discovery significance, and migraine overlap limits specificity. Access: Relevant full-text methods and results reviewed.
