Lack of relationships between ketamine treatment and peripheral neurotrophic and inflammatory factors in a randomized controlled ketamine trial of major depressive disorder.

Rengasamy M, Panny B, Hutchinson Z, Marsland A, Kovats T, Griffo A, Spotts C, Howland RH, Wallace ML, Mathew SJ, Hossein S, Price RB. · Brain, behavior, and immunity · 2025 · Secondary biomarker analysis of randomized trial

Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

Ketamine and saline groups did not differ in inflammatory-marker trajectories or their relationship to depression change. Extensive subgroup analyses yielded only one IL-1RA exception.

Recorded limitations

Peripheral null findings do not exclude central mechanisms, but they constrain claims that ketamine routinely works by lowering blood IL-6. Access: Indexed abstract reviewed; full-text methods not comprehensively assessed.
