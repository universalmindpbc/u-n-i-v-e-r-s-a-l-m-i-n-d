Findings from the Tushirikiane-4-MH (supporting each other for mental health) mobile health–supported virtual reality randomized controlled trial among urban refugee youth in Kampala, Uganda

Logie Carmen H., Okumu Moses, Admassu Zerihun, MacKenzie Frannie, Gittings Lesley, Kortenaar Jean-Luc, Khan Naimul, Hakiza Robert, Musoke Daniel Kibuuka, Nakitende Aidah, Katisi Brenda, Kyambadde Peter, Lester Richard, Mbuagbaw Lawrence · Cambridge Prisms: Global Mental Health · 2025 · Randomised controlled study

Original publication

Recorded summary

In a trial with 335 urban refugee youth aged 16–25 in Kampala, the two VR-containing programmes improved self-compassion at 16 weeks. Adding a group intervention also improved mental-health literacy. Neither programme showed a depression advantage over the control. The VR programme did not use wearable feedback.

Recorded limitations

The Kampala trial tested VR and group support for refugee youth.
