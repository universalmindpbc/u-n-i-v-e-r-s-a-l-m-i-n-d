Child-parent psychotherapy: 6-month follow-up of a randomized controlled trial.

Lieberman AF; Ghosh Ippen C; VAN Horn P · Journal of the American Academy of Child and Adolescent Psychiatry · 2006 · Follow-up report of a randomized controlled trial

Original publication · Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

Child behavior benefits persisted six months after treatment. Maternal distress benefits were present for completers but not significant in intention-to-treat analyses.

Recorded limitations

Follow-up of the original CPP trial, not an independent replication; differential completer and ITT findings temper maternal-effect claims.
