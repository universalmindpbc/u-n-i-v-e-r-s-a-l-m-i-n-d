Associations of three major physiological stress systems with suicidal ideation and suicide attempts in patients with a depressive and/or anxiety disorder.

Wiebenga JXM, Heering HD, Eikelenboom M, van Hemert AM, van Oppen P, Penninx BWJH. · Brain, behavior, and immunity · 2022 · Cross-sectional cohort biomarker analysis

Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

Higher IL-6 was associated with combined ideation and attempt history, but inflammatory markers did not distinguish ideation without attempts from nonsuicidal patients.

Recorded limitations

Attempt history and current ideation are different outcomes. Adjusted association does not establish prospective prediction or a treatment effect. Access: Indexed abstract reviewed; full-text methods not comprehensively assessed.
