Oxytocin system alleviates intestinal inflammation by regulating macrophages polarization in experimental colitis

Tang Y, Shi Y, Gao Y, Xu X, Han T, Li J, Liu C. · Clinical science (London, England : 1979) · 2019 · Experimental mouse colitis and macrophage OXTR perturbation

Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

Oxytocin reduced inflammatory mediators including IL-6 and favoured IL-4-responsive macrophage features. Myeloid OXTR deficiency worsened colitis; β-arrestin-2, NF-κB and STAT6 experiments provided mechanistic support.

Recorded limitations

M1/M2 labels simplify a continuum of macrophage states. Mouse Arg1, CD206 and Chil3 responses are not validated human recovery endpoints; barrier injury and cytokines remain model-specific. Access: Indexed abstract and bibliographic record reviewed.
