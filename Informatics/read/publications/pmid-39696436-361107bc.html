Recorded summary

The published meta-analysis identified 11 principal significant CpG associations. DOCK2 was not among the reported principal significant loci.

Recorded limitations

This supplies large-cohort context, not a DOCK2 treatment-response test. Absence from the principal findings alone is insufficient to label this an exact-site DOCK2 replication failure. Access: Relevant full-text methods and results reviewed.
