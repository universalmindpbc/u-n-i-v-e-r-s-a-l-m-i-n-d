Biofeedback-Enhanced Virtual Reality Training for Stress Regulation: Concept and Pilot Study Results

Schwarz Jessica, Kelter Markus, Baljan Mara · Lecture Notes in Computer Science · 2026 · Participant study

Original publication

Recorded summary

A seven-person pilot used heart signals to vary the field of view during a demanding game. The result establishes a working prototype. Occupational relevance does not establish a military sample.

Recorded limitations

The field of view changed with a stress level derived from HRV in a seven-person pilot.
