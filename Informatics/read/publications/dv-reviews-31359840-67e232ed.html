Compared to What? A Meta-Analysis of Batterer Intervention Studies Using Nontreated Controls or Comparisons.

Cheng SY; Davis M; Jonson-Reid M; Yaeger L · Trauma, violence & abuse · 2021 · Systematic review and meta-analysis

Original publication · Original publication · Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

Official-record outcomes favored programmes overall, but survivor-reported IPV did not; randomized-trial effects were nonsignificant while quasi-experimental effects were significant.

Recorded limitations

Outcome source and study design change the conclusion. Pooling nonrandomized designs can overstate causal benefit.
