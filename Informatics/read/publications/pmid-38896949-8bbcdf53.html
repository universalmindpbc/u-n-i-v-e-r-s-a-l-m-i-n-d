Receiving self-compassion in the virtual world: Testing the potential value of biofeedback

2024 · Randomised controlled study

Original publication

Recorded summary

Sixty people received the same VR self-compassion practice with or without a pulsing light showing their heart rate. Outcomes improved from baseline across both conditions. Adding the light produced no additional detectable benefit.

Recorded limitations

A visual pulse showed the participant’s own heart rate during self-compassion practice.
