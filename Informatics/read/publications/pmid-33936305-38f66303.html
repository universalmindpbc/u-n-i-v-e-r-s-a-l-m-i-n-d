Serum Levels of Interleukin-6 and Tumor Necrosis Factor Alpha in Children With Attention-Deficit Hyperactivity Disorder.

Elsadek AE, Al-Shokary AH, Abdelghani WE, Kamal NM, Ibrahim AO, El-Shorbagy HH, Suliman HA, Barseem NF, Abdel Maksoud YH, Azab SM, Nour El Din DM. · Journal of pediatric neurosciences · 2020 · Case-control study

Original publication · Original publication · Original publication

Recorded summary

Serum IL-6 was higher in ADHD, but did not correlate with parent-rated symptom severity or IQ. TNF did not differ significantly.

Recorded limitations

Cross-sectional single-cohort finding; a large group difference without symptom association does not establish causation or individual prediction. Access: Indexed abstract reviewed; full-text methods not comprehensively assessed.
