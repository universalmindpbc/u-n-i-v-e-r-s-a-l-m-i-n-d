Eye Movement Desensitization and Reprocessing for PTSD

VA National Center for PTSD · VA National Center for PTSD · 2023 · Official clinical guidance; evidence summary

Original publication

Recorded summary

VA/DoD guidance identifies EMDR as a recommended trauma-focused psychotherapy. The clinical protocol provides a relevant reference intervention for trauma research.

Recorded limitations

Guidance is not a new trial and does not establish a DOCK2 response marker or efficacy of a specific Universal Mind intervention. Access: Official guidance reviewed; accessed 21 November 2026.
