Research hypothesis

We hypothesize that the unicursal hexagram may describe a sequence of valence-electron transitions. We plan to investigate this through Hamiltonian modelling, initially exploring quantum annealing with D-Wave Leap.

Odom’s construction

Odom’s construction uses the Phi Ratio to circumscribe a circle around an equilateral triangle.

Join the midpoints A and B of two sides of an equilateral triangle.

Take AB as one unit.

Extend AB beyond B by 1/φ to reach C on the circle through the triangle’s three vertices.

AB divided by BC equals φ. So does AC divided by AB. The yellow labels use AB as 1, making BC equal to 1/φ.

The Phi Ratio is the limit of the ratio of successive Fibonacci numbers. As the sequence continues, each number divided by the previous number approaches φ ≈ 1.618034. Odom’s construction expresses that same ratio geometrically.

This construction applies to every equilateral triangle. Whether it helps explain patterns across Fibonacci moduli remains a research question.

Reduction to M9

Original phase is preserved. Beta begins at 4; its recognised cycle is 1 → 2 → 4 → 8 → 7 → 5. Zero residues are displayed as 9 after reduction.

Why the sequences match

For multiples of nine, (Fₙ mod 9k) mod 9 = Fₙ mod 9. Gamma selection and interior sums preserve this reduction. This is an exact arithmetic relationship.

All 111 multiples of nine through M999 passed the Python sequence comparison. Longer periods contain repeated M9 cycles; their full-circle geometry is not necessarily identical.
