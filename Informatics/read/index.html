Atlas topics and research directions

The Vision Atlas studies human visual stimulation and the EMDR hexagon hypothesis. Computer vision for genomics studies algorithms for hyperspectral image analysis. These are separate research directions. The word “vision” does not make them the same project, method or evidence base.

Quantum Atlas uses a coordinated AI research workflow to investigate biological questions through classical calculations, simulation and quantum measurements. Its Wiki explains the agent roles and public MCP, API and A2A participation. OXTR supplies its first worked investigation. Its hexagonal atom arrangement and quantum-stability hypothesis are separate from the Vision Atlas EMDR tracking hypothesis and from the proposed computer vision algorithm.
