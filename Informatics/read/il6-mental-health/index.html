IL-6 and mental health

Interleukin-6 (IL-6) is an immune signalling protein. This chapter examines its relationship with mental illness, with a separate section for each condition and related clinical outcome.

The review contains 120 peer-reviewed publications: 117 directly relevant mental-health papers and three contextual substance-exposure papers. Randomized trials lead the appraisal. Positive, null and opposite-direction findings remain visible; publication counts are not counts of independent studies.

An association with IL-6 does not establish that IL-6 causes a condition or that lowering it improves symptoms. The evidence varies by diagnosis, mood state, specimen and study design. ADHD is a neurodevelopmental condition; suicidal ideation and behaviour are outcomes considered separately from a diagnosis.

The relationship between this literature and oxytocin/OXTR interventions is considered as a research hypothesis. The separate oxytocin, OXTR and IL-6 chapter contains the original cell, animal and human pathway review.

IL-6 and mental health: overview

A shared research pathway with diagnosis-specific limits.

IL-6 is a credible transdiagnostic research pathway, with repeated group-level associations across depression, psychosis, bipolar disorder, PTSD and substance use disorders. That does not establish IL-6 as the core cause of every mental illness, or as abnormal in every affected person. Anxiety and ADHD findings are less consistent. The most useful question is which phenotype, tissue, time course and signalling process is involved, and whether changing that process improves an outcome that matters to people.

Zhang Y 2023 · Goldsmith DR 2016 · Wei ZX 2020

This chapter contains 120 peer-reviewed publications: 117 directly relevant mental-health papers and three contextual substance-exposure papers. These are publications, not 120 independent experiments or 120 randomized trials. Reviews reuse primary studies, some reports share cohorts, and several analyses are secondary outcomes from trials. The original 42-source OXTR register remains alongside this expansion; its source types include material beyond peer-reviewed experiments. The combined chapter has 162 distinct source records.

The hierarchy begins with direct pathway intervention, followed by randomized interventions that measure IL-6, genetic and longitudinal studies, case-control comparisons, and mechanistic work. A treatment RCT can establish an intervention effect without proving that IL-6 mediated it. A meta-analysis can strengthen evidence for an association while inheriting confounding from the studies it pools. A genetic instrument can help test causality, but is not equivalent to a prescription or a short-term change in blood IL-6.

Foley ÉM 2026 · Girgis RR 2018 · Bekkevold OJ 2023

Childhood IL-6 preceded later depression and psychotic experiences in ALSPAC, but the estimate for diagnosed psychotic disorder was imprecise. Temporal ordering is useful evidence, not a complete causal demonstration. Similar cohort names across papers signal related evidence rather than new independent populations. No individual diagnostic threshold or reliable suicide-risk classifier is established by this evidence map.

Depression

Direct IL-6 receptor trials, adjunctive RCTs, symptoms and genetic evidence.

The most direct test in this set is the 2026 double-blind tocilizumab trial in difficult-to-treat depression with repeatedly elevated CRP. Thirty people were randomized and 29 received an infusion. The primary day-14 somatic outcome was null: adjusted difference −0.12, 95% CI −2.51 to 2.28. Later patterns favouring treatment justify further investigation, but do not establish efficacy. Small descriptive response and remission differences are especially unstable. Baseline CRP, not IL-6, tracked improvement.

Foley ÉM 2026

Indirect intervention trials are mixed. A small celecoxib trial improved symptoms and lowered IL-6, while a pentoxifylline study reported changes in several cytokines, including lower IL-10. Conversely, zinc and probiotic studies reported selected clinical or cognitive benefits without IL-6 reductions. A larger ketamine biomarker analysis found no consistent relationship between inflammatory trajectories and improvement. These results separate treatment benefit from a claim that IL-6 suppression is its necessary mechanism.

Abbasi SH 2012 · El-Haggar SM 2018 · Ranjbar E 2014 · Rudzki L 2019 · Rengasamy M 2025

Minocycline illustrates the uncertainty around inflammatory selection. The small CRP-enriched trial was negative overall, with exploratory signals in a higher-CRP subgroup; a separate 71-person trial analysis found no overall IL-6 change. The EPA dose-finding study also missed its prespecified IL-6 target and its clinical response comparisons were nonsignificant. These are research leads, not established biomarker-guided treatment rules.

Nettis MA 2021 · Hasebe K 2022 · Mischoulon D 2022

Observational syntheses repeatedly associate depression with higher IL-6. Longitudinal studies suggest small relationships in both directions: inflammation can precede symptoms, and symptoms can precede inflammation. Fatigue, sleep, appetite and anhedonia do not all share identical associations. Genetic analyses support some IL-6-pathway hypotheses, while HUNT provides imprecise or discordant estimates. Metabolic, behavioural and immune mechanisms may overlap without being interchangeable.

Dowlati Y 2010 · Haapakoski R 2015 · Mac Giollabhui N 2021 · Milaneschi Y 2021 · Ye Z 2021 · Bekkevold OJ 2023

A secondary hyperthermia analysis is a useful biological counterexample: a brief increase in the IL-6-to-soluble-receptor ratio related to later improvement. The authors interpret this as compatible with classical signalling, but the ratio is an indirect measure and the sample is small. This supports testing the timing and type of signalling rather than assuming that every IL-6 increase is harmful. A linked correction notice is retained in the source record.

Schizophrenia and psychosis

Peripheral and central associations; direct blockade remains unproven.

The double-blind schizophrenia tocilizumab trial randomized 36 adults and found no benefit on symptoms or cognition. CRP decreased while measured IL-6 increased. This is direct evidence that peripheral target engagement can occur without clinical improvement, and that receptor blockade is not the same as reducing the measured ligand concentration. The result does not settle every early-illness or inflammation-selected population, but it is central counterevidence.

Girgis RR 2018

The 207-person BeneMin trial also found no benefit of minocycline on negative symptoms or plasma IL-6 over 12 months, constraining smaller positive trials. Berberine produced positive findings in an open-label study with substantial attrition and per-protocol analysis. The small inflammation-enriched canakinumab trial targeted IL-1β, with strong CRP changes but limited clinical evidence. None establishes an OXTR-to-IL-6-to-recovery pathway.

Deakin B 2018 · Zhang L 2018 · Pu Z 2023 · Weickert TW 2024

Associations are more consistent than therapeutic results. Medication-naive first-episode studies show IL-6 elevations, reducing the likelihood that antipsychotics explain the entire association. Larger syntheses report elevations in both acute and chronic illness, while earlier reviews found greater state dependence. A CSF meta-analysis also found elevated IL-6, but blood and CSF evidence remain separate and neither measures all brain-cell signalling.

Upthegrove R 2014 · Miller BJ 2011 · Halstead S 2023 · Warren N 2024

Clinical-risk groups show a small pooled IL-6 elevation, yet IL-6 did not reliably distinguish future converters from non-converters. Nor does distributional evidence consistently identify a discrete inflammatory subgroup. Stratifying a trial by repeated inflammation measurements is a testable research strategy, not an established biological subtype or a diagnostic tool. Genetic receptor associations add a different evidence layer with their own assumptions.

Inflammatory signals are neither uniform nor synonymous with relapse reduction.

An exploratory CBD analysis in cocaine use disorder reported lower IL-6, but a biomarker result alone does not demonstrate abstinence or relapse prevention. OXTR and IL-6 in mental illness: a research hypothesis

Separate each link in the proposed causal chain.

A plausible systems model connects a defined intervention to OXTR-related signalling, immune regulation, neural or behavioural change, and meaningful clinical outcomes. The existing cell and animal OXTR literature supports selected links. The mental-health literature supports IL-6 associations and some pathway hypotheses. Combining those literatures creates a research hypothesis; it does not complete the causal chain in humans.

Szeto A 2008 · Szeto A 2017 · Clodi M 2008 · Foley ÉM 2026 · Girgis RR 2018

IL-6 can signal through membrane-bound IL-6 receptor and gp130, or through soluble-receptor complexes acting on gp130-expressing cells. Soluble gp130 provides important regulation of trans-signalling. Receptor blockade, lower ligand concentration, altered receptor shedding and altered cell responsiveness are different perturbations. Human IL-6 administration can also raise IL-10 and IL-1RA, demonstrating that pro-inflammatory and regulatory responses are linked.

Jostock T 2001 · Steensberg A 2003 · Mac Giollabhui N 2024 · Girgis RR 2018

For Universal Mind, a defensible question is whether a precisely defined intervention changes repeated IL-6-related measures and clinical outcomes, and whether OXTR-related biology contributes. Use a suitable comparator, prespecified outcomes, realistic masking, repeated baseline samples and a prespecified analysis of inflammation-related variation. Measure clinical benefit separately from biomarkers; a mediation analysis needs appropriate timing and assumptions and remains weaker than direct pathway perturbation.

Foley ÉM 2026 · Deakin B 2018 · Ray LA 2025

A practical exploratory panel can include IL-6, hs-CRP, TNF, IL-1β, IL-10 and IL-1RA, with sIL-6R and soluble gp130 when the signalling question justifies them. Record detection limits, specimen type, collection time, recent infection, BMI, smoking, sleep, medication and substance exposure. IL-10 is regulatory, CRP is an acute-phase marker, and neither has a universally desirable direction. Lower IL6 RNA, lower circulating protein and lower pathway activity are not interchangeable endpoints.

Portelli J 2019 · Munkholm K 2015 · Shiels MS 2014 · Gandarela L 2024 · Steensberg A 2003

Measure OXTR-related endpoints with cell-type and assay specificity. Plasma oxytocin, OXTR methylation and receptor expression do not prove receptor activation in the brain. No included paper validates the combined Universal Mind intervention–OXTR–IL-6–mental-health-benefit chain. The opportunity is a focused, transparent human research programme; any claim of global novelty needs a separate, protocol-specific search.

Review method and limitations

Transparent source counts, access depth and limits.

Searches and identifier checks were conducted on 28 November 2026 using public PubMed/Europe PMC records, publisher pages and accessible full text, with trial-first, diagnosis-specific and contradictory-result searches. The register contains 120 unique peer-reviewed publications, including 117 directly relevant mental-health papers and three contextual exposure papers. Protocols, registry entries, preprints and correction notices are not counted as new publications in this expansion.

This is a structured narrative evidence map, not a registered systematic review, an exhaustive database search, an individual-participant meta-analysis or a formal GRADE assessment. Searches were bounded and selection was purposive. All included abstracts were appraised; selected available full-text methods and results were checked for pivotal trials. Each record states access depth. Linked correction notices remain attached, with unresolved correction content explicitly flagged.

Nature-family, Frontiers and other specialist journals are represented, together with veteran and Navy-associated research. PubMed/NIH indexing and a government domain aid traceability; they do not guarantee low bias. Sample size, randomization, masking, outcome prespecification, attrition, comparator, confounding, multiplicity and replication carry more weight than a publisher or domain name. Publication Trust Factor is study-design shorthand, not a clinical-efficacy or certainty grade.

Bremner JD 2020 · Sun S 2023 · Shapira I 2022

The companion register retains original identifiers, individual findings, limitations, trial/cohort-family links and bibliographic provenance. Reviews overlap primary studies. Where cohort independence, masking or a correction cannot be resolved, uncertainty remains visible. The website-wide JSON/CSV snapshots and Universal One use the same appraised records. Updating Universal One means versioned retrieval knowledge; it does not mean that model weights were fine-tuned.
