Study question

Do quantum responses generated from Fibonacci and M9 P24 representations improve our ability to predict transcription factor occupancy around OXTR?

Geometry explanations from the interactive controls
ControlExisting explanation
fibonacciSuccessive Fibonacci ratios approach the Phi Ratio. This is an exact limiting relationship. It does not by itself specify a physical arrangement of atoms.
modularM9 P24 means nine residue classes in a 24-step cycle. The Atlas places period positions on a circle; Gamma and Epsilon visit six equally spaced positions. M1–M1001 lets us compare modular representations. These are mathematical coordinates, not nine physical spatial dimensions.
odomOdom’s construction gives MP/MN = φ exactly: M and N bisect two sides of an equilateral triangle, and the ray MN meets its circumcircle at P. A regular tetrahedron has equilateral faces, so this construction applies within each face.
honeycombHales proved that regular hexagonal tiling minimizes boundary length when the plane is divided into equal-area cells. This is a geometric efficiency theorem; it does not establish quantum stability or minimum energy for every physical system.
quantumWorking hypothesis: does the hexagonal structure associated with these representations contribute to quantum coherence? A direct test varies pulse ordering and atom geometry independently, with matched controls and a coherence measurement. The Nature study supports Fibonacci-driven quantum effects; it does not establish this geometric mechanism.

Completed genomic follow-through

answer
There is no demonstrated benefit yet for predicting binding around OXTR. Adding Fibonacci and Phi Ratio features gave an inconclusive result. The new classical test did not evaluate biological prediction by M9 P24 Alpha, Beta, Gamma or Epsilon. This pilot concerns CTCF, one DNA-binding protein measured in one stem-cell context, not transcription factors generally.
what_we_did
The agent completed a comparison of ordinary DNA binding-pattern and spacing features, with and without Fibonacci/Phi Ratio features. It learned on chromosome 1, chose the method on chromosome 2 and tested OXTR on chromosome 3, without seeing OXTR laboratory answers during learning or selection. The agent also prepared a new matched pair of DNA windows: one overlapping a reported CTCF peak and one comparison window. For each, it compiled the same DNA eight ways—ordinary, Fibonacci, Phi Ratio, Alpha, Beta, Gamma, Epsilon and shuffled controls—and completed 16 local CUDA-Q CPU checks.
meaning
At OXTR, average precision rose from 0.0719 to 0.0739. This is a ranking score, not percent accuracy. The uncertainty range for the change extended from −0.0263 to +0.0082, leaving benefit unresolved; the noisy increase establishes no advantage. The pilot covered only five OXTR peak regions and used laboratory data with quality warnings. Saved CUDA-Q/Aquila readings describe programmed atom excitation, not binding probabilities; the two saved samples overlap none of those peaks, so they do not validate binding prediction. The new matched pair checks programs, not predictive performance.
next_dependency
A larger quantum prediction test still needs more matched quantum measurements linked to laboratory binding results, and a larger independent biological test remains necessary. Today’s instruction prohibits new Aquila submissions; none were made, and no new quantum jobs were submitted. Fresh device validation and explicit allowance are required before Aquila submission; new paid qBraid work also lacks a credit allowance. This is an automated preliminary report, with Gemini oversight deferred. The inconclusive result usefully separates completed program checks from evidence of predictive benefit.

    OXTR · STUDY 01

    Study insights

    Connection verification

    Fibonacci pulses and longer-lived quantum information

    DUMITRESCU ET AL. · NATURE 607, 463–467 · 2022

    Laser pulses arranged in a Fibonacci pattern kept quantum information stable for longer in the atoms of a quantum computer.

    These observations concerned the edge qubits of a ten-ion processor. The Fibonacci-driven information remained coherent for the full 5.5-second observation period. The 1.5-second comparison is specific to that experiment, not a universal qubit lifetime.

    The authors attribute the protection to symmetries emerging from the pulse ordering. Their experiment did not test a hexagonal atom arrangement or M9 P24.

    A stimulus for our investigation

    Quantum Atlas investigates the hexagonal and triangular structures in Fibonacci’s modular representations across M1–M1001. We are exploring whether those relationships have measurable consequences in quantum systems, using Fibonacci, Phi Ratio, Alpha, Beta, Gamma and Epsilon. OXTR DNA supplies the first biological test inputs.

    The geometric mechanism illustrated below is our working hypothesis. Odom’s construction and the honeycomb theorem establish mathematical relationships; a connection to quantum coherence remains to be tested.
