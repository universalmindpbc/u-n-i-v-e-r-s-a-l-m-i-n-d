MATHEMATICAL CONTEXT

Odom’s construction

Odom’s construction uses the phi ratio to circumscribe a circle around an equilateral triangle.

  1. Join the midpoints A and B of two sides of an equilateral triangle.
  2. Take AB as one unit.
  3. Extend AB beyond B by 1/φ to reach C on the circle through the triangle’s three vertices.

The midpoint segment is 1; its extension to the circle is 1/φ ≈ 0.618. Together, AC = φ ≈ 1.618.

PHI AND FIBONACCI

The phi ratio is the limit of the ratio of successive Fibonacci numbers. As the sequence continues, each number divided by the previous number approaches φ ≈ 1.618034. Odom’s construction expresses that same ratio geometrically.

This construction applies to every equilateral triangle. Whether it helps explain patterns across Fibonacci moduli remains a research question.

Assessment framework

We will assess peer-reviewed evidence using three proposed weighted scientific significance (WSS) tiers:

  • Creates cellular life — High, WSS 8–10
  • Supports cellular life — Medium, WSS 5–7
  • Creates elemental life — Low, WSS 1–4

Evidence review will consider study quality, relevance and reproducibility. Where applicable, randomised trials will be assessed using the Jadad scale (0–5), prioritising scores of 4–5.

These tiers describe our research framework. No WSS score or biological significance has yet been established.
