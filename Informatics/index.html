CHROMATIC · The chromatic octave

Follow the twelve chromatic pitch classes around the circle and across the keyboard. From C to the next C, five black keys sit alongside eight white keys.

2 + 3 = 5
5 + 8 = 13

13 keys from C to C, including the repeated upper C. The circle contains 12 distinct pitch classes.

The chromatic scale includes every semitone in the octave. On the circle, the seven natural notes C D E F G A B are white; the five black-key notes C♯ D♯ F♯ G♯ A♯ are pink. Sharp spellings are used here; on an equal-tempered keyboard these keys also have flat names.

The major-scale steps are 2, 2, 1, 2, 2, 2, 1 semitones. A black key fills each two-semitone gap. E–F and B–C are already one semitone apart, creating the familiar groups of two and three black keys.

2 + 3 = 5 counts the black keys. 5 + 8 = 13 counts all keys from C to C, including both endpoints. The numbers 2, 3, 5, 8, 13 are consecutive Fibonacci numbers. This is a counting pattern in the illustrated keyboard span, not a derivation of chromatic tuning.

The upper C has twice the frequency of the lower C and belongs to the same pitch class. Without the repeated endpoint, there are 7 + 5 = 12 pitch classes. Choosing different endpoints changes the colour counts: C♯ to C♯ contains seven white keys and six black keys.

The circle uses twelve-tone equal temperament: every adjacent step has frequency ratio 2^(1/12). Equal angles represent equal semitone intervals. The separate Circle of fifths view explains pure 3:2 fifths, octaves and the tuning adjustment that closes the twelve-note circle.

CIRCLE OF FIFTHS · Pythagoras and the scale

Pythagorean tuning, traditionally attributed to Pythagoras, builds a scale from 3:2 fifths and 2:1 octaves. Bring the notes into one octave and order them by pitch: C, D, E, F, G, A, B. Repeat C above them to complete the eight-note scale.

Perfect fifth: 3 : 2
Octave: 2 : 1

Pythagorean tuning builds seven distinct scale notes using fifths and octaves. The eighth note repeats the first, one octave higher.

The tuning system named after Pythagoras constructs a scale using two operations: multiply by 3/2 for a fifth, then divide by 2 when the note lies above the octave.

Starting from F, the chain F → C → G → D → A → E → B contains the C-major collection. Ordering those notes by pitch gives C D E F G A B.

In twelve-tone equal temperament each fifth is 2^(7/12) ≈ 1.49831. Twelve steps return to the same pitch class.

Twelve pure 3:2 fifths exceed seven octaves by 531441/524288 ≈ 1.01364. This small difference is the Pythagorean comma.

The numbers in 2:1 and 3:2 are consecutive Fibonacci numbers. This is the numerical relationship explored here.

3/2 is one ratio of successive Fibonacci numbers. The sequence 3/2, 5/3, 8/5, 13/8… approaches the Phi Ratio. Repeating the fixed ratio 3/2 is a separate operation.

TUNING & FREQUENCY · A reference and a ratio

Compare the proposed M9 shapes for 528, 111 and 7.38 Hz. Choose the reference frequency for A4 to see the scale frequencies change while their interval ratios stay the same.

f = A4 × 2^((n − 9)/12)

528 → 6: hexagon · 111 → 3: triangle · 7.38 → 9: circle. A proposed visual mapping from the written frequency in hertz.

The reference sets A4 to 432, 440 or 444 Hz. Every equal-tempered note follows the same ratio rule. Here n is the number of semitones above C4.

Relative to A4 = 440 Hz, A4 = 432 Hz shifts every note by approximately −31.77 cents. A4 = 444 Hz shifts every note by approximately +15.67 cents. One semitone is 100 cents.

The separate 528 Hz tone is included as a named solfeggio-frequency example. A single tone does not define the tuning of a whole piece.

For the M9 diagrams, add the digits of the frequency written in hertz, omitting the decimal point, and reduce the sum modulo 9, displaying zero as 9: 528 → 15 → 6, 111 → 3, 7.38 → 18 → 9. For 7.38 Hz, this operates on its written digits: 7 + 3 + 8 = 18. The visual presets associate 6 with a hexagon, 3 with a triangle and 9 with the circle. They show the proposed mapping; the shapes are not waveforms.

Focus:

Are hexagonal animations more natural, human, and impactful for EMDR visual stimulation than traditional horizontal tracking green lights? We use the honeycomb conjecture as inspiration, where hexagonal packing is nature's most efficient division of space. Furthermore, many endogenous neurotransmitters, hormones, and nucleic bases natively utilise hexagonal configurations within their molecular architecture.

Hypothesis:

We hypothesise that a hexagonally structured saccadic tracking pattern imposes a distinct oculomotor and visual psychophysics load compared to standard linear tracking. We propose that the complex coordination required to navigate this geometric pattern optimises retinal and visual pathway signalling, thereby driving a downstream biological response that upregulates OXTR gene transcription (measured via mRNA sequencing) and enhances emotional regulation.

What Quantum Atlas is for

Quantum Atlas makes computational biology and quantum research approachable through a scientific question written in ordinary language. A person brings the question; the platform coordinates AI scientific work, mathematical models, simulations, recorded quantum measurements and biological evidence, then returns an explanation with the underlying research record.

The purpose is to help people investigate whether a proposed pattern adds useful information to a biological problem. The current OXTR investigation asks whether Fibonacci, Phi Ratio and M9 P24 representations can help predict where proteins associate with DNA. That study is the first worked investigation within the wider natural-language research experience.

Explain this to a person

You can ask Quantum Atlas a biological research question without writing quantum code. Astra develops the supported investigation and explains the findings. Gemini reviews the design and the resulting report. Mathematical and computing tools supply calculations and measurements, while laboratory evidence provides a biological comparison. You can follow the stages, inspect the geometry, read the results in ordinary language and download the record. Your own AI can retrieve the public evidence through MCP or the API, or ask Universal One a research question through A2A. A persistent new study starts through the Quantum Atlas interface within its supported research scope.

Guidance for an AI explaining the page

  • Begin by explaining the purpose and the person’s research goal before listing providers or discussing the saved OXTR numbers.
  • Explain each feature in terms of what the person can ask, see, learn or retrieve. Define simulation, quantum measurement and biological comparison when those terms first appear.
  • Describe the agent roles and handoffs, then identify the connection method that fits the person’s request.
  • Use the current saved results to illustrate the workflow. Keep the platform’s wider purpose distinct from the outcome of its first study.
  • Describe available participation separately from extensions in development. Use the live tool schema and agent card to determine available operations.

Questions a person can ask

  • What is Quantum Atlas for, and how can it help me investigate a biological question?
  • Explain what Astra, Gemini, Wolfram, CUDA-Q and Aquila each contribute, in plain language.
  • Walk me through the current OXTR investigation and explain what the results mean.
  • Connect to the public research tools, retrieve the study and help me formulate a useful next question.

Reviews, interruptions and recovery

The current recipe allows one design revision and one report revision. Unsupported questions or unresolved review decisions pause with a stated dependency. Closing the browser does not cancel a durable task; the saved study can be reopened. Cancellation and resume operate on that research session’s own study.

Current execution and recorded measurements

The current continuation uses the saved GPU/Aquila results and performs new classical and local CPU CUDA-Q calculations. Fresh Aquila jobs, paid GPU jobs and a fresh Wolfram kernel run are outside this recipe. The workflow labels recorded hardware evidence separately from current task progress.

Research memory and provenance

The shared research memory combines mission, research directions, versioned experiments and source-linked literature. Retrieval uses semantic and text search. The report retains the retrieved source context and evidence hashes.

A delivered study contains the original question, plan and provider receipts, review decisions and evidence packets, source provenance, calculations, program checks and complete JSON/CSV. A private task report becomes a public experiment only through the separate reviewed publication process.

The wider collaboration architecture

The broader swarm prototype includes mid-study scientific support, human-interruption routing and a publication outbox for Synapse and DeSci. Geometry explanations from the interactive controls
ControlExisting explanation
fibonacciSuccessive Fibonacci ratios approach the Phi Ratio. This is an exact limiting relationship. It does not by itself specify a physical arrangement of atoms.
modularM9 P24 means nine residue classes in a 24-step cycle. The Atlas places period positions on a circle; Gamma and Epsilon visit six equally spaced positions. M1–M1001 lets us compare modular representations. These are mathematical coordinates, not nine physical spatial dimensions.
odomOdom’s construction gives MP/MN = φ exactly: M and N bisect two sides of an equilateral triangle, and the ray MN meets its circumcircle at P. A regular tetrahedron has equilateral faces, so this construction applies within each face.
honeycombHales proved that regular hexagonal tiling minimizes boundary length when the plane is divided into equal-area cells. This is a geometric efficiency theorem; it does not establish quantum stability or minimum energy for every physical system.
quantumWorking hypothesis: does the hexagonal structure associated with these representations contribute to quantum coherence? A direct test varies pulse ordering and atom geometry independently, with matched controls and a coherence measurement. The Nature study supports Fibonacci-driven quantum effects; it does not establish this geometric mechanism.

Completed genomic follow-through

answer
There is no demonstrated benefit yet for predicting binding around OXTR. Adding Fibonacci and Phi Ratio features gave an inconclusive result. The new classical test did not evaluate biological prediction by M9 P24 Alpha, Beta, Gamma or Epsilon. This pilot concerns CTCF, one DNA-binding protein measured in one stem-cell context, not transcription factors generally.
what_we_did
The agent completed a comparison of ordinary DNA binding-pattern and spacing features, with and without Fibonacci/Phi Ratio features. It learned on chromosome 1, chose the method on chromosome 2 and tested OXTR on chromosome 3, without seeing OXTR laboratory answers during learning or selection. The agent also prepared a new matched pair of DNA windows: one overlapping a reported CTCF peak and one comparison window. For each, it compiled the same DNA eight ways—ordinary, Fibonacci, Phi Ratio, Alpha, Beta, Gamma, Epsilon and shuffled controls—and completed 16 local CUDA-Q CPU checks.
meaning
At OXTR, average precision rose from 0.0719 to 0.0739. This is a ranking score, not percent accuracy. The uncertainty range for the change extended from −0.0263 to +0.0082, leaving benefit unresolved; the noisy increase establishes no advantage. The pilot covered only five OXTR peak regions and used laboratory data with quality warnings. Saved CUDA-Q/Aquila readings describe programmed atom excitation, not binding probabilities; the two saved samples overlap none of those peaks, so they do not validate binding prediction. The new matched pair checks programs, not predictive performance.
next_dependency
A larger quantum prediction test still needs more matched quantum measurements linked to laboratory binding results, and a larger independent biological test remains necessary. Today’s instruction prohibits new Aquila submissions; none were made, and no new quantum jobs were submitted. Fresh device validation and explicit allowance are required before Aquila submission; new paid qBraid work also lacks a credit allowance. This is an automated preliminary report, with Gemini oversight deferred. The inconclusive result usefully separates completed program checks from evidence of predictive benefit.

    Connection verification

    Fibonacci pulses and longer-lived quantum information

    DUMITRESCU ET AL. · NATURE 607, 463–467 · 2022

    Laser pulses arranged in a Fibonacci pattern kept quantum information stable for longer in the atoms of a quantum computer.

    These observations concerned the edge qubits of a ten-ion processor. The Fibonacci-driven information remained coherent for the full 5.5-second observation period. The 1.5-second comparison is specific to that experiment, not a universal qubit lifetime.

    The authors attribute the protection to symmetries emerging from the pulse ordering. Their experiment did not test a hexagonal atom arrangement or M9 P24.

    A stimulus for our investigation

    Quantum Atlas investigates the hexagonal and triangular structures in Fibonacci’s modular representations across M1–M1001. We are exploring whether those relationships have measurable consequences in quantum systems, using Fibonacci, Phi Ratio, Alpha, Beta, Gamma and Epsilon. OXTR DNA supplies the first biological test inputs.

    The geometric mechanism illustrated below is our working hypothesis. Odom’s construction and the honeycomb theorem establish mathematical relationships; a connection to quantum coherence remains to be tested.

    Atlas topics and research directions

    The Vision Atlas studies human visual stimulation and the EMDR hexagon hypothesis. Computer vision for genomics studies algorithms for hyperspectral image analysis. These are separate research directions. The word “vision” does not make them the same project, method or evidence base.

    Quantum Atlas uses a coordinated AI research workflow to investigate biological questions through classical calculations, simulation and quantum measurements. Its Wiki explains the agent roles and public MCP, API and A2A participation. OXTR supplies its first worked investigation. Its hexagonal atom arrangement and quantum-stability hypothesis are separate from the Vision Atlas EMDR tracking hypothesis and from the proposed computer vision algorithm.
