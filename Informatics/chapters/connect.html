MUSIC ATLAS · MEASUREMENT ARCHITECTURE

Non-invasive measurement before mRNA sequencing

Validating the research approach for stakeholders before a substantial investment in mRNA sequencing.

Our hypothesis: does electronic music modulate OXTR via Fibonacci numbers? Our initial non-invasive study, N = 33, brings physiology, audience experience and musical structure together to validate the research approach and establish the case for mRNA sequencing investment.

These graphics are visual mockups of a real Universal Informatics Music Atlas Python script: custom software Ben Korbel is developing with Gemini to measure and unify these metrics together as one. Implementation is in progress.

Brain & autonomic response

EEG from Muse S Athena, galvanic skin response (GSR / EDA), heart-rate variability, HRV power spectral density (PSD) and baroreflex sensitivity. Brain-state analysis brings amplitude and phase–amplitude coupling onto the shared timeline.

Optical signals & inference

A 400-channel laboratory hyperspectral analysis and smartphone-grade RGB analysis reconstructed into 31 spectral bands. Inferred oxytocin is an exploratory model output created by Immersion Neuroscience via research with DARPA. Explore Immersion Neuroscience ↗ and SIX ↗.

Audience & musical structure

Continuous audience feedback and a participant peak-moment trigger, aligned with real-time song analysis: position within a 64-bar sequence, melodic anticipation and resolution, chords, arpeggios, and Fibonacci / Pisano landmarks.

NEXT STAGE · mRNA SEQUENCING

Validating the investment for stakeholders

Following validation of the research approach through the N = 33 non-invasive study, we will invest in mRNA sequencing through the Kinghorn Centre for Clinical Genomics at the Garvan Institute of Medical Research ↗, from whom we have received a commercial quote.

mRNA sequencing represents an investment of A$600–A$1,000 per participant. The initial non-invasive phase establishes the case for that investment for stakeholders. Sequencing will compare OXTR mRNA expression in samples collected before and after the intervention or song.

EMDR | Vision Sciences

Explore visual and ocular biology, light exposure, gene expression and the EMDR literature.

Research comparison · hypothesis under investigation

Focus:

Are hexagonal animations more natural, human, and impactful for EMDR visual stimulation than traditional horizontal tracking green lights? We use the honeycomb conjecture as inspiration, where hexagonal packing is nature's most efficient division of space. Furthermore, many endogenous neurotransmitters, hormones, and nucleic bases natively utilise hexagonal configurations within their molecular architecture.

Hypothesis:

We hypothesise that a hexagonally structured saccadic tracking pattern imposes a distinct oculomotor and visual psychophysics load compared to standard linear tracking. We propose that the complex coordination required to navigate this geometric pattern optimises retinal and visual pathway signalling, thereby driving a downstream biological response that upregulates OXTR gene transcription (measured via mRNA sequencing) and enhances emotional regulation.
