UNIVERSAL MIND LAB · A WORKING CONCEPT

A shared place.
A world of possibility.

Music, fashion and mindfulness, brought into a green space shaped with its community.

The Lab imagines a travelling creative programme for young people, primarily ages 16–24, with room for the wider community. One gathering pavilion anchors an open lawn. Food, play and individual listening sit around it.

HERE, AND ONLINE

Free membership. Meaningful connection.

Proposed entry routes include referrals through participating headspace services and nominations from NSW schools. A supported application process helps match limited places with interests, access needs and available support.

An online experience and live TV are planned for every event, including for people unable to attend in person. Optional professional support is part of the service brief.

THE PROGRAMME WE IMAGINE

Fashion and music-making workshops with a feature artist; collaborative creation on smart devices; beautiful food and cooking; spaces to play, listen and connect.

Guest concepts include Charli XCX, Lady Chu and Justin Hemmes / Merivale. These are proposed collaborations, with participation yet to be agreed.
