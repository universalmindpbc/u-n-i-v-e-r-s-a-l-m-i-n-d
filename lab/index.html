Proposal

Illustrative site layout with one main pavilion, food and play pavilions, a listening area, and support facilities. Site dimensions and architecture remain in development.

Main pavilion
An open dance floor, scattered Tropicália lounges and hanging seats, people, four line arrays and a panoramic screen following the roof profile. The attendance ambition remains approximately 100 people.
Arrival
The opening story follows the arriving road train, ten seconds of construction and progressive fit-out. The site opens automatically, then moves from day to night after about twenty seconds. Replay restarts the story.
Food and play
Cooking, dining, a sheltered activity pavilion and a separate fluid sculptural playground with rainbow metallic surfaces.
Listening area
Tropicália furniture studies, tubular glass listening pylons and AKG K812 headphone representations. The Apple Music interface is a proposal.
Area flags
Triangular flags combine the Universal Mind flower with monochrome headspace, MSF, NSW Education and Apple Music identities. A support vehicle and staff figure illustrate contextual branding.
Music and video
Play Neverender by Justice and Tame Impala. TV overlays the video at an adjustable transparency. Motion controls the animated landscape. Day/Night also allows a manual lighting choice after opening.
Fashion runway
The central approach doubles as a runway, with model walks, six video panels, sculptural pieces and a Vogue flag over the Universal Mind flower. Indigenous fashion collaboration is the programme direction.
Stage visuals
The supplied Hexeosis animation blends across the curved stage screen with the current Justice/Tame Impala video. Live-performance footage is a planned source.
Programme examples
Charli XCX for music and fashion, Lady Chu for food, and a Merivale collaboration are proposed examples. Smart-device workshop tables illustrate the Ableton Link direction.
Light rain
Toggle Rain shelter to review covered connections and side screens. Drainage, structural design and weather limits remain part of site-specific planning.
Night production
A rising geodesic light framework, patterned roof projections, lighting towers, lasers and wristbands form the night study. Proposed d&b sound, TDC screens and teamLab projections inform the direction.
Membership
Free participation, with proposed self-nomination, headspace referrals and school nominations. Online participation and live TV are planned.

Membership arrangements and named partnerships are proposed. Applications are not collected on this page.
