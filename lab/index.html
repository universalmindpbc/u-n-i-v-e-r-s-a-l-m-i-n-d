Proposal

Electronic music and fashion as mindfulness. Universal Mind Lab brings creative education, emotional awareness and access to support into one shared experience.

Main pavilion
An open dance floor, scattered Tropicália lounges and hanging seats, people, four line arrays and a panoramic screen following the roof profile. The attendance ambition remains approximately 100 people.
Arrival
The opening story follows the arriving road train, ten seconds of construction and progressive fit-out. The site opens automatically, then passes through a five-second golden and peach sunset. An automatic tour visits all seven places before returning to the clearing. Replay restarts the story.
Food and play
Cooking, dining, a sheltered activity pavilion and a separate fluid sculptural playground with rainbow metallic surfaces.
Listening area
Moroso Tropicália furniture by Patricia Urquiola, tubular glass listening pylons and AKG K812 headphones create individual listening spaces.
Area flags
Triangular flags combine the Universal Mind flower with monochrome headspace, MSF, NSW Education and Apple Music identities. A support vehicle and staff figure illustrate contextual branding.
Music and video
Play Neverender by Justice and Tame Impala. TV overlays the video at an adjustable transparency. Motion controls the animated landscape. Day/Night also allows a manual lighting choice after opening.
Fashion runway
The central approach doubles as a runway, with model walks, six video panels, sculptural pieces and a Vogue flag over the Universal Mind flower. Indigenous fashion collaboration is the programme direction.
Stage visuals
The supplied Hexeosis animation blends across the curved stage screen with the current Justice/Tame Impala video. Live-performance footage is a planned source.
Programme examples
Charli XCX for music and fashion, Lady Chu for food, and a Merivale collaboration are proposed examples. Smart-device workshop tables illustrate the Ableton Link direction.
Light rain
Toggle Rain shelter to review covered connections and side screens. Drainage, structural design and weather limits remain part of site-specific planning.
Night production
A rising geodesic light framework, patterned roof projections, lighting towers, lasers and wristbands form the night study. Proposed d&b sound, TDC screens and teamLab projections inform the direction.
Mental health and education
Creative workshops invite people to practise emotional awareness, regulation and connection. The programme aims to dissolve stigma and make support and referral pathways part of everyday participation.
Change your emotions, change your world
Apple Watch heart-rhythm data feeds Immersion Neuroscience / SIX. Oxytocin–dopamine and cortisol inferences inform changes in light, colour, motion and sound. The watch measures cardiac signals; the neurochemical interpretation is inferred, not a direct hormone measurement. The cortisol mapping reflects the provider explanation shared with Ben Korbel. The browser demonstration uses an illustrative control and collects no health data.
Responsive environment
Apple Watch, teamLab, TDC and Arup form the partnership direction for the responsive experience. The aim is to support short-term stress reduction and felt connection through mindfulness practice.
Support and referrals
A shared support area brings together headspace, Médecins Sans Frontières (MSF), and the Australian Association of Buddhist Counsellors and Psychotherapists (AABCAP). The pathway connects school nominations and headspace referrals with a welcome conversation, optional on-site support and onward referral.
Tickets
Free participation through headspace referrals, school nominations and expressions of interest. Online participation and live TV extend access beyond the site.

This is a partnership proposal. Named organisations, artists, architectural adaptations and ticket pathways remain subject to agreement. Applications are not collected on this page.
