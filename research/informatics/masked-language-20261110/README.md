# Masked-language research chapter

Scope: Language research chapter 7, five lead mechanistic studies and 33 supporting references. All 11 unique references from the supplied Gemini discussion are retained, including background explainers and the unresolved video. External recorded masked speech and internal silent mental mantra have distinct definitions. Anaesthesia remains a separate evidence strand.

Ranking combines relevance to masked-language processing with experimental controls, awareness assessment, direct measurements and converging experiments. It is an editorial narrative map, not a systematic review or numerical certainty score. Library Trust Factor scores retain their separate study-design meaning.

Primary publication and bibliographic records support each entry. Metadata for 26 records was reconciled with Europe PMC; publisher-only and background sources retain explicit identity and scope labels. The 2024 subcortical preprint is linked to its 2025 eNeuro publication. The Black and Lavretsky caregiver reports are identified as linked trial publications.

Existing catalogue inspection found active mantra, meditation, HRV and molecular research, but no dedicated external masked-language section. Existing records are preserved during expansion; new records gain search, detail and export access. Search facets retain source-supported physiology, research focus, conditions, communities, age groups, study design, date, journal, topic, Trust Factor and Jadad status. Molecular targets remain empty unless the paper supports the tag. Egermann reports participant blinding without verified random allocation and receives two crosses under the requested single-blind rule; the trial-only filter excludes it. Reviews and non-randomized studies have Jadad marked not applicable.

Files: build-section.py contains the curated source inputs; metadata.json retains bibliographic reconciliation; the public JSON and CSV are generated by that builder. verify.mjs exercises the permanent staging route in a separate headless Chromium process with --mute-audio and media playback disabled. No native fullscreen is invoked.

Production is outside the scope of this release.
