# Domestic & Family Violence staging integration

This release integrates the 117 publication summaries approved by Ben: 115 new records and two corrected existing records. The 13 withheld candidates remain outside the published library and Universal One's approved evidence collection.

Every record has one primary collection: Sound 8, Vision 10, Language 99. These are navigation categories; they do not imply that every paper tests a sensory treatment. The collection contains 51 randomised trial reports, 25 reviews or meta-analyses, and 41 other publications. Reports and reviews can overlap in the underlying studies they discuss.

## Reader experience

Each collection has an **AI Guided Recommendations → Domestic & Family Violence** chapter, with an overview, evidence-design counts, source access, limitations and publication approval labels. Classification covers applicable search filters without inventing missing ages, measured genes or molecular targets. Related trauma research is labelled separately from direct domestic-violence evidence.

## Universal One

The staging assistant retrieves the versioned approved records, preserves their findings and limitations, resolves existing aliases and deduplicates database results. This is retrieval-based knowledge integration, not model fine-tuning. There were no shared database mutations, batch embeddings or new uploads of third-party full texts. Live checks exercised the request, server, OpenAI response and rendered interface.

## Evidence and limits

The source audit and all approved findings and limitations are preserved exactly. The source material available for appraisal consists of 74 abstracts, 22 selected-methods records and 21 retained-full-text appraisals. Publication approval does not represent independent human clinical peer review, a completed formal risk-of-bias assessment, or a clinical treatment recommendation.

The attached JSON reports record source fidelity, filter/navigation checks, desktop/mobile browser checks, live assistant responses and complete-download verification. `staging-release.json` identifies the committed deployed source, worker version and final dataset. The final dataset also preserves the separately completed Quantum guide updates.

Production has not been modified by this task.
