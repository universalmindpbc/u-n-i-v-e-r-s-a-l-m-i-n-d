## Coverage and limits

All 1,108 records were screened using indexed titles, available summaries, limitations, curator excerpts and recorded evidence. Europe PMC supplied 725 metadata matches. Crossref supplied another 160, for 885 external metadata matches and 765 abstracts. Five targeted publisher, dissertation or full-text checks resolved selected ambiguous terms and populations. A metadata match alone does not establish source identity when the titles conflict.

The result contains 202 records with a direct match, 13 with contextual relevance, 57 with inferred related relevance, five uncertain cases and 831 with no supported match in the checked text. Categories overlap. The default filters cover 215 distinct records; related research expands this to 272. No-match means no match in the available checked text, not proof of irrelevance in unread full text. This is a repository classification exercise, not a systematic review, full-text appraisal or validated accuracy benchmark.

## Semantic checks

- A hospital's Armed Forces affiliation does not establish a military patient sample.
- Love Army is a charitable initiative, not an armed service.
- Combat stress, used as a verb, does not establish combat exposure.
- Indigenous theory in music-therapy methodology does not identify Indigenous peoples.
- A combined demographic category does not establish a distinct Indigenous cohort.
- Domestic-violence shelters do not automatically establish homelessness.
- PTSD, anxiety, depression and gender-based violence do not by themselves establish intimate-partner or family violence.
- Explicit conflict-survivor methods support war-context tagging without inferring military membership.
- Protocols, reviews, reports, resources, historical accounts and animal studies retain their existing source types and evidence limits.
