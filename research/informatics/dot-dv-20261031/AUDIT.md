# Codex assessment of Dot’s domestic-violence research

Recommendation: publish **117 qualified research summaries**, comprising **115 new library publications and two existing-entry updates**. Withhold **13** records from this first release. This recommendation concerns the accuracy and usefulness of the summaries, not a claim that 117 studies prove a treatment works.

Ben delegated the evidence assessment to AI. No paper-by-paper review is being assigned back to him. A release decision is separate from scientific appraisal, and no “Reviewed by Universal Mind” label or human paper-level approval has been fabricated.

## What was reviewed

All 120 frozen records, ten later candidate records, the 16 existing-record methods appraisals, the later advisory and its follow-up source corrections, the screening/deduplication machinery, trust classification, immutable records, review interface, exports and staging isolation. The original frozen batch and all 120 assessment hashes are preserved. New audit revisions are separate.

Fresh Europe PMC core metadata covered all 115 PMIDs. Crossref supplied 14 of the remaining 15 DOI records. Titles/DOIs were reconciled with those records and primary publisher/institutional evidence. The Greek music-therapy DOI was not found in Crossref; primary search records establish identity but did not expose enough substantive text for this audit. That summary is withheld. An absent Crossref record is not evidence that a paper is invalid.

The audit compared every candidate’s headline finding, design and limitations with indexed abstracts or available primary material, and adjudicated retained methods proposals using their source ledgers and selected source passages. It does **not** claim independent full-text replication of all earlier appraisals, formal RoB 2/GRADE scoring, a clinical peer review, or reconstruction of every reported effect size. Fresh metadata/status checks are not an exhaustive guarantee against every later correction or retraction.

## What Dot got right

- Most summaries preserve negative or inconclusive findings, distinguish improvement within a treated group from benefit against a comparator, and state limitations.
- Population relevance is marked direct/context/related. Historical childhood-abuse research is not automatically treated as evidence for ongoing partner violence.
- Reviews and meta-analyses do not inherit trial crosses. No verified double-blind trial has been identified in this release.
- Dot correctly rejected an RCT label in the title of PMID41024298 because the actual study was uncontrolled. Its author-name correction and ethics-code distinction were already handled correctly; these are confirmed findings, not new repairs.
- The original batch and subsequent advisory preserved provenance. Pending evidence was kept out of the approved library, generated exports, retrieval inputs and production.

## Corrections now applied

Five substantive amendments are incorporated: Strength at Home Men’s later violence comparison was inconclusive; Nguvu does not establish a clear adjusted benefit and has an exploratory adverse IPV signal; Ethiopia PST is feasibility evidence with serious allocation errors and a reported assault event of unspecified attribution; WEAVE’s secondary depression result was not durable and participation could be emotionally distressing; the breathing/testimony experiment does not establish durable or breathing-specific benefit. These summaries retain the specific source limits and do not equate a safety signal with demonstrated treatment-caused harm.

Further methods updates improve HOPE, MBSR, yoga, the immigrant qualitative study, CPP follow-up and two later perpetrator-program reports. Unconfirmed registry/protocol links are kept separate from publication results. For PCIT (PMID20712147), the copy now distinguishes caregiver psychological distress, which decreased, from a separate parental-distress measure, which did not. The preschool allocation cross is withheld, and its earlier proposed nonrecipient count is corrected from eight to seven using the later original-paper evidence.

The Frontiers review PMID35185725 had already disclosed conflicting numerical reporting. This audit adopts a stricter first-release decision and withholds it until those pooled estimates are reconciled. Publisher reputation does not override a methods or numerical concern.
