# IL-6 and mental health: evidence expansion

Search and appraisal date: 28 November 2026.

Scope: at least 111 unique peer-reviewed publications concerning IL-6, mental health and related neurodevelopmental or substance-use conditions. Depression, schizophrenia/psychosis, bipolar depression, mania, hypomania, anxiety, PTSD/trauma, suicidal ideation/behaviour, ADHD and substance-use disorders are explicit domains. Preserve the existing OXTR chapter and distinguish mechanistic hypotheses from clinical efficacy.

Search priority: randomized trials, verified masking where reported, then systematic reviews/meta-analyses, prospective cohorts, genetic causal-inference studies and informative cross-sectional or mechanistic work. Journal/domain preference is a discovery aid, not a study-quality grade. Nature and Frontiers journals, PubMed/NIH repositories and relevant VA/DoD research receive attention. Government or military hosting is not peer-review certification.

Exclude protocols, registry-only entries, preprints, conference-only abstracts, retracted reports, corrections counted as new studies, unrelated somatic disease without relevant psychiatric outcomes, and duplicated bibliographic identities from the 111-publication minimum. Retain exclusions with reasons. Mark related trial reports and overlapping meta-analyses; publications are not independent studies.

Assess IL-6 protein, IL6 expression, receptor/signalling and genetic proxies separately; blood and brain measures are not interchangeable. Distinguish diagnosis, symptom scores, trait/state, medication, infection, adiposity, smoking, substance exposure and withdrawal. Positive, null and opposite-direction findings remain visible. Blinding and allocation features require source confirmation rather than journal reputation.

This is a structured narrative evidence map, not a prospectively registered systematic review or a formal GRADE assessment. Record search queries, provenance, source-access depth and uncertainty. Abstract-only records receive concise source-qualified appraisals; anchor trials and disputed findings receive full-text review where accessible.

Publish the expanded chapter, source register, discovery/export data and Universal One retrieval to permanent staging only. Production requires Ben’s later sign-off.
