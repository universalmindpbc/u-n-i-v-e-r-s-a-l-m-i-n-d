## Assessment

The pass combines a full-corpus metadata and existing-classification screen with focused review of intervention, outcome, delivery and practice bridges. It is not a systematic literature review or a new full-text appraisal. Primary publication metadata matched for all 80 candidate and anchor records; abstracts were available for 67. Missing abstracts remain explicit in the source register. One candidate record is a practice-context source supported by bibliographic identity and title, without a treatment-effect claim.

Each new link records a category-specific rationale, one or more existing direct/contextual community records, source identity, a transfer limit and pending Universal Mind review. The original 50 paper approvals are preserved separately and do not approve new relationships automatically.

Defence links concern adult trauma therapies, veteran-relevant mindfulness and mantra questions, music interventions, addiction treatment and remote care. Refugee links additionally cover youth interventions and cross-cultural practice. Housing links connect documented trauma and psychiatric needs with treatment and access questions; a home-based intervention does not establish practical access for someone without stable housing. Indigenous links focus on pediatric trauma, youth mindfulness, cross-cultural music practice and digital participation. Generic PTSD, disadvantage or marginalisation alone does not establish Indigenous identity or cultural safety.

The MYRIAD youth mindfulness null finding and the neutral primary PTSD outcome in the pediatric medical-trauma EMDR trial remain included. Related status measures relevance, not a favourable result or evidence quality. Existing quality ratings and curated editions are unchanged.

## Review and interface

The public index contains only record identifiers and category/relation pairs. The visitor-facing label remains **Related · inferred**, available through **Include related research**. Internal assessments and calibration workings are not added to the interface. No new links claim community-specific efficacy, cultural validation or cohort membership.

The screening register's “no-new-link-in-this-pass” decision records this pass's scope. It does not certify that no additional relationship exists. The new audit is separate from the immutable baseline audit so later review can accept, revise or remove individual links without rewriting the historical approval record.

Browser verification covers every category with the checkbox off and on, exact result counts, downloaded record identities, related badges, restoration of direct counts, URL state, combined filters and mobile widths. Production requires Ben's separate explicit publication instruction.
