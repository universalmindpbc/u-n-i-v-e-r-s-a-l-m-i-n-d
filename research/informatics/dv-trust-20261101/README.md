# Trust Factor correction · 1 November 2026

The Domestic & Family Violence subchapters were missing the Trust Factor because their topic scores were left unassigned. This correction applies the library’s existing study-design thresholds to the approved records and displays the standard white crosses beside the paper counts in the contents and in the chapter heading.

- Sound: 8 papers, one cross (at least three peer-reviewed studies).
- Vision: 10 papers, two crosses (at least three distinct randomised controlled trials).
- Language: 99 papers, two crosses (at least three distinct randomised controlled trials).

Known reports of the same trial are grouped by the audited study-family identifier. The introduction explains that these are study-design thresholds, not an effectiveness rating for every approach. No three-cross rating is assigned: double blinding is not verified in this collection.

All 117 original paper records, findings, limitations and individual paper ratings are unchanged. Universal One’s exposed knowledge fields are unchanged; this is a library presentation and topic-metadata correction. Complete exports and the static reading pages are rebuilt from the same corrected data.

Desktop and mobile checks exercise the contents link, paper count, accessible Trust Factor label, white cross icons, chapter heading and introduction in all three collections. `staging-release.json` records the committed release and its verification.

Production has not been modified by this task.
