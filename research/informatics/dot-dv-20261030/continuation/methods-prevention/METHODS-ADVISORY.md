- **PMID26613288, Strength at Home Men:** replace the broad claim of comparative benefit through six months. Modeled physical/psychological IPV trajectories favored treatment, and immediate post-treatment physical recidivism was 23.3% versus 43.3% (p=.02). At three months after treatment, approximately six months after baseline, it was 18.5% versus 26.7% (p=.30). Final-follow-up physical IPV Hedges g=0.002 (95%CI−0.36 to0.37). Assessment retention was 45/67 versus 58/68 at final follow-up; ITT inclusion of 135 is not complete follow-up. Registry assessor masking does not establish double blinding. [Published paper, Figure1, Tables2–4 and Results](https://strengthathome.org/wp-content/uploads/2020/05/RCT-for-SAH-Mens-Program-2016.pdf), [linked registry](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT01435512).

The proposed revised record is held for source checking. [Registry](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT00827879), [institutional repository identifying the paper](https://mdsoar.org/items/678bffc8-ab30-4cff-ab3e-6728defb3cd0).
