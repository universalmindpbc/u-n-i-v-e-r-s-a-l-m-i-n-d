## What confidence improved

**PMID 35285428 — Galano long-term follow-up.** The publisher's original full text provided methods and results. Allocation was described as alternating six-family blocks; the authors called this block randomization but did not describe a stochastic sequence or starting-order mechanism. The proposed RCT-cross hold remains provisional. Nine control families crossed over and the analysis modeled changed assignment, requiring qualification of the reported ITT label. The main treatment-by-time results remained null. The simpler post-hoc eight-session analysis favored lower internalizing growth, but the dose-by-time term in the fully adjusted model lost significance after multiple-testing correction. The 68-person long-term visit count should not be represented as the whole longitudinal analysis denominator. [Primary source](https://www.cambridge.org/core/services/aop-cambridge-core/content/view/B1A750E6EE2865948819845018AF7C9B/S0954579422000104a.pdf/div-class-title-eight-year-trajectories-of-behavior-problems-and-resilience-in-children-exposed-to-early-life-intimate-partner-violence-the-overlapping-and-distinct-effects-of-individual-factors-maternal-characteristics-and-early-intervention-div.pdf).

**PMID 25596929 — original preschool trial.** Its own full methods remain subscription-only. Its public CONSORT figure shows 58/62 allocated,50/49 analyzed at Time 2 and 36/35 at Time 3. This improves flow extraction but does not resolve the allocation mechanism or ITT analysis denominator. The classification concern is explicitly provisional and arises from related-family evidence; direct access remains abstract plus public figure. [Publisher article](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10578-015-0532-4).

**PMID 16865033 — CPP follow-up.** The original PDF returned 403. The accessible 2011 primary reanalysis explicitly links the 2005/2006 datasets and reports 27 CPP plus 25 comparator follow-ups. The program-author 2018 summary describes 50 children and additional comparator exclusions. Both sources also distinguish dropouts from seven CPP families treated before follow-up was added. No reconciled analyzed denominator is proposed, and the original maternal ITT null finding remains. [Related primary reanalysis](https://childparentpsychotherapy.com/wp-content/uploads/2016/11/Ghosh-Ippen-Harris-Van-Horn-Lieberman-treatment-help-highest-risk.pdf), [program-author summary](https://childparentpsychotherapy.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/01/CPP-research-fact-sheet-Jan-2018.pdf).
