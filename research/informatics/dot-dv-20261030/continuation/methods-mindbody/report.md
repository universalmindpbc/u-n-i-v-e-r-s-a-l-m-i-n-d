## Findings for the next human-review revision

**Gallegos:** Random-number allocation was stratified by PTSD symptom severity. Assessors were explicitly not blinded. Follow-up was 7/19 versus 9/10 at eight weeks and 8/19 versus 9/10 at twelve weeks, with smaller physiological/cognitive subsets. The authors’ modified intention-to-treat analysis used available assessments. MBSR involved two-hour groups, whereas controls received fifteen-minute individual calls. The comparative conclusion remains null; within-arm effect sizes cannot establish superiority. The manuscript incongruously prints `p < .05` alongside “nonsignificant,” so no corrected p-value is invented. The registry reports 35 actual enrollees versus 29 in the paper and was first posted after recruitment began. No systematic adverse-event counts were located; body-scan distress and adaptations are described. [Author manuscript via NCBI BioC](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/research/bionlp/RESTful/pmcoa.cgi/BioC_json/PMC8052636/unicode), [official registry](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT03484390).

**Franzblau:** The author deposit confirms within-race random assignment. Its four-day study has no sustained endpoint. Masking, concealment and adverse-event monitoring remain unreported. Apparent contact-time differences, one missing combined-arm baseline and inconsistent statistical presentation warrant caution. Reported benefits cover selected self-efficacy factors; breathing-specific efficacy and synergy are not established. The referenced Table 6 was not exposed in extracted text, so access is conservatively marked selected-methods. Precise effects require facsimile checking. Publisher-blocked routes were not retried. [Coauthor-deposited article](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/228698848_Take_a_Breath_Break_The_Silence_The_Effects_of_Yogic_Breathing_and_Testimony_About_Battering_on_Feelings_of_Self-Efficacy_in_Battered_Women).

**Clark:** Allocation used availability sampling into sequential groups. Calling exercises “mind-body” was an attempt to mask participants, without verification of its success. Six of eight yoga participants and six of nine controls completed. One participant’s questionnaires were unusable after intake; reported outcomes/reactions involve at most sixteen. The PTSD instrument changed after baseline and efficacy outcomes were descriptive. Session-level harm checks reported no harm, but half found questionnaires emotionally difficult, and incomplete follow-up limits safety reassurance. [Author manuscript via NCBI BioC](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/research/bionlp/RESTful/pmcoa.cgi/BioC_json/PMC4215954/unicode).

**BSHAPE qualitative paper:** Eighteen recent-IPV intervention recipients supplied feedback; forty-two of the 144 parent-study participants had recent IPV. Methods explicitly cite the parent evaluation, PMID34626840 / DOI10.1016/j.cct.2021.106591. Four weekly thirty-minute calls used trained masters-level facilitators, alongside safety planning and other program components. Weekly safety checks and referrals are documented, but adverse-event frequencies are not. Proposed family linkage prevents treating the eighteen women as an additional independent trial sample. [Primary article via NCBI BioC](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/research/bionlp/RESTful/pmcoa.cgi/BioC_json/PMC9564648/unicode).

## Outcome hierarchy correction after validation

Gallegos's PCL-5 is the registry's sole primary outcome at twelve weeks. DERS, HRV, UFOV and FFMQ are registered secondary outcomes at twelve weeks. The article's Method/Design paragraph broadly calls emotion regulation, attention, physiological stress and PTS primary; proposed labels follow the registry while preserving this discrepancy. Registration was first posted on 30 March 2018, after the 15 August 2016 study start, so prospective prespecification is not established. The combined PTS/DERS label has been split, and the mirrored evidence fields and old/new deltas agree. FFMQ has no results located in the accessed manuscript; no result is invented. Null comparative conclusions and crosses remain unchanged.
