## Findings that must remain visible

- The [2025 video-NET feasibility study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12130420/) involved five adolescents. One participant withdrew after an overdose with reported suicidal intent; intervention causation was not established. Three showed reliable PTSD improvement, but only one met clinically significant change criteria. This is a small uncontrolled feasibility report, not established efficacy.
- The [2019 CBT/EMDR trial](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6505052/) allocated139 and analyzed102, with differential dropout. Classroom and academic outcomes did not show clear benefit. Allocation/concealment concerns and ongoing-violence dropout are retained.
- [Galano's eight-year follow-up](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35285428/) had no significant intention-to-treat intervention effect; favorable per-protocol associations must not replace that result.
- [Overbeek's randomized comparison](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23948313/) did not show superiority of IPV-specific elements over the active comparison.
- [Sugira Muryango spillover report PMID40163481](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40163481/) was excluded because PubMed links it to [retraction PMID40638580](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40638580/). Both remain in the screened ledger as status evidence.
