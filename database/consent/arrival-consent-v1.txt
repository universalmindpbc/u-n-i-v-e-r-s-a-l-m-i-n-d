Before we begin

Universal Mind would like your permission to remember your check-ins and Universal One conversations across visits, so we can offer you more helpful and beautiful experiences.

Privacy comes first. Your information is encrypted, you can ask to export or erase it, and you remain the owner of your data.

Our long-term intention is for Universal Citizens to share in the value created by collective learning. HAPPINESS rewards are separate; we will only ask about them if you choose Rewards inside the experience.

We use a pseudonymous Universal Citizen identity. We do not ask for your name or email at arrival.
