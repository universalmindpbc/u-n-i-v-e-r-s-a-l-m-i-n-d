01

THE BRIEF

Universal Mind’s I AM portal primarily serves as a check in function, for how you are feeling, transcending stigma by living within electronic music and fashion culture, together as one.

In addition to this core function, Universal Mind are exploring optional EMDR, sound and sub-audible mantra layers, that are evidence backed, as ways of supporting audience members with anxiety and PTSD.

Our intention is not replacing existing therapies or medications but rather providing a free, online community, designed around evidence based research - for the hours and days between therapeutic support. Further more, Universal Mind acts as a referral pathway to clinician and therapy, together as one.

Here is a summary, as well as more rigorous collection, of our relevant research, supporting Universal Mind’s vision for entertainment platforms supporting mental health.

03

RESEARCH PROTOCOL

  1. 01

    Peer reviewed first. We prioritise clinical guidelines, systematic reviews, meta-analyses and randomised controlled trials.

  2. 02

    Delivery matters. The current entertainment layer and the planned clinician-led pathway are evaluated separately, with protocol fidelity and product-specific outcomes required for clinical equivalence.

  3. 03

    Limits stay visible. Heterogeneity, risk of bias, small samples and indirect evidence appear beside positive findings.

  4. 04

    The record stays alive. Hero research can be refreshed independently of the original PDF dossiers as stronger studies are published.
