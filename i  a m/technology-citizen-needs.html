Sensing horizons
  1. Horizon 1 Self-report Language · Voice · Opt-in Video Selfie

    Current opt-in layer: language, voice and opt-in video interpreted as inference signals, never diagnosis.

  2. Horizon 2 Remote Biometrics rPPG Physiology

    Future physiology layer for remote HR, HRV, BP, SpO2, haemoglobin and more, with explicit consent, no internet connection required, privacy by design.

  3. Horizon 3 Contactless Molecular Inference Autofluorescence

    Research roadmap exploring whether advances in hyperspectral imaging, autofluorescence and AI may enable estimation of molecular and transcriptional states without invasive sampling.

MENTAL HEALTH

GPT interprets voluntary language to estimate which mental-health concepts may be most relevant.

This becomes one input to adaptive content serving and, where appropriate, real time review via human in the loop.

Current Role

  • Provides psychological context
  • Interprets emotional language
  • Supports evidence-informed reasoning

Current MVP

GPT analyses voluntary, opt-in conversations to estimate which mental health concepts may be most relevant.

Research Roadmap

Future versions may incorporate clinician review, validated assessments and privacy-first multimodal physiological signals.

MASLOW’S HIERARCHY

GPT estimates which human needs appear most relevant from your voluntary check-in.

Those inferred needs become one dimension used when selecting music, fashion, film and other adaptive experiences.

Current Role

  • Identifies unmet needs
  • Prioritises potential interventions
  • Provides motivational context

Current MVP

GPT estimates which human needs appear most relevant from voluntary conversations.

Research Roadmap

Future versions may improve estimation using behavioural, physiological and optical signals.

MINDFULNESS × DADIRRI

Current Role

  • Encourages reflection
  • Supports emotional regulation
  • Reduces reactive interpretation

Current MVP

GPT considers reflective language alongside emotional content to improve interpretation.

Research Roadmap

Future versions may investigate how contemplative practices influence long-term mental health outcomes.

MEMORY

Past experiences provide context for understanding the present.

GPT compares the meaning within your voluntary check-in with patterns found across music, film, fashion and other media to estimate which memories may be most safely and meaningfully supported.

Current Role

  • Maintains conversational continuity
  • Identifies recurring patterns
  • Adds personal context

Current MVP

Previous voluntary interactions help improve consistency and personalisation.

Research Roadmap

Future privacy-first memory systems may support richer longitudinal understanding under user control.

MONO MYTH (HERO’S JOURNEY)

A narrative framework describing growth through challenge, adaption and transformation.

GPT estimates where you may be within a broader life journey, then favours content that supports your next step rather than simply matching your current mood.

Current Role

  • Recognises life transitions
  • Provides narrative context
  • Supports meaning-making

Current MVP

GPT identifies themes of challenge, resilience and personal growth within conversation.

Research Roadmap

Future research may explore how narrative progression relates to long-term mental health outcomes.

TENSOR KERNEL (WITH NEURAL NET)

The computational layer where multiple inference domains are integrated into a shared representation.

Hundreds of needs vectors transform into many millions of multi-dimensional data points, uncovering novel supportive relationships between music, fashion, content and citizen needs.

Current Role

  • Combines multiple knowledge domains
  • Generates context-aware inference
  • Produces personalised insights

Current MVP

GPT integrates language, context and multiple reasoning frameworks to estimate which citizen needs may currently be most relevant.

Research Roadmap
