Executive Summary & Clinical Mission

The modul8 infinity trial evaluates a scalable, non-pharmacological digital intervention—combining 10-minute electronic music streams with embedded bilateral stimulation (EMDR), isochronic brain entrainment, and silent mantra—to measure immediate physiological and transcriptomic stress reduction.

Our Mission

To provide free, accessible, and evidence-backed digital support for individuals navigating anxiety and PTSD. Designed specifically to serve as an immediate bridge between scheduled therapy appointments and to complement existing medication regimens, this platform aims to increase equitable access to care and establish direct referral pathways to clinical support—ideally through strategic integration with national youth mental health networks such as Headspace Australia.

1. Trial Architecture & Arm Design

A 4-arm, double-blind, randomized controlled trial delivered seamlessly via the Universal Mind online platform. Automated platform routing ensures participants and researchers remain blinded to arm allocation.

Arm A (Full Active modul8 infinity):

Livestream Electronic Music + Active EMDR + Active Isochronic Entrainment + Silent Mantra

Arm B (Visual Sham):

Livestream Electronic Music + Static Visual (No EMDR) + Active Isochronic Entrainment + Silent Mantra

Arm C (Auditory Sham):

Livestream Electronic Music + Active EMDR + Flat Audio (No Entrainment) + Silent Mantra

Arm D (Full Control Sham):

Standard Livestream Music + Static Visual + Flat Audio (No active features)

1.1 Phased Study Execution & Stage-Gated Rollout

To optimize capital efficiency, ensure participant safety, and de-risk technical pipelines early, the study is structured across three adaptive, stage-gated phases. Advancement to each subsequent phase requires meeting defined operational and statistical milestones.

Phase 1: Proof-of-Concept & Technical Validation (N=10)

Focus: Validate digital platform delivery, rPPG signal integrity, buccal RNA extraction quality (Fisher Biotech), and local Python TELiS bioinformatic pipelines.

Exploratory Sub-Study: Conduct the initial UNSW FabiLab scRNA-seq optical feasibility protocol on a subset (N=5–10).

Gate Criteria: 100% pipeline completion, zero safety/adverse events, and confirmed RNA data quality.

Phase 2: Signal Detection & Parameter Refinement (N=50)

Focus: Evaluate initial statistical trends across all 4 arms for psychometric, autonomic (HRV), and candidate gene transcriptional shifts (OXTR, DRD2, NR3C1, etc.).

Gate Criteria: Demonstrable effect-size or directional significance (p<0.05) in post-session autonomic parameters or target transcript shifts in Active Arm A versus Shams.

Phase 3: Definitive Efficacy & Clinical Translation (N=100)

Focus: Execute the fully powered double-blind RCT to establish definitive clinical efficacy for journal publication (Frontiers in Psychiatry / Nature Digital Medicine) and present data for Headspace AU integration.

3. Biometric Capture Engine (rPPG Integration)

To ensure frictionless remote data collection, biometrics are captured non-invasively via contact-free remote Photoplethysmography (rPPG) integrated into the platform stream (licensed via the SaMD-certified Shen.AI engine).

Capture Windows:

Baseline pre-session (T minus 2 mins to T0), continuous passive stream during the 10-minute session, and immediate post-session (T plus 10 mins).

Validated Biomarker Outputs:

  • Cardiovascular: Heart Rate (HR), Blood Pressure (BP) estimation
  • Autonomic Nervous System: Heart Rate Variability (HRV parameters: RMSSD, SDNN, LF/HF ratio) to track immediate parasympathetic activation (vagal tone)
  • Hemodynamics & Respiratory: Oxygen Saturation (SpO2) and Baroreflex Sensitivity estimates

3.1 Cognitive & Affective Endpoints (CANTAB Integration)

To quantify immediate shifts in executive function and social-emotional decoding, the platform integrates web-administered cognitive micro-tasks licensed via Cambridge Cognition’s CANTAB platform.

Assessment Windows:

Baseline pre-session (T minus 5 mins) and immediate post-session (T plus 15 mins).

Validated CANTAB Modules:

  • Spatial Working Memory (SWM): Measures visual-spatial working memory capacity, executive strategy, and acute cognitive load resolution.
  • Emotion Recognition Task (ERT): Quantifies facial empathy and social-emotional acuity by testing the subject's ability to decode subtle facial expressions across primary affective states.
  • Multi-Layer Triangulation: Maps CANTAB working memory and emotional recognition deltas (Δ) directly against rPPG autonomic recovery (vagal tone) and single-cell OXTR transcriptomic dynamics.

4. Transcriptomic Protocol & mRNA Seq

Primary Non-Invasive Specimen Collection:

Buccal swab sampling using kits sourced from Fisher Biotech for simple, self-administered cellular collection without requiring invasive phlebotomy.

Pipeline Optimization:

Active exploration of lollipop-based microfluidic collection (such as CandyCollect) pending initial assay validation and feasibility prototyping with Garvan’s Kinghorn Centre for Clinical Genomics.

Sampling Timeline & Gene Panel:

Matched baseline (T0) and post-session (T plus 30 mins to T plus 60 mins) buccal swabs to capture immediate-early transcriptional responses and TFBS activity across candidate targets:

  • Neuropeptides & Neurotransmitters: OXTR, DRD2, SLC6A4, NMDAR
  • Immune & Stress Markers: IL6, DOCK2, NR3C1, CD38

4.1 Downstream Bioinformatics: TELiS Promoter Mapping

To determine the upstream cellular signaling pathways driven by the modul8 infinity intervention, raw transcriptomic data will undergo TELiS (Transcription Element Listening System) bioinformatic analysis.

Mechanism:

Rather than assessing gene expression in isolation, the TELiS computational pipeline scans the upstream promoter regions of differentially expressed genes against transcription factor binding motif (TFBM) databases (JASPAR/TRANSFAC).

Time-Domain Profiling:

Evaluates temporal waves of transcription factor activation (e.g., CREB, NF-κB, GR) controlling candidate neurotransmitter, neuropeptide, and immune targets across the T0 to T+30m window.

Pipeline Architecture:

Dry-lab execution via local Python bioinformatic pipelines developed in collaboration with Garvan’s Kinghorn Centre for Clinical Genomics, with potential academic cross-validation alongside UCLA Social Genomics Core research frameworks.

4.2 Exploratory Sub-Study: Optical & Single-Cell Genomics (UNSW Collaboration)

To establish proof-of-concept for non-invasive, computer-vision-based gene expression tracking, a nested exploratory sub-cohort (N=5–10) will evaluate single-cell transcriptomic dynamics alongside facial optical flow.

Single-Cell Profiling (scRNA-seq):

High-resolution single-cell transcriptomic profiling targeting OXTR expression and autofluorescence dynamics, executed via the UNSW FabiLab HyperSeq protocol.

Optical & Hyperspectral Alignment:

Synchronized alignment of facial region-of-interest (ROI) blood flow dynamics—captured via rPPG during the 10-minute intervention—against scRNA-seq ground truth.

R&D Objective:

To train downstream machine learning algorithms capable of detecting acute OXTRtranscriptional shifts through contact-free facial optical analysis.

Hyperspectral CV Extraction Architecture:

Passive facial frame recording during the stream is processed asynchronously via offline spectral reconstruction algorithms (extracting 31 hyperspectral bands from standard RGB optical frames) to train non-invasive ML models against UNSW single-cell transcriptomic ground truth without impacting stream performance.

Geometric ROI Optimization:

Evaluation of 2D equilateral triangular meshes and 3D tetrahedral volumetric spatial modeling within the facial ROI during Phase 1 to reduce spatial-temporal compute complexity, lower inference GPU overhead, and establish a lightweight geometric architecture for real-time spectral tracking.

5. Intervention Technical Specifications

01

Visual Bilateral Stimulation (EMDR):

Grid layout featuring 10 or more animated GIFs combining high-contrast fashion-style animations by Xaviera Lopez with complex hexagonal geometry by Hexeosis, driven by controlled horizontal bilateral oscillation.

02

Isochronic Acoustic Architecture:

7.38088 Hz Isochronic beat frequency paired with a 111.01 Hz carrier tone and a 528.088 Hz constant sine wave overlay.

03

Silent Mantra:

Contemplative silent mantra curated by Saajz Azura (Bless My Life), delivered via integrated on-screen visual/rhythmic cues.

6. Media, Storytelling & Participant Engagement (Vogue Campaign)

To capture the lived experience of trial participants and share the human narrative behind the research (for potential media features such as Vogue), the platform implements an optional, ethically isolated video journal pathway.

Dual-Consent Model:

Participation in the RCT clinical trial and participation in the media/video journal are entirely decoupled. Declining the video journal has zero impact on trial eligibility or clinical care.

Media Production Compensation:

Participants who opt to record short video entries (e.g., vertical short-form/reel updates) are compensated specifically for their time and creative production efforts, managed separately from clinical data collection.

Editorial Authority & Trial Integrity:

Participants retain final approval rights over any video footage prior to public release. Video recording actions occur strictly outside the immediate 10-minute intervention window to preserve double-blind parameters.
