01 / find your world

Four editorial worlds.
One fluid wardrobe.

A piece may live in more than one world. The edit follows feeling, form and context rather than a rigid shop floor.

01

James Bondage

The body as voltage. Lingerie, second skin and after-dark structure.

02

Champagne Bohemia

Salt, silk and a little hedonism. Swim, colour and pieces for long afternoons.

03

inner planes

Movement as restoration. Form, softness and clothes that let the body breathe.

04

modul8 infinity

Future ritual. Performance, transformation and silhouettes with a digital pulse.
