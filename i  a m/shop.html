04 / about

James
Bondage

James Bondage is 24/7 electronic music television broadcasting sex x mindfulness, bringing emotional intelligence to the bedroom, soundtracked to minimal, electro and EBM - Electronic Body Music. Our James Bondage Health series [ sex-ed ] shares female lead conversations about tantra, relationships, intimacy and mental health, from the leading voices in the space. This shop brings together an edit from 24 retailers representing the world of James Bondage Health and its ambassadors. Each piece is presented here before the journey continues to the retailer.
