What is deliberately visible

The model separates costs that are often collapsed or omitted, so stakeholders can replace planning assumptions with executed terms.

Rights and creators

Composition/publishing, master recordings, event catalogues, fashion content and animated-GIF creator royalties are distinct budget lines. The surplus pool does not discharge contractual licence obligations.

People and safeguarding

Paid human-in-loop escalation coverage, a safeguarding lead, clinical governance, EMDR clinicians, moderators, creative mentors and A$400 industry guest rooms are budgeted rather than treated as free labour.

Research integrity

Participant vouchers, blood-pressure equipment, ethics/HREC review, specialist study management, data work, logistics and contingency sit alongside the A$1,000 per-person Garvan/Kinghorn sequencing allowance.

Technology at usage level

Agora participant minutes, GPT-5.6 Terra tokens, GPT-Realtime-2.1 audio tokens, Cloudflare Stream minutes and Panthur hosting are driver-based and source-dated.
