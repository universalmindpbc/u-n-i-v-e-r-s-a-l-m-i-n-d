01 / Abstract Provisional Patent - USA

System and Method for Non-Invasive Mobile Inference of Neurotransmitter-Associated Gene Expression

A system that transfers laboratory measurement of neurotransmitter-related gene expression into a non-invasive smartphone camera inference system, using hyperspectral autofluorescence and valence electron orbital data, trained against wet-lab measurements of gene transcription.

The system infers OXTR (oxytocin), DRD2 (dopamine) & SLC6A4 (serotonin) gene transcription, performed on-device with no internet connection and computed without ever decrypting the data. The system and its standard smartphone camera are trained against multiple ground-truth representations of gene transcription, including wet-lab hyperspectral signatures, and population-scale hyperspectral video analysis.

The system produces a gene expression probability score updating a decentralised, encrypted knowledge graph capable of supporting mental health checkups, referral pathways and content recommendations across entertainment, educational, therapeutic and social media environments. The mental health checkup is located within streaming media and social media platforms, enabling mental health screening and referral pathways to occur within culturally relevant digital environments such as music and fashion apps and websites.
