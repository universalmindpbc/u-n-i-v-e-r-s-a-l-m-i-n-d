This is an allocation lens, not a payment-priority order. Contracted technology and licensing costs are settled under their agreements; territory terms are negotiated with each distribution partner; the remaining fourth lane represents profit for youth mental-health impact. Final partner deals may change the relative territory share.

Advertising: four non-skippable, full-screen native films per viewer-hour at US$0.15 per qualified view of more than one minute with audio on.

Memberships: ARC assumes 10% of daily viewers are supported through government-funded annual memberships. Optional member subscriptions assume 5%.

Affiliate: the supplied base case uses US$3,850 per 10K viewers, equivalent to a rounded 7.7% commission. 7.77% remains the working target.

Working assumptions for understanding scale; not an audited forecast.
