Sources + methodology

Talent audience methodology

Snapshot captured 7 August 2026. Values combine available public Instagram followers, YouTube subscribers and TikTok followers. Facebook is excluded until a consistent verified public count is available across the full lineup.

People follow the same artist on several platforms and follow several artists in the same lineup. The 415.3M total therefore measures gross addressable social connections, not unique people.

Artist-owned analytics, geography, engagement, livestream conversion and historical attendance must be added before it becomes a forecast.

David Guetta appears in the proposed lineup and performance strip, but is not included in this 415.3M chart because no approved like-for-like value is carried into this model.
