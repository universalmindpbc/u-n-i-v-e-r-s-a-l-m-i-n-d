Business Model

Television Advertising

Universal Mind is a free service for everyone, focused on distributing free digital mental health services to at-risk communities globally. We are not a subscription service like Netflix: we believe mental health should be accessible without asking people to pull out a credit card. Like free-to-air television, Universal Mind primarily covers its running costs and generates profit through television advertising. As a public benefit corporation, our net revenue is shared equally between the masthead, content and licensing rights holders, and mental-health organisations.

Advertising assumptions

Working rate: US$150 CPM, equivalent to US$0.15 per full-screen qualified play of more than one minute. One advert per day per viewer covers running costs. The four-advert hourly schedule creates three additional US$0.15 shares for the stakeholder groups.

Brand partnerships begin at US$100,000. Advertising is conceived as full-screen native cultural and mental-health films rather than conventional banners.

Audience sources + methodology

Vogue global audience — official global unique users, May 2025.

Boiler Room — published cross-platform monthly reach.

VICE and Resident Advisor — estimated website visits, June 2026.

Coachella — estimated peak-month website visits, April 2025. Tomorrowland — estimated website visits, January 2025.

These public signals use different definitions and periods. Partner-owned audited data must replace them before a commercial forecast is finalised.

Additional revenue + operating assumptions

Government-sponsored seats are US$1,000 per at-risk citizen per annum. Optional freemium feature unlocks are US$100 per power user per annum. Affiliate revenue assumes 1% of the viewing audience completes one US$500 average retail sale per annum, returning 7.7% to Universal Mind.

Our working delivery vision is that clinicians are covered through Medicare bulk billing in groups of nine people; on-call clinicians are covered through a Headspace partnership; and moderators and peer support are voluntary programs between Universal Mind and Headspace.

Independent research maintains its own philanthropy and government-funded budget outside the Universal Mind operating budget.
