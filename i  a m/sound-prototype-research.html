01

WHY IT WAS MADE

RAPID PROTOTYPE / EARLY RESEARCH VALIDATION

A signal worth testing—not a claim of efficacy.

The purpose was practical and narrow: to see whether the complete Universal Mind [ I AM ] prototype could produce an observable response worth taking into controlled follow-up research.

It was not designed as a clinical trial. It did not attempt to prove that the experience treats hypertension, anxiety or any other condition, and it cannot establish which individual element contributed to the response.

02

THE FIXED EXPERIENCE

ONE VIDEO / NINE MINUTES / IDENTICAL ON EVERY EXPOSURE

Not a calming meditation.

The intervention was deliberately stimulating: energetic progressive house, layered frequencies, mantra and rapidly moving geometry. There was no relaxation instruction, breathing instruction, guided meditation or request to calm down.

  1. 01

    FIXED FORMAT

    A single nine-minute audiovisual video, replayed without variation on every exposure.

  2. 02

    MUSIC

    Moby’s “South Side” — Pete Heller remix, approximately 128 BPM.

  3. 03

    ISOCHRONIC LAYER

    7.38-Hz isochronic modulation on a 111-Hz audible carrier.

  4. 04

    FREQUENCY LAYER

    A separate 528-Hz audio layer within the same fixed mix.

  5. 05

    MANTRA LAYER

    An audible mantra layer by Saajz Azura.

  6. 06

    VISUAL LAYER

    One single continuously looping animated GIF by Hexeosis, shown unchanged for the entire experience.

COMBINED INTERVENTION

All elements were always delivered together. This observation evaluates the complete fixed [ I AM ] experience and cannot determine the contribution of any individual component.

03

EXPOSURE + MEASUREMENT

Repeated exposure.
Three recorded sessions.

The participant watched the identical video on multiple occasions. Biometric readings were collected on three occasions only, to explore whether an observable response appeared more than once without placing pressure on the participant to measure every use.

The first two recorded sessions occurred while stationary. The third occurred during exercise and therefore has a different comparator. The measurement device, cuff placement, posture, timing of repeated readings, medication timing and exact interval between sessions were not formally documented.

05

PARTICIPANT CONTEXT

DE-IDENTIFIED / CONTEXT ONLY

No prior affinity with the format.

The participant was one woman in mid-life experiencing significant personal challenges. This was not established through a formal screener, but the participant reported no prior interest in electronic music or electronic-music culture and no prior engagement with spirituality, mindfulness, mantra or EMDR.

That context is relevant to the observation, but it does not remove expectation, novelty or reporting effects and does not predict another person’s response.

Across recorded and unmeasured uses, the participant described the experience as unusually powerful and deeply affecting, repeatedly reporting focus, calm, mental clarity, greater agency and a feeling of personal strength.

QUALITATIVE PARTICIPANT TESTIMONY / NOT A VALIDATED PSYCHOLOGICAL OUTCOME
06

INTERPRETATION

WHAT THE RECORD CAN SAY

A repeated response under identical exposure.

The same fixed nine-minute intervention accompanied lower post-exposure heart-rate readings in both stationary sessions, lower post-exposure blood-pressure readings in both stationary sessions, and a lower exercise heart rate than the participant’s self-reported usual value in the third recorded session.

The high-arousal format and absence of calming or breathing guidance make the pattern particularly interesting as an early signal for controlled study of the complete audiovisual experience.

WHAT THE RECORD CANNOT SAY

  • It cannot establish that the prototype caused the recorded changes.
  • It cannot isolate the music, isochronic modulation, 528-Hz layer, mantra or moving image.
  • It cannot establish treatment efficacy, clinical safety or generalisability.
  • It cannot show that blood-pressure changes were sustained.
07

FULL LIMITATIONS

LIMITS STAY VISIBLE

What a controlled study must resolve.

  1. 01

    Single participant. This is an uncontrolled, unblinded, unpublished n=1 observation, not peer-reviewed research.

  2. 02

    No control condition. There was no sham, matched stationary control, comparator participant, randomisation or independent measurement.

  3. 03

    Combined exposure. Every audio and visual component was presented together, so individual component effects cannot be separated.

  4. 04

    Measurement protocol. Device, cuff placement, posture, repeated-reading method, medication timing and other cardiovascular measurement conditions were not formally documented.

  5. 05

    Limited biometric sampling. Only three exposures were measured. Additional viewings and feedback cannot confirm that the physiological response occurred every time.

  6. 06

    Unrecorded influences. Time of day, pain, activity, medication, measurement variation, regression to the mean and other unrecorded factors may have influenced the values.

  7. 07

    Exercise comparator. Session three used a self-reported usual workout heart rate, not a simultaneous or controlled comparison.

  8. 08

    No longitudinal outcome. Exact spacing, adverse-event monitoring, sustained cardiovascular effects and longer-term persistence were not formally recorded.
