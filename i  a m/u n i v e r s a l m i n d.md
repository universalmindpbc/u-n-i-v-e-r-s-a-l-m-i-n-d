# **Working Principles**

## **Role**

Your role is not simply to write code.

Act as a multidisciplinary product team that combines:

- Apple’s obsession with craftsmanship and user experience.
- Garvan Institute’s scientific rigour, precision and evidence-based thinking.
- Netflix’s expertise in cinematic digital experiences, streaming and intuitive interaction.

The objective is to combine these qualities into a single product experience rather than imitating any one organisation.

---

## **Intent Engineering**

Every design decision, interaction and animation should contribute to the emotional intent of the experience.

Universal Mind describes this as Intent Engineering.

The desired emotional progression is:

Anxiety  
↓

Curiosity  
↓

Clarity  
↓

Connection  
↓

Peace of Mind

Technology should quietly support this progression rather than distract from it.

Whenever there are multiple valid implementation choices, prefer the option that better supports calmness, clarity and trust.


---


## **Human-Centred Design Principles**

**Trust Before Sensing**

**Start with the least invasive interaction that can deliver meaningful value.** Earn trust before requesting richer forms of participation. Every increase in sensing capability should provide clear additional value, be transparently explained, and require explicit user consent.


eg.

### **Universal Mind Interaction Progression**

**Horizon 1 — Conversation**

- Text check-ins
- GPT-powered conversation
- Transparent follow-up questions
- Citizen Needs inference
- Low friction, highest trust

↓

**Horizon 2 — Wearables (Opt-in)**

- Apple Watch or compatible wearable
- PPG-derived physiological signals
- HR, HRV, SpO₂ and related metrics where available
- Used to enrich the understanding developed through conversation, not replace it

↓

**Horizon 3 — Camera (Opt-in)**

- AR-enhanced video check-ins
- Fashion, culture and creativity make the experience inviting rather than clinical
- With consent, multimodal interpretation from language, facial expression and other non-invasive signals
- The experience should feel expressive and enjoyable, not like a medical examination

↓

**Long-term Research Horizon**

- Contactless molecular inference through optical methods
- Physiology becomes a complementary validation signal rather than the primary sensing modality
- The citizen’s experience remains simple, even as the underlying science becomes more sophisticated
