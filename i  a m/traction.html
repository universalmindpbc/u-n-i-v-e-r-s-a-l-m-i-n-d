08 / 09 — CLINICAL + GENOMICS TRACTION

THE RCT
WAS ALREADY
IN MOTION.

Six months of groundwork in 2019 moved the research from a proposal into institutional planning.

GARVAN INSTITUTE / KINGHORN AGREED
TO PARTICIPATE

Following a stakeholder meeting with the head of the Kinghorn sequencing laboratory, Garvan supplied a technical-services quote for mRNA sequencing and offered to manage the full randomized controlled trial.

GARVAN + FISHER BIOTECH BUCCAL
SWAB PATH

Ben project-managed the sourcing of buccal-swab technology for a more humane sampling pathway, bringing Fisher Biotech from Perth to Sydney for a technical meeting with Garvan that concluded with agreement to proceed.

PROFESSOR PATRICK McGORRY DIRECT
RESPONSE

After receiving the RCT proposal, Orygen and headspace founder Professor Patrick McGorry responded personally that he understood the power of music and would be happy to help.

BINAH.AI / BIOMETRICS EXECUTED
AGREEMENT

A legal stakeholder agreement with early rPPG biometrics platform Binah.ai established the technical pathway that preceded today’s optional video-selfie Heart Rate experience. The live beta uses Shen.AI’s free development plan; Binah.ai remains part of the original partnership groundwork.

The programme paused for health reasons. The groundwork now provides a clear starting point for re-engagement.
