# Universal Citizen ownership boundary

## Product position

Universal Citizen ownership is a future, major Universal Mind product lane. It may allow eligible citizens to hold a legally recognised interest in a future Universal Mind public-benefit corporation and to participate in voting, distributions or other corporate actions defined by the governing documents.

It is deliberately separate from HAPPINESS rewards:

- HAPPINESS `[ I o H ]` is a non-cash, non-transferable rewards programme in the pilot;
- a HAPPINESS balance never represents equity, a dividend, an investment return or a promise of conversion into equity;
- attendance, referrals, wellbeing activity and agent contributions cannot automatically issue shares;
- Apple Wallet displays the rewards relationship only;
- no ownership token is offered, sold, minted or distributed before the legal share class, disclosures, eligibility rules and regulated operating partners are approved.

## Provider-neutral integration seam

The future ownership service will sit behind a provider adapter rather than coupling the product to one token standard. Swarm Capital / Swarm Markets is Universal Mind's selected integration direction because its regulated platform connects tokenization to conventional companies, shares and other real-world assets. SX1411 is the selected future ownership standard, subject to Swarm onboarding, technical diligence and approval of the regulated corporate structure. SRC20 remains an important architectural predecessor but is not the implementation target.

The adapter must support:

1. the corporation's authoritative shareholder register and cap table;
2. jurisdiction, offering exemption and investor-eligibility policies;
3. identity, KYC/AML, sanctions and residency checks where required;
4. allowlisted wallets, transfer restrictions, freezes, recovery and court-order handling;
5. voting, distributions, tax records and other corporate actions;
6. reconciliation between on-chain balances and the legally authoritative register;
7. disclosures, consent receipts, audit logs and human review;
8. migration to a replacement protocol without changing HAPPINESS balances or citizen identity.

## Current implementation rule

Only schemas, interfaces and test fixtures may be prepared before compliance approval. Any test token must be inert, clearly labelled as a sandbox, confer no rights, remain inaccessible to the public and never appear inside the HAPPINESS rewards balance.

The production issuer, legal rights, chain, token standard and regulated providers remain unselected.
